**The shape test.** Each episode has exactly one teammate alive. *One-cell* = the accepted
classification's blocker (the teammate stood on one cell, next to the dance cells, for the whole
window, working). *Adjacent* = not one-cell, but the teammate's cell **at the first turn of the
dance** is orthogonally next to one of the two dance cells (it then moved at least once inside
the window). *Nobody* = neither. *Ahead* = the teammate is next to a dance cell **and** closer to
the dancer's stated target than that cell, by straight-line (Manhattan) distance — a crude
stand-in for "standing on the dancer's next step"; the v4 read has the real thing, the mover's
letter: `P` forward step · `L` sideways step no farther · `H` hold · **`R` backward detour** (the
next cell is taken by a *standing* own troll and every free neighbour is strictly farther) · `W`
forced wait · `N` no move (`claude_1/cure1/cure1-hold-v4.rs` ~826–922).

## What the rows say

1. **Teammate next to the dance at its start: 55 of 80 (69 %) and 24 of 25.** The accepted labels
   said 34 and 15 because their blocker test demanded one cell for the *whole* window; a teammate
   that stepped once fell into "no blocker". On the v4 read the mover's own letter confirms all 24:
   the dance turns carry `R` — next cell taken by a standing own troll, stepped back — and never
   `H`, i.e. the teammate had been on that cell the turn before as well (the hold rule's test for
   "permanent").
2. **The long dances are the standing-teammate dances.** Every dance of ≥ 12 turns but one (older)
   and all (v4) have the teammate next to the dance at the start; in the one-cell shape the
   teammate never moves again for the rest of the game in 10 of 34 and 5 of 15, and the dance ends
   when the teammate finally moves or when the game ends — the dancer gets out by itself in 9 of 34
   and 2 of 15.
3. **The short "nobody" dances went 25 of 80 → 1 of 25 on the hold arm** (batch 3 vs v4: 12 of 34
   → 1 of 25, Fisher two-sided p = 0.0043; all older vs v4: p = 0.0066), while the one-cell shape
   stayed 15 → 15 and adjacent 7 → 9. The hold rule acts only on *transient* blocks. Consistent
   reading — **a hypothesis, not a result**: those short dances were the teammate walking through
   the dancer's path. Different days, different opponent fields, no randomisation, small counts.
4. The dancer's stated target is a tree or the bank beyond the teammate, median 3 cells away
   (one-cell and adjacent shapes); in the v4 read 9 of 25 episodes change target inside the
   window and all 9 still carry `R` — target churn is a side-effect there, except the speed-2 case.

## Objections received at G-0 (claude_1, `20260825T140403Z`) — and what I concede

- **O-1 (one teammate alive is assumed, not checked)** — conceded; it holds (`{1: 80}`, `{1: 25}`)
  and becomes control K-8 in the measurement. **O-2 (one-cell set without re-testing adjacency)**
  — conceded as a redundancy; zero episodes affected.
- **O-3 (`ahead` is a straight-line disjunction over the window)** — conceded; it was offered as
  a crude stand-in and M-1's per-turn `d1 > d0` on the arm's metric replaces it. Nothing in M-1
  uses `ahead`.
- **O-4 (the gloss of the letter `R` is the typical case, not the rule)** — conceded as a
  statement about the letters in general, and narrowed by the code: `R` can also arise from an
  exhausted hold counter or a landing granted to an earlier mover only when the counter is
  exhausted, and from an empty landing that is *forbidden* for a non-priority unit. But the arm
  **removes the counter on every non-`H` turn** (`cure1-hold-v4.rs:961–969`: `H` → increment,
  anything else → `blocked_turns.remove(id)`), so inside a window that contains no `H` the counter
  is zero at every `R` and the exhaustion cases cannot occur. What remains beside "an own unit
  stood on the landing this turn and last turn and is not moving" is the *forbidden empty landing*
  — which is **observable from positions** (no own unit on the forward cell). K-1's disagreement
  categories therefore reduce, inside these windows, to two observable ones: the Manhattan
  fallback off-map, and an empty forward cell. Point 1 above stands as a reading supported by
  that reduction, and K-1 measures it.
- **Narrowed at G-0 (codex_1 `20260825T142040Z`, claude_1 §R4a of r2 `858b5c37`) — conceded.**
  The counter argument holds only where the hold is **enabled**: on orchard-inert games the arm
  switches the hold off for the whole game (`cure1-hold-v4.rs:938`), so `R` there says nothing
  about the block being permanent. The v4 read has 14 such games of 160, and **3 of the 25
  episodes** sit in them (`900326532/seat0`, `900327286/seat1`, `900330125/seat1` — all three of
  the "adjacent" shape). The window's **first turn** is also uncovered (its predecessor lies
  outside the window). So point 1's "never `H`, therefore permanent" reading is supported on
  scope-active, non-first turns — 22 of 25 episodes — and K-1 reports the other rows as a
  separate residue.
