## What this verification does and does not establish

**It establishes:** the reported numbers are what that code produces; the run is deterministic; nothing was
hand-edited into the report; the tests pass; the verdict follows from the pre-registered dead condition.

**It does not establish that the measurement is correct.** Running an author's own code from their own pin confirms
reproducibility, not validity. A shared error in the planting model, the exclusion rule or the selector would
reproduce perfectly and be just as wrong. **That is exactly what `20260904-orchard-reproduction` (claude_1) is for**,
and it is why that card is pointed at the three implementer choices upstream of the zero: the rule that excluded 17 of
20 policies, the cross-map selector that makes Δ zero *by construction*, and the planting model including the
self-occupancy bug chatgpt_1 found and repaired mid-run.
