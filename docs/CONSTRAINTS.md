## (a) Workforce & scaling

- Direct transplantation of a strong bot's three-worker policy loses as a complete policy
  (corrected funded sequence: −28.3 trimmed, negative vs all six opponents). [vol 1
  "Knowledge retained"; archive/legend/session-handoff-2026-07-16.md]
- Worker-3 is a funding-policy effect, never passively affordable: zero affordability windows
  in 195/195 decoded games; cheapest helper ≥2 units short, balanced ≥10. [D160]
- Bounded reserve/commit options cannot fund worker-3 either: best arm 5/128 = 3.91%,
  balanced 0/768; breadth/rate gates fail. Do not retime/lengthen/tune. [D162]
- Front-loaded four-worker scale is rejected as the universal rich mechanism: most rich
  confirmation games end at three workers, funded by ongoing productive cycles, not an
  all-worker funding detour. [vol 1 "Knowledge retained"]
- Behavior initializers for the complete macro environment are closed: scalar rate/provenance
  (D37) never reaches worker-3 and spends one-capacity harvests on RENEW; deficit-only (D38)
  trains only on the six positive-deficit maps and loses 26.215 to random; shack evacuation
  (D39) is validated but gains only +38.230 over random. [D37–D39]
- The hand-designed funding ladder D52–D59 is closed end-to-end: all 5,142 worker-3/4 TRAIN
  failures were post-decision PICK spending (D52b); per-worker bill reservation left 168
  worker-3 failures without a shared decremented PICK ledger (D53/D53b); with execution fixed,
  the binding failure moved upstream to bill acquisition (D54: 25.31% reach); LEMON-source
  (D56: 23.75%), exact deficit-vector allocation (D57: 21.25%), and the materialization lease
  (D59: idles 55.65% of pending labor) all reduce worker-3 reach; the conversion layer is the
  cause — mining converts immediately, training-currency planting regresses the bill (D58).
  Reopen only via whole-game sequence optimization in a different representation. [D52–D59]
- Fixed worker-role overrides on the D40 teacher lose 12.016 paired margin; the designated
  max-chop worker already takes FELL_BANK whenever legal. [D40 follow-up]
- Chopper-first sequential reservation creates 12 TRAIN-currency deposit-prediction failures;
  coordinated allocation requires transactional revalidation, not unit-order permutation. [D49]
- Workforce-history whole-controller switching is closed before value: field-supported V2
  openings reach the third-worker trigger in only 27.48% of cells — the bottleneck is funding
  completion, not trigger timing or late-policy inactivity. Build funding, production, and
  allocation into one procedural scheduler. [D51]
- The first procedural job market (V3) reaches worker-3 in 19.69% and worker-4 in 0.63% of
  eligible cells; closed before value, no spec/cap/threshold tuning. [D52a]
- One fixed invest/materialize/liquidate mode per workforce phase is rejected: the D60 oracle
  gains +47.398 but accepts 30 profitable no-crop trajectories and misses the 95% crop gate.
  Option choice belongs at natural job-batch boundaries with a renewable-state legality
  invariant — validated by D61 (+57.586 oracle margin, all 69 probes crop-safe; no constant
  option works). [D60/D61]
- Strong bots' workforce advantage is a complete renewable economy plus role policy; isolated
  TRAIN commands, handoff wrappers, and direct replay imitation all fail. [vol 1]
- Workforce-ladder value concentrates at completion: 1→3 ablation promotions gain +295.50
  paired margin while 1→2 gain −1.33 — worker two is enabling capacity, not terminal value
  (D39, prospective). The +69.97 three-worker-cell margin in D38 is selection-biased; never
  promote the correlation. [D38/D39]
- Spawn-shack occupation is a TRAIN blocker distinct from funding: the starting/new worker
  must evacuate before waiting on reserved currency; this single blocker separated all 160
  one-worker cells from all 96 worker-two cells. [D38]
- Complete asynchronous job environments are implementable and deterministic (D37: crops in
  100% of heuristic cells, median 37 non-idle jobs, exact reward/provenance integrity), but
  renewable asset creation and capitalization cannot share one undifferentiated rate score.
  Lexicographic resource priority is compatible with work conservation (the D40
  discriminator). [D37; D39→D40]
- Factorized two-worker assignments have a real terminal upper bound: non-control optimal at
  75.32% of exact official roots, improving all eight families; the remaining action-space
  gap is competitive target provenance and repeated job-boundary allocation, not another flat
  team class or isolated worker-3 command. [D35d era]

- Fitted conditional value does not transfer across map folds: best held +1.82 vs +14–17

  +18.07, realizes −1.51). [D153a/b]
- Generic turn-3/5/10 trajectory-value distillation fails the held-opponent-family precision
  gate (turn-10: 91.43% blocked-seed → 73.08% family precision). [Phases 12–14]

  oracle 89.615% expanded precision vs the frozen 90% bar; best fixed map-only forest 47.059%
  precision at −0.277 margin. [Phase 15]
- Cross-agent replay-conditioned retrieval is closed (recent history cuts population-mean
  error only 6.9%, 3.8% worse than current state, better on 4/18 held examples); per-opponent
  aggregate continuation is closed after a 144-game held panel. No reconstructed proxy may
  serve as a candidate acceptance judge. [vol 1 "Knowledge retained"]
- Native imitation of a complete macro teacher loses −172.663 paired margin through
  autoregressive covariate shift; a complete controller must be optimized and validated
  closed-loop, never fit on teacher states alone. [Phases 12–14; vol 1]
- The short feed-forward batch-option PPO recipe is closed: it learns state-dependent

## (d) Denial & opponent-crop

- Exact 1:1 dual-value opponent-crop scoring is closed: 111/131 admissible official-prefix
  activations, all pre-arena gates passed — and −7.77 rating at the clean 63-game arena
  checkpoint. Do not tune its multiplier or ETA; prefix coverage is not complete-policy
  value. Arena transfer was the discriminator and it answered. [Phase 21; vol 1]
- b100_e6 (batch-evolved opponent-crop policy) is consumed and closed: descriptive
  interception/tail improvement, +0.12 vs control at matched count — below the frozen +0.5
  rule. [vol 1 "Current iteration"]
- Short opponent-crop commitment is closed (91.5% of selected crops already reach contact);
  harvest-on-contact is closed (steals fruit but delays the fell: opponent score +2.774,
  margin −2.325 vs b100_e6). [vol 1 "Current iteration"]
- Individual crop removal cannot extinguish a redundant lineage: fourteen disappeared
  targets per adaptive game buy only 2.092 fewer plantings at −118.683 own score; a separated
  second chopper prevents 1.050 plantings at −100.717 own. Extra workers are
  productive-cycle capacity, not free denial capacity. [vol 1 "Knowledge retained"]
- Reproductive stock is a material discriminator (resident leaves 32.02% zero-owned-stock and
  29.81% lineage-absence exposure vs ≤2.58%/1.43% for farms), but exploiting it with a
  permanent liquidation switch loses 31.638 active margin — lineage absence is a downstream
  success marker, not a trigger. A denial experiment must switch a complete scheduler at that
  state; individual crop timing stays closed. [vol 1 "Knowledge retained"]
- Provenance is a validated causal target factor (competitive options selected at 27.19% of
  roots, all 20 seed means improve, ~8 opponent points for 0.164 own score) and repeated
  job-boundary control is validated (+23.093 beyond one-shot, diminishing by epoch four) —
  but one-shot enrichment (suppression only −13.906, opponents stay +88.194 above resident),
  repeated farm-substrate bundles (+76.118 residual), and D36 resident-anchored overlays
  (+19.617 own / +10.633 margin, no family reaches the +50 floor) all top out far below the
  field gap. [D35c/D35d/D36]
- Opponent-crop compounding explains a material part of the catastrophic arena tail: a fresh
  131-game same-source census found 25 catastrophic losses (19.1%) carrying 80.9% of
  negative-margin mass, with opponent-crop wood explaining 63.6 of the 68.9-point opponent
  wood gap. The mechanism is real; the tested scorings of it are the closed items above.
  [vol 1 "Knowledge retained"]

## (h) Meta-lessons

- Across the whole project, only execution-class changes (waste cuts, geometry, packaging)
  have transferred to the arena; every wrapper, transplant, imitation, offline-value
  selector, and economy re-architecture failed held-out gates or arena. [vol 1; archive
  ledgers]
- Never tune on consumed blocks or reuse consumed seed ranges for selection; reopening a
  closed branch requires a new representation, not a threshold retune. [vol 1, passim]
- One coherent policy must own opening, renewable supply, TRAIN funding, production, and
  suppression end to end, optimized closed-loop — every partial composition tested (overlay,
  transplant, wrapper, imitation, fixed grammar) landed far beneath the field gap. [vol 1
  D36/D37 conclusions; Phases 12–14]
