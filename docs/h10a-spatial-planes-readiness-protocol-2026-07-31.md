## Question

Can the register's sanctioned H10a intervention—"swap D172's feature extractor for
the 104-channel board, all gates frozen"—be specified and executed literally on the
existing exact-label, official-map substrate? If not, what is the narrowest valid
spatial observation that preserves D172's decision and gates?

This is a written-evidence and source-interface audit. It does not export states, fit a
model, open a range, or alter a frozen module.

## Required checks

- Inventory every 104-channel group and classify it as generic board state,
  player-relative state, selected-unit state, Level-1 target/episode state, or history.
- Determine whether each group has one outcome-blind definition at a D172 armable state.
- Verify whether the D172 bulk corpus stores a board state or only its 81-field input.
- Verify official-map provenance, exact-label provenance, state-key uniqueness, and
  whether consumed-state replay can recover a deduplicated spatial table without new
  outcome labels.
- Compare the 36-plane D29 and 137-plane D18 alternatives without treating their
  different decision targets as scientific duplicates.
- Calculate compact row/state counts, tensor sizes, and a parameter-count example; do
  not materialize bulk data or train.
- Name every D172 gate and partition that can remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

## Frozen verdicts

- `VERBATIM_104_READY`: all 104 channels have exact D172 meanings, the required states
  already exist or can be joined exactly, and no intervention semantics need invention.
- `NARROWED_TO_GENERIC_SPATIAL_AUGMENTATION`: literal 104-channel reuse is invalid, but
  an outcome-blind player-relative board tensor can be added to the frozen D172 input
  on the same states/labels and under the same selection gates.
- `SUBSTRATE_BLOCKED`: spatial states or exact joins cannot be recovered without opening
  new outcome labels, altering frozen decisions, or using a non-official map substrate.
- `DUPLICATE_CLOSED`: a prior official-substrate experiment already tests the same
  D172 option vocabulary, exact labels, and spatial representation.
- `UNIDENTIFIABLE`: named evidence cannot resolve readiness.
