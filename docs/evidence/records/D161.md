# D161 — D40/q6 is not a valid resident-competition substrate

**Status:** `closed`  
**Review state:** `proposed`  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-23  
**Scope:** Control-substrate choice for resident-competition experiments.

## Question

Can D40/q6 serve as the control substrate for experiments intended to improve the live resident?

## Decision

No. Experiments must anchor on the exact resident, or first prove same-panel dominance over it.

## Decisive evidence

- **terminal_oracle_delta**: +3.42 vs resident; CI [−8.70,+15.54]  
  Population: frozen D161/D158 same-panel tasks  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 194-196) [panel_causal]
- **catastrophe_change**: 22 → 43 catastrophes  
  Population: same frozen D161/D158 panel  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 194-196) [panel_causal]

## Attempts

- D158 substrate comparison
- D161 full terminal-oracle check

## What this does not prove

- q6 is useless as a research representation.
- No future controller can dominate the resident.
- D40 has no mechanism value.

## Limitations and counterevidence

- The decision is scoped to resident-competition substrate validity, not all uses of D40/q6.


## Relations

- `narrows` → `external:D158`
- `constrains` → `external:ALL-RESIDENT-EXPERIMENTS`

## Reopening conditions

- A proposed substrate demonstrates statistically credible same-panel dominance over the exact resident under frozen terminal semantics.

- None.
