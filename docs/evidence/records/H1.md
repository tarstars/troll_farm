# H1 — The joint economy package cannot pay on the current scheduler

**Status:** `closed`  
**Review state:** `proposed`  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-29  
**Scope:** Grounded finite-windfall accounting stress test on 220 resident games; conditional on fixed pricing constants and the current scheduler.

## Question

Can the proposed multi-lever economy package plausibly pay after pricing displacement on real resident states?

## Decision

No. Even the own-side-only sensitivity remains negative; the current scheduler cannot capitalize the finite windfall into a fourth worker.

## Decisive evidence

- **own_side_sensitivity**: −2.49 rating; CI [−2.78,−2.21]; 6/220 positive  
  Population: 220 resident games under own-side-only displacement pricing  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 894-909) [accounting_model]
- **worker3_affordability**: 36.8% of games; median turn 78  
  Population: 220 resident games with real bill and post-move legality  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 894-909) [accounting_model]
- **worker4_affordability**: 0/220 ever  
  Population: 220 resident games under finite-windfall credit  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 894-909) [accounting_model]
- **calibration**: D175a own-side −5.41 actual vs −4.32 predicted  
  Population: D175a intervention used as accounting-model calibration  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 894-909) [accounting_model]

## Attempts

- H1 headline accounting model
- H1 own-side-only sensitivity correction
- D175a calibration check

## What this does not prove

- No renewable economy can exist.
- A new architecture cannot coordinate the levers.
- The headline −21.33 extrapolation is a causal ladder estimate.

## Limitations and counterevidence

- Conditional accounting model, not an intervention.
- Pricing constants and omitted complementarities limit interpretation.
- Ladder-effect language is explicitly a projection, not arena-measured evidence.
