# D30 — Synthetic-map results are not field evidence

**Status:** `invalidated`  
**Review state:** `proposed`  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-01  
**Scope:** Evaluation substrate and map-domain validity for all downstream controller experiments.

## Question

Can results from the legacy constant-six-water synthetic generator be treated as evidence about official maps?

## Decision

No. The legacy generated-map substrate is outside official-map support; official SHA1PRNG map generation is required for field-relevant evidence.

## Decisive evidence

- **official_roots_outside_support**: all 80 official roots outside model support
  Population: 80 official generated roots  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 201-206) [observational_audit]
- **distribution_shift**: −78.05 total shift; −72.12 from scalar features  
  Population: synthetic-vs-official map-domain comparison  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 201-206) [observational_audit]
- **official_generator_confirmation**: 120/120 untouched confirmation  
  Population: 120 official roots under the SHA1PRNG port  
  Evidence: `docs/CONSTRAINTS.md` (lines 201-206) [mechanics_proof]

## Attempts

- D29c synthetic map audit
- D30 domain invalidation
- D31/D33 official generator repair and confirmation

## What this does not prove

- The accepted official generator is a complete referee implementation.
- Every result obtained before D33 is numerically useless; only field-generalization claims are invalidated.

## Limitations and counterevidence

- The decisive domain-shift figures summarize the audit in CONSTRAINTS rather than exposing a pilot-local raw table.
