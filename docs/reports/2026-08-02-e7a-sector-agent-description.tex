\section*{1. Executive assessment}

E7a is an unusually interpretable candidate. It does not add a wrapper, a new state machine
or a learned model. It changes one strategic bit---which natural fruit species receives the
denial bonus---only in a narrow opening geometry sector. This preserves the established
parent on most maps and avoids the large loss of a global PLUM flip.

At the 160-game checkpoint it was the project's highest mature live scorer: 82 wins, three
ties, 75 losses, score 25.34 and rank 11/131. The result is encouraging but not causal: the
rule was selected on consumed development outcomes, its bootstrap interval crosses zero,
and there is no same-window parent control.

The bot is also not liveness-clean. A new exact-replay audit found period-2 MOVE episodes of
at least six turns in 25/160 games. In game \texttt{897832286}, a worker carrying two wood
alternates between cells $(6,3)$ and $(6,4)$ from turns 160 through 286. The high ladder
score therefore coexists with a material inherited execution defect.

\section*{3. Overall controller architecture}

\begin{center}
\fbox{protocol input}\ $\rightarrow$ \fbox{GameState}\ $\rightarrow$
\fbox{opening plan}\ $\rightarrow$ \fbox{per-worker candidates}

$\downarrow$

\fbox{joint assignment}\ $\rightarrow$ \fbox{movement/door resolver}\ $\rightarrow$
\fbox{secure orchard + endgame}\ $\rightarrow$ \fbox{commands}
\end{center}

The parser maintains terrain, units, plants, inventories and the turn counter. The inner
controller produces scored actions for every worker. With two workers it exhaustively
scores compatible candidate pairs. A movement layer reserves landing cells, applies
orchard priorities and keeps shack doors usable. The outer secure-orchard wrapper may
reserve one worker for an APPLE mother. Endgame logic converts fruit into wood when a full
plant--chop--return cycle fits before turn 300.

The controller permanently caps itself at two workers. It is a refined two-worker denial
and conversion policy, not a scalable economy.

\section*{4. The E7a near-tie sector rule}

Let $D_L$ be the sum of shortest-path distances from every walkable orthogonal door beside
our shack to every natural LEMON tree. Let $D_P$ be the corresponding PLUM sum. An
unreachable tree contributes 10,000.

The stable parent chooses PLUM only if $D_P < D_L$; ties go to LEMON. E7a chooses PLUM when

\[
  D_P < D_L \quad\text{or}\quad 0 \le D_P-D_L \le 8.
\]

It retains LEMON only when LEMON's aggregate path advantage exceeds eight. Therefore the
patch changes parent behavior only in a LEMON-default near-tie sector. The result is cached
as \texttt{type\_to\_cut} during opening; it is not recomputed each turn.

For a viable tree, base chop value is approximately

\[
  V_{cycle}=1000\,\frac{\text{expected wood}}{\text{travel + chop + return turns}}.
\]

When the tree type equals \texttt{type\_to\_cut} and the rival has no more than two
workers, the controller adds

\[
  V_{denial}=\frac{900}{1+\operatorname{Manhattan}(tree,enemy\ shack)}.
\]

This is a soft valuation shift. It does not command ``chop PLUM''; a higher-value bank,
mine, harvest or different tree may still win. The bonus is strongest close to the enemy
shack, where denial has the greatest strategic intent.

\section*{5. Opening and workforce}

The opening enumerates a second troll with movement, carry and chop in $1..3$ and harvest
fixed to zero. For each specification it estimates the ETA to collect the necessary PLUM,
LEMON and, where present, IRON bill. It prefers carry at least two and chop at least one,
uses a 15-turn horizon, accepts up to 15 turns of ETA slack for a stronger worker, and has
a hard turn-35 downgrade deadline.

Before training, both trolls collect missing bill resources; carried material is banked
instead of abandoned. At the deadline the controller chooses the strongest affordable
fallback. After the second troll is created, \texttt{can\_train} always returns false for
another worker. Recent top-player evidence makes this cap expensive: several stronger
agents reach worker three in over 80\% of games and worker four in roughly 40--95\%.

\section*{6. Per-worker candidate generation}

\textbf{Banking.} A carrier at a shack door receives a very high DROP candidate. Otherwise
it receives a MOVE-to-bank candidate discounted by travel ETA.

\textbf{Fruit.} The controller predicts travel, tree ripening and required waiting. It
issues HARVEST only on the tree. Persistent regenerative cargo is tracked as a commitment
so a seed is not casually abandoned.

\textbf{Iron.} A worker routes to a walkable cell adjacent to ore and issues MINE when in
position.

\textbf{Wood.} A chop candidate forecasts path length, growth and health, opponent chop
damage, own chop duration, return path and final deposit. Unreachable, nonviable or too-late
cycles are rejected. Throughput value is augmented by the E7a focus bonus.

\textbf{Seeds.} When resources are scarce after turn 100 and a worker is at a door, the
controller may pick banked fruit for regeneration. In ordinary play, however, the policy
remains predominantly chop-oriented.

\section*{7. Joint scheduling, collisions and doors}

Candidates target a shack, bank, cell, tree or no exclusive object. Compatibility prevents
two workers from claiming the same tree or cell and verifies that simultaneous PICKs do not
exceed bank inventory. Because the bot caps itself at two workers, it evaluates the full
Cartesian product and maximizes summed score rather than relying on unit order.

The movement resolver projects each landing cell at the unit's movement speed. Stationary
units and earlier mover destinations are reserved. An orchard starter can receive priority,
while the mother cell is forbidden to other units. A blocked landing triggers an orthogonal
detour minimizing the remaining BFS distance; if no detour is legal, the worker waits.

A specialized door-clear layer handles carriers, blockers and swap patterns so that the
own shack retains a usable door. These are strong local safety rules, but no global progress
measure prevents two consecutive replans from reversing each other's target. The 160-game
liveness result demonstrates the gap.

\section*{8. Secure APPLE orchard}

The outer wrapper moves through \texttt{Dormant}, \texttt{CarryingSeed}, \texttt{Active}
and \texttt{Abandoned}. It may establish one APPLE mother on an empty water-adjacent door
cell near our shack. Activation requires at least two shack doors, distant natural trees,
an enemy-safe mother, a trained second worker, an APPLE in the bank, alternate access for
the other worker, and no sacrificed contested natural tree.

The starter picks the seed, plants the mother, deposits cargo, harvests ripe APPLEs and
otherwise waits. Other workers cannot occupy the mother or steal its seed. If ownership or
safety fails, the wrapper abandons and returns to the inner controller.

This creates a protected renewable asset but can monopolize half the workforce. Across the
new agent's 160 decoded games, only 10 of 1,704 attributed created crops were reaped by the
agent. The live policy is therefore not a broad self-reproducing economy.

\section*{9. Endgame fruit-to-wood conversion}

After turn 250---or when very few plants remain and the bot trails---fruit can be planted,
chopped and returned before turn 300. A worker already on a tree receives very high chop
priority. Ripe-fruit fallback is admitted only when travel, harvest, return and deposit all
fit before the deadline.

This is a central behavior, not a corner case: the replay audit attributed 942 completed
post-turn-250 planted-and-chopped conversions across the 160 games. It embodies a useful
two-phase economic idea: renewable fruit is valuable early, while terminal wood is worth
four points and dominates when no future harvest can amortize planting.

The stable parent's repeated mature median was 24.19 and best recorded mature run 24.77.
E7a is +1.15 over the median and +0.57 over that best, but the comparison crosses eras and
matchmaking populations. It is not an A/B estimate.

The new public-replay census decoded all 160 E7a games with zero fetch or state-decoder
faults. Twenty-five games have a continuous period-2 MOVE run of at least six turns. The
worst exact trace is game \texttt{897832286}: worker 2 carries two wood and alternates
$(6,3)\leftrightarrow(6,4)$ for 127 consecutive turns. Commands execute successfully, so
this is planner liveness, not collision blocking or a runtime error.

\section*{12. Ideas worth retaining}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Tiny conditional patch.} Preserve a strong exact parent and alter one
        strategic choice rather than adding a broad wrapper.
  \item \textbf{Geometry as policy context.} Convert a global denial decision into a
        map-sector decision.
  \item \textbf{Aggregate access burden.} Sum route costs over the resource field instead
        of looking only at the nearest tree.
  \item \textbf{Near-tie trade.} Accept a bounded own-efficiency penalty for a more useful
        denial target, but only where the costs are close.
  \item \textbf{Frozen strategic intent.} Make the species choice at $t=0$ to prevent
        high-level target churn.
  \item \textbf{Behavioral bridge.} Prove exact parent behavior outside the sector and
        exact treatment behavior inside it.
  \item \textbf{Mechanism decomposition.} Distinguish own production gain from opponent
        suppression; E7a's development gain is primarily the latter.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{13. Limitations and next priorities}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The selection used consumed labels; no same-window parent control exists.
  \item The policy has a hard two-worker ceiling and no renewable macro-economy.
  \item Only PLUM versus LEMON is conditioned, and the decision never adapts after $t=0$.
  \item Scores are hand tuned and deterministic; there is no learned opponent model.
  \item The orchard can reserve one of two workers indefinitely.
  \item The live tail is large: 35 catastrophes and 10,045 negative-margin mass.
  \item Legal movement is not sufficient for progress; long executed ABAB routes remain.
\end{itemize}

The best immediate engineering task is a baseline-preserving bank-commitment/liveness fix:
once a wood carrier chooses to return, its destination and progress measure must remain
stable until DROP or a proved obstruction. Validate against all 25 E7a counterexamples and
the earlier ring counterexample before any value test. The larger research priority remains
a scalable renewable economy with worker-three funding and concurrent production.
