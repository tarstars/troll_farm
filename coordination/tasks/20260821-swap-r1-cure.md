## THE RULE BEING IMPLEMENTED (owner-approved, R-1, 2026-08-16)

> When two of our trolls need to pass each other, the movement coordinator must recognize
> it and issue the coordinated exchange — both step toward each other in one tick. The
> referee explicitly allows own-unit circular swaps (`docs/mechanics.md:54-56`); today's
> resident never generates them, which is self-imposed. In dead-end corridors a swap is
> the ONLY resolution (yielding has nowhere to go).

Game facts that frame it: two of OUR units cannot end on one cell (contested → highest id
wins; circular swaps allowed); a blocked MOVE emits `MOVE_BLOCKED` and the unit stays put;
enemy units may share our cell, so **every block is our own troll**.

## WHERE IT LIVES — the transport level and nothing else

The seam is `MoisanBot::resolve_move_conflicts_with_priority_and_forbidden`
(`candidate-door1-pure-deletion.rs:720`): it projects each selected MOVE's landing cell,
reserves the cells of **stationary** own units, and a mover whose landing is reserved takes
a detour neighbour or becomes `WAIT`. It never considers the exchange. α adds exactly that:

- **Trigger:** a mover's projected landing is the cell of a stationary own unit U (U's
  command this tick is not a MOVE), AND either U is idle (`WAIT`) or the mover has **no
  detour** (no free walkable neighbour that does not lose ground toward its target).
- **Action:** emit the exchange in the same tick — mover → U.cell, U → mover.cell — as two
  MOVEs; U's pending command (CHOP / HARVEST / WAIT) is displaced for that tick and the
  planner re-targets U next tick (expected on 005/027: U is back on the tree within 2 ticks;
  **measure it**, do not assume it).
- **Not touched:** candidate generation, `MoisanBot::select` (the pair selector),
  `force_unique_door_clear`, any scoring, the generator's fallbacks, the replant block. A
  change outside `resolve_move_conflicts*` is out of scope and must be declared if it
  proves unavoidable.
- **Inert by construction:** on any tick where the trigger does not fire, the command
  stream must be **byte-identical** to the base's. That is G-1's parity control and the
  reason this cure can travel separately from the benching cure.
