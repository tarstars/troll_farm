## Owner hypothesis

Search for an outcome-blind sector of the joint initial state — starting resource vector
plus map configuration — in which changing the resident's behavior has positive terminal
value. Example only, not a conclusion: on genuinely rich maps, collect a specific training
bill earlier and train a harvest/carry/chop worker.

The target is an interaction: `sector × finite intervention → incremental value`. A sector
that merely predicts which games are easy, who later scales, or terminal score does not
justify a policy change.
