## The rule (owner, plain words)

The second troll is never weaker than speed 2, carry 2, chop 2 (harvest power stays 0, as before). The bot waits until it can pay for such a troll (5 plums, 5 lemons, 1 apple, 5 iron); when a stronger one is fundable within its usual 15-turn horizon it takes the stronger one, exactly as before. The turn-35 deadline stays: from turn 35, if the wanted troll is still unaffordable, the bot takes the strongest floored troll it can afford right now, and otherwise keeps waiting for the basic 2/2/0/2 — it never gives up training. Everything else is the champion.

Why: training buys talents at a quadratic price (plums n+speed², lemons n+carry², apples n+harvest², iron n+chop²); a felled tree's wood is dealt one piece per round to each chopper with free carry, so carry caps the haul (carry 1 takes 1 wood of 4); chop power divides the turns to fell. The leaders (yaichi, Stounate, skotz, Risen, goq, DaNinja, GoodDevel) train 2/2/0/2 or a one-step upgrade in ≥ 95 % of games and never below it; our chooser (`choose_second_troll`) takes the strongest troll fundable within 15 turns and settles for 1/2/0/2, 2/1/0/2, 2/2/0/1 … when the draw is poor. In the apple farm's round 2 those games won 34 % against 66 %; in round 3, 51 % against 61 % (own score −48 in both). Every real ladder map has iron (0 of 24,021 without), so 5 iron is always minable.
