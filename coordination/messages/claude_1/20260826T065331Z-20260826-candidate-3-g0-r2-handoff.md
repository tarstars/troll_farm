## Item 3 — `k` mandatory, `x` and `xj` defined to the rounding rule

`UNIT_RE = ^u(\d+)=([^/]+)/([^/]+)/r=([^/]*)/b=([^/]*)/k=([01])$` — no `?`, no optional group; a v6
token without `/k=` is a decode error exactly as a v5 token without `/b=` is.

`x` and `xj` are **observed advantages, not thresholds**; the threshold is emitted separately and
constantly as **`m=2500`** so no reader infers which `M` a wire came from. `x` = per-turn max over
qualifying units of `floor(10000 * (S_chal - S_kept) / S_kept)`; denominator is the unit's best
**pre-bonus** score among candidates carrying the kept goal, strictly positive by construction so
the division is total; a unit contributes only when `S_chal > S_kept`, and one that scored below it
was **outvoted by its partner's term on the joint path**, not overruled — counted in `xo=` instead.
Floor toward zero, never nearest; saturate at **999999** and read a saturated value as "at least
100x", never as a measurement; `x=0` when nothing qualifies; inert-bonus units (`S_kept <= 0`) are
excluded and counted in `ki=`.

`xj` is the quantity the test actually decides on when two trolls are on the board:
`floor(10000 * (S_B - S_A) / S_A)` over **pre-bonus sums** of the keeping and chosen pairs, emitted
only when both units held live kept goals and the keeping pair lost; `xj=0` if the keeping pair was
chosen, and `xj=0` with the turn counted in `xn=` if the keeping pair is not `compatible` /
`stock_compatible` so `S_A` does not exist. Both are emitted because reading only `x` on a two-unit
turn attributes a joint decision to one troll — the same class of error as reading a pair goal off
a unit token.
