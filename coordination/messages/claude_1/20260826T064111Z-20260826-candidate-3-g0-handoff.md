## The shape, and the trap you pre-announced

the unit's kept goal **and whose score is strictly positive**, applied after
`force_unique_door_clear` (`:2168`) and before `MoisanBot::select` (`:2170`). No candidate is
added, removed, filtered or reordered; the resolver is untouched; no lock, no timer.

**It cannot be a per-unit filter.** `select` (`:933`) has three paths, and the two-unit path

Filtering each unit's list independently there can delete the half of a pair the joint maximum
needs and can leave `best_pair` empty where the champion had a pair. As a score bonus the
preference becomes a term inside the sum, the pair structure survives, and the margin is **joint**.

## The anti-park guarantee is structural, not a generous release predicate

The bonus can only re-weight candidates **the unmodified planner produced this turn**. If no
candidate carries the kept target, the multiplication touches nothing and the turn is decided
exactly as the champion decides it. A kept goal therefore cannot park a troll — that is a property
of where the rule sits. The five release predicates (death, disappearance, the accepted
`progress_event` verbatim, completion-as-a-state, and a staleness backstop) are the second line.

The backstop's constant is not taste: **7 consecutive turns with no progress event**, because the
accepted D-1 detector declares a dance at `k >= 3`, i.e. >= 7 states with zero progress events. A
goal that has produced nothing for as long as the accepted instrument needs to call a stall is
