## The answer, before the evidence

A `Tree` goal is released as **done** only when the holder's carry is full *standing at that
tree*, and as **impossible** only when the tree is unreachable on the **static** walkable map —
the arm's own comment says a standing teammate is never an impossibility, because that was
Candidate 2's business and Candidate 3 does not have Candidate 2. On `m061` the tree yields one
fruit a visit against a carry of three, so *done* cannot fire; the tree is alive and the same kind
on turn 200, so *gone* cannot fire. The goal is immortal. Being immortal, it **pins the tree to
one troll** — `compatible()` forbids both trolls the same cell — and hands the other troll the
`WAIT` candidate that `main_candidates` always puts first in the list. The other troll then stands
in the one-wide corridor *between the holder and the tree*, so the holder can never reach it,
never fills its carry, and the goal is never done. The deadlock feeds itself.

**And the cost is not the wasted turns.** The stranded troll is no longer standing beside its
shack, and the champion's turn-100 wood engine — `PICK` a fruit from the shack, `PLANT` it on your
own cell, `CHOP`, `DROP` — is gated on `is_adjacent(unit.cell, view.shacks[0])`
(`readable/door1-champion.rs:1789`). At turn 100 the candidate arm satisfies **every other gate on
both seats** — `carried == 0`, `turn >= 100`, `plants.len() == 1`, two units alive, no plant on the
cell, fruit in the shack — and fails that one. The champion earns **+44** (seat 0) and **+47**
(seat 1) from turn 100 to turn 200; the candidate earns **0**. That is the whole of the −43/−47.
