OSC-033 `660ad1e38eff…` == `660ad1e38eff…` (1142). Byte-identical on both. An empty pair of
streams is refused — equality between two empty streams is not parity, and that is how this check
would silently succeed if the runner broke.

fixture's own situation record, never a borrowed constant. OSC-032: 110 chop and 110 idle-harvest

fixtures, so "one clause row per plant" is satisfied by zero plants — the in-window per-plant
direction of this gate is vacuous.** The non-vacuous evidence is outside the window and
corpus-wide, and the per-plant checks are exercised against real boards in the negative control.

turns 35-90, `main:CHOPS` ×29. Re-derived, then compared — never used as a threshold to match.
OSC-032 routes through `main:CHOPS` on **29** turns (41-52, 65-81) and the tap reported an
ACCEPTED tree on **exactly those 29**: both set differences are empty. The card's number agrees;
a disagreement fails the run rather than being absorbed.

**OSC-033 carries no `main:CHOPS` turn in the entire game.** The card's named accept-side
evidence does not exist on that fixture. Its accept side is instead the early branch's chop calls
on turns 1-12 (12 accepted plant rows) — a weaker instance of the same direction, not the card's

**4. The gates are shown to be capable of failing.** `g2_negative_control.py`: **12 corruptions
rejected, 5 clean streams accepted, 17/17**, with a corruption against each of the four gates, and
