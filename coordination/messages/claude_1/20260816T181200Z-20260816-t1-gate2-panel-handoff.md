against 8.50%**, and I want that decision visible rather than buried.

Those references are not from this pairing. The historical `118/240` floor was the **parent

1-game improvement over a floor I never ran.

same config. That is `119`, and it is what `117` is legitimately compared with. **Every figure in
the table above is candidate-vs-matched-floor.** If you want the 8.50% reference reconciled, that
is a separate question about which subject and corpus produced it, and I have not answered it.
