**E1 — the D-switch projection (question 1) is a hole.** The champion's `collection_eta` counts one
item per round trip from the door (`missing × (2d + 2)`); it ignores carry and harvest power. With the
floor costs `n + v²` and the shack spent down after each purchase (the componentwise-maximum spec),
the fourth troll's floor is 34 items: at turn 106 the design's eta is 34 × 8 / 2 = 136, so 106 + 136
= 242 > 185 and the mode switches to Deforest right after the third troll — in every game. The corpus
buys a fourth troll in 61 % of games (median turn 132–138). Exact edit: eta per resource =
trips × (2d + dwell), trips = ceil(missing / carry), dwell = ceil(min(carry, missing) / harvest);
with the `2/3/1/2` funder that gives 158 at roster 3 (stay) and 212 at roster 4 (switch), which is
the corpus frontier. Two unit tests named in the review; also say that iron is omitted from the sum
on iron-free maps, or the sentinel switches those maps on turn 1.

**E2 — priority step 3 "carrying anything: bank" is a hole.** A troll gains min(harvest, free) a turn;
under step 3 every troll whose carry exceeds its harvest power (all but the first) banks after one
fruit — three trips per load for a carry-3 funder. That is the opposite of the 91 % full-load drops
the step cites, and the champion's own code does not do it (partial loads bank only when adjacent to
the shack or out of work; the standing tree scores +900). Exact edit: delete step 3; let the
champion's HARVEST/MINE candidates run for partial loads; bank a partial load only when the gate has
no productive candidate or the unit is adjacent to the shack. One unit test named.

**Question 2 — the one orchard job plus one banana job: no hole.** The July loop is closed at its
source (reserved cell before PICK, one global job, no job on a TRAIN turn, the selector's stock
check). Three things to state as one line each: the banana loop's fuel is the starting stock only
(about six a seat; the corpus converts 6.1 a game, so the bound is consistent); a carried
next-floor-deficit fruit is banked, never claimed by an orchard job; the orchard closing at the
switch drops the corpus's late plum/lemon planting (P 309 / L 266 in turns 250–299) — a named
default, and the first item for the refinement-loop list.

**Question 3 — v6: no nonzero counter is required.** All-zero counters satisfy the four census
equations; `b=` must be 0 and `k=0` throughout. What must carry truth: `t=`, one token per own
unit in id order, the chosen target of the emitted command, and `r=` from the resolver (`N` on a
turn without MOVE, `P` on a primary-path MOVE, `W` on a forced WAIT). One `MSG` token per turn,
first, banner inside it before `NARRATE`; a separate turn-1 announcement `MSG` fails the bed.
