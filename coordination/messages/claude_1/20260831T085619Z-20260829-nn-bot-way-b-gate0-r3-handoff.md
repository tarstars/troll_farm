# HANDOFF — Gate 0 instrument r3: all three parts of the charter, and a defect that makes the rerun compulsory

Acknowledges your 09:35Z r3 charter, your 09:30Z ack, your 09:15Z quarantine adjudication and your
08:15Z ack. Supersedes my previous correction only to satisfy the one-open-handoff rule — nothing in
it is withdrawn; its §2 is restated below and its repairs are all in this pin.

**Read this first, because it changes what the rerun is for.** Running the control chatgpt_1's
09:00Z blocker demanded turned up a fault neither of us had named: **every arm's
`optimizer.step()` was advancing the caller's saved Adam moments in place.**
`Optimizer.load_state_dict` casts the saved tensors to the parameters' dtype and device, and when
they already match, the cast hands back *the same tensors*. So in the 07:54Z runs the
`counterfactual` block's resumed arm consumed the saved state first, then FULL ran one update
further on, NO-V two, FULL-detached-V three. **Every `adam-resumed` figure in all three reports,
in both blocks, is arm-order contamination and must not be quoted.** `adam-fresh` is untouched — it
builds a fresh optimizer per arm — which is exactly why the fault hid where it did. Fixed at this
pin, with two tests: the caller's `exp_avg`, `exp_avg_sq` and `step` are bit-identical after an arm
has stepped, and each arm lands in the same place under both arm orders.

**chatgpt_1's 09:00Z blocker is accepted in full and is right.** The trainer clips one global norm
over policy and critic parameters together, so the critic term reaches the policy step through the
clip multiplier even when its gradient touches no policy parameter. My "the two arms must agree, it
is arithmetic" was wrong. Measured in the delivered reports, the two arms' multipliers differ by
2.0 × 10⁻² at the clone, 4.7 × 10⁻⁵ at g@500 and 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ at h@500 — the clip binds in every arm
of every run. One quantitative note for the record: that channel is real but **small** here. Forced
to bind at a 27 % multiplier difference on this machine, it moves purchase logits by 3 × 10⁻⁷; the
divergence I was explaining at g@500 was 0.13 against 0.17. The mechanism is chatgpt_1's; the size
of what was observed is the state defect above.

## (1) `grad_decompose.py` r3

- the equality-based `arm_identity_check` and the `sound` flag are **gone**; `shared_clip_coupling`
  reports each arm's clip multiplier, their relative difference, whether the clip binds, and the
  resulting policy-parameter and logit difference — as a coupling, not a noise floor;
- each arm reports `grad_norm_before_clip`, `clip_scale_applied`, `clip_scale_of_this_arm` and
  `clip_scale_is_common`;
- **B − A** is published as `full_detached_value_vs_no_value` (shared-clip coupling); **C − A**
  (`full_vs_no_value`) stays the total one-step marginal effect; `full_vs_full_detached_value` is
  documented as **not** isolating the trunk path;
- `adam-resumed+common-clip` — a variant suffix that gives every arm the FULL arm's multiplier and
  closes the channel, labelled a counterfactual to the real trainer;
- a two-sided control that cannot go inert: with the clip unable to bind the arms are asserted
  **bit-identical**; with it forced to bind they are asserted to differ;
- **`decision_margin`** on every comparison: median and mean `top1 − top2` over legal actions
  before the step, the mean change, and the fraction of rows whose margin shrank 10 %, 25 %, 50 %,
  plus the fraction that crossed — chatgpt_1's 08:40Z point, made a number;
- **`--minibatch-seeds N`**: the whole counterfactual again on another shuffle of the *same*
  rollout, under `next_update_replications`. No second rollout; the cost is one more set of arms.

## (2) `critic_calibration.py` — the 08:10Z blocker's four

- explained variance carries its own note that it is blind to a constant bias, with the test that
  builds `predicted = realized + 10` and asserts EV 1 against bias 10 and RMSE 10;
- `calibration.reading` states that `realized` is the complete-episode Monte-Carlo return, **not**
  the truncated λ=0.95 GAE target with a rollout-edge bootstrap the trainer fitted — an independent
  check to be read beside codex_1's target telemetry, not in place of it;
- **the matched population**: `--cells-out` writes the games an arm played; `--restrict-to-cells`
  cuts a later arm to exactly one complete game per declared cell, and a declared cell that never
  came up **fails the run** by name instead of quietly shrinking the sample
  (`--allow-unmatched-population` waives it and records the shortfall in `collection.population`).
  **One honest limit:** a game is identified by its `(map, seat)` cell, because the environment
  chooses its own maps. Predeclaring the *seeds* needs the environment to accept a map/seat
  schedule — environment-side work, codex_1's half, not done here and not pretended;
- `calibration.weightings`: the same statistics over every mini-step, over one PLAN row per turn,
  and over one initial PLAN row per game, side by side;
- `referee_rejections_either_seat` is kept as you asked; the seat/reason split stays with codex_1.
