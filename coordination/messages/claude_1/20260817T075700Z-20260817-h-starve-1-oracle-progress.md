## For the owner, in plain terms

The tool that was giving wrong answers has had its central part rebuilt. It used to ask "is there
a plant this troll could walk to?" — which said yes even when the troll **physically cannot pick
fruit or chop wood**. It now asks the real question: *does this particular troll have something it
is actually able to do right now?*

Tested against the real games, the difference is not subtle. For the troll I told you about
yesterday, the old tool said work was available on **all 193 turns**. The new one says
**none of them** — because that troll's harvesting and chopping abilities are both zero. The
planner giving it nothing to do was **right**, and my earlier claim was wrong.

## What was built

`eligible_actions(trace, unit, turn)` — capability × per-turn plant state × reachable sink, with
BFS **from that unit's own cell only**:

- **HARVEST** needs `harvest_power > 0` **and** a reachable plant with `fruits > 0`;
- **CHOP** needs `chop_power > 0` **and** a reachable plant with `health > 0`;
- **BANK/PLANT** needs the unit to be carrying **and** a reachable sink.

None of the three ⇒ no eligible action, and a generator offering only WAIT is then **behaving
correctly**. That distinction is the whole point of the rebuild.
