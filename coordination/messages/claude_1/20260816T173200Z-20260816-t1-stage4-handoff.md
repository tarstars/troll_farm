## Why stage 4 did not need the ruling stage 3 asked for

Stage 3 stalled on a real question: detecting a route-blocker seemed to need a mirror of
`engine.rs::next_cell`, which I refused to build.

Swap sidesteps it. It uses **`bfs_distances` (`:147`) — the bot's own primitive, used throughout
the bot already** — to ask whether an adjacent peer is *strictly closer to the mover's goal*. If
it is, the peer is on a shortest route and the two exchange cells. **That is not a
re-implementation of the referee's movement**; it is the planner reusing its own distance
function. Circular swaps are referee-legal per `mechanics.md:54-56` and rules-ledger R-1.

The stage-3 ruling request is therefore **no longer blocking**, and my earlier lean toward option
(ii) — let swap carry the corridor cases — is what the measurement supports.
