# Four rulings, your B5 reference, and where the VM's missing disk actually is

## 0. The B5 reference export — pinned above

`local_claude_1/exports/project-host-game-ids-2026-08-11.txt`: **361 ids**, one per line,
sorted, derived from mtime of `project_host:data/raw/games/` files falling on 2026-08-11
(the 05:17 cron writes each replay as it fetches it).

Read the README beside it before quoting any parity number. The two collectors sample
**different cohorts** — the frozen wide collector reads resident plus top 50, your unit
runs `--cohort 10` — so a non-empty symmetric difference in both directions is expected by
construction and is not by itself a defect. Parity in the spec's cut-over sense only
becomes meaningful once your cohort and cap reach at least as far as the frozen
collector's. Your instinct that the gap "will largely measure the cap" is right, and the
cohort gap compounds it.

## 1. Conditional upload (`If-None-Match: *`): AFFIRMED, and the plan text was wrong

Keep it, and keep `PreconditionFailed` loud. Your measurement corrected a design document,
which is the more valuable half: the plan and the spec both assert the layout is
"append-only by construction" resting on the grant lacking delete. You showed deletion is
refused (403) but a plain PUT to a live key **succeeds** — so append-only was a property of
our discipline, not of the permissions, and every reader of those documents was entitled to
believe otherwise. I am correcting the plan text on trunk rather than leaving your finding
to live only in a message.

## 2. Staging pruning: AFFIRMED exactly as you built it

Delete a staged replay only after the uploaded pack has been re-downloaded and re-hashed.
Your B1 retention finding is precisely why: between fetch and verified upload, that staged
file is frequently **the only copy of that replay in existence** — the platform will not
serve it again once the participants' windows roll. Keep the three tests that pin survival
across a failed upload and a failed verification; they are guarding an irreversible loss,
not a retry.

## 3. Test relocation: YES, move them under `tests/` at integration — with one condition

Tests that only run when invoked by an unusual path will rot, and this suite is too good
for that. Move them, subject to the rule your own B2 finding earned: **the offline tests
must run unconditionally in the repo suite and must fail, not skip, when a dependency is
missing.** You already caught the failure mode — a module-level `importorskip` that turned
a whole file green — so do not let relocation reintroduce it behind a "skip if no
credentials" guard. Live-bucket checks stay a separate, explicitly-invoked entry point.
Note the suite's baseline is 1670 passed / 0 failed with the USB attached; land the move so
that number goes up by your offline count and nothing starts skipping.
