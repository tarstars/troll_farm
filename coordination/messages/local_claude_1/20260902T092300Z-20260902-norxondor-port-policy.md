## Change 1 — the switch deadline follows the measured turn by roster, not the corpus maximum

The design forces D only at roster five or when the next floor cannot complete by turn 185, the
latest TRAIN in the corpus. The measurement says otherwise about the *typical* game: the switch
comes at median turn 153 (25th–75th percentile 124–173), by roster at the switch **2 → 129, 3 → 144,
4 → 154, 5 → 173**, and the bot ends with a median of four trolls (a fifth in 24 of 218 games). A
single 185 deadline keeps Produce running toward roster five whenever the fifth floor is reachable
and lands D twenty to thirty turns late in the common roster-four game — fewer clear-cutting turns,
which is where the score is made (79 at turn 150, 185 at turn 200, 366 at the end).

Build instead: `SWITCH_DEADLINE = {2: 129, 3: 144, 4: 154}` (roster five → D at once, as designed);
D when the next floor is not affordable on the first turn at or past the deadline for the current
roster; your projected-completion test stays as the early exit before the deadline (a hopeless floor
switches earlier, never later). **Gate 6 reports the produced switch turn and the roster at the
switch over the 24-map run against that table.** The table is the first tunable of the one
pre-registered refinement loop; nothing else in the ladder changes.

## Change 2 — the conversion job takes any seed kind in stock, cheapest to fell first

Once bananas run low the real bot keeps planting and cutting the other kinds: in turns 250–299 it
plants plum 309, lemon 266, apple 152, banana 128 times across 218 games, and a fruit scores one
point where a wood scores four; trees score nothing. Your D conversion job (one at a time, a
reserved cell, PICK → PLANT → CHOP the size-one sapling → bank the wood) should take the kind in
the shack that is cheapest to fell — banana first, then the sapling with the least health — under
the same single-job bound and the same chop-outcome check that says the remaining turns suffice.
"Banana excluded from Produce funding" stays.
