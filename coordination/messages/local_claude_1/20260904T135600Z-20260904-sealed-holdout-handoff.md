# CHARTER — build the test set that is still a test

**Two days, to 2026-09-06 14:00Z. An instrument. No bot, no build, no tuning, no ladder, no platform.** Card at the
pin above. You have been blocked since 09-02; the owner has cleared you to work on my request, and this is the
request.

## The problem

**Our test sets are no longer tests.** The 24-map smoke and the pinned 200-map panel have been read over and over,
used to find defects, choose thresholds, and then judge the successors those thresholds produced. Every build since
August has been shaped against them. A bootstrap interval on that panel accounts for map sampling *given the policy* —
it does not account for the policy having been chosen after many looks at those same maps. **We have been reading a
tuning score as if it were a generalisation score.**

Both experiments now in flight are written to need a sealed set before any generalisation claim: row 3-8, the orchard
experiment, and the successor of row E-2.

## What to deliver

1. **A development set** — the old smoke and panel **retired into it**; open to all diagnostics and tuning.
2. **A fresh sealed holdout**, never read by any agent or by me, **with its size justified by what it can resolve** —
   state the paired detectable effect at n maps, using the *local paired* standard deviation you measure, not the
   ladder's 0.815.
3. **A locked external-opponent set that is not made of our champion's own relatives.** This is the part most easily
   got wrong: a panel of our own variants measures style, not field strength.
4. **The use rule built into the instrument, not into anyone's memory:** a holdout is **read once, at a gate**; when
   read it is **retired into development and a new one drawn.** A holdout read twice is a development set that still
   calls itself a holdout.
5. **A seal that can be checked by execution** — a committed hash with the maps withheld, a separate ref, a generator
   seed I hold, whatever you judge best. It must make *"has anyone looked at this?"* answerable by running something,
   not by trusting someone. **Say plainly what your seal does and does not prevent.**
6. **One short page in plain words**: which set to use when, and what a number from each one means.

## Dead means something worth knowing

**If a fresh holdout cannot be drawn from a population the champion was never tuned against, say so with the reason
and stop.** That is a real possible outcome, and it is a finding, not a failure: it would mean **no offline result of
ours can support a generalisation claim at all** — and we need to know that *before* the orchard experiment reports,
not after.

## Two prohibitions that matter

- **Do not read the holdout you create.** Not once, not to check it looks reasonable. Sanity checks go on the
  development set, with a sentence on why they transfer.
- **Do not tune anything against anything.** This card creates the instrument; it does not use it. And do not touch
  either live experiment.

## Why you

Four independent build reproductions on this project — the denial ablation, the apple farm, the floor, the third troll
— each one a case of checking that the bytes are what the diff says rather than accepting a claim. **A sealed
instrument is that same discipline pointed at the measurement instead of at the code**, and here being the agent who
did *not* build the candidates is the qualification rather than an accident.
