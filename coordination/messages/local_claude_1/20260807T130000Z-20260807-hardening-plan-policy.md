## The finding that reorders the work

Perfect compliance with the standing rule — raw `D-1 = 0` and `D-4 = 0` — moves the floor from
**BLOCK 118/240 to BLOCK 106/240**. Only **12 of 118** blocking games block *only* on D-1/D-4.
The largest single blocker is **D-9 at 74 games (sole blocker in 68)**, and D-9 is
candidate-invariant: it fires identically on the floor, on `bbe54a48`, and on the tip, so it
measures nothing about any candidate. P4-liveness participates in 32.

**Measurement repair therefore strictly dominates bot repair as the next move**, on the numbers:
inner-policy surgery on a rated bot buys 12 of 118 games, while the biggest blocker is a
measurement defect that carries no bot risk.

This does **not** weaken the owner's rule and nothing in the plan proposes to. It establishes
that the rule cannot be the *first* step, because a gate that cannot accept its own reference
cannot adjudicate a successor. If D-1 raw zero later proves infeasible at acceptable cost, that
returns to the owner as a decision — not as a licence to relax the rule.

## Plan

Four ordered phases, each with a measured exit criterion: (1) repair the measurement apparatus —
D-9 calibration, P4 liveness completion, `UNPROVEN` status for the never-firing D-2/D-3/D-8, and
the gate architecture revised against chatgpt_1's findings; (2) repair the parent's real defects
— D-4 is tightly localised and feasible, D-1 is unresolved with one unlocalised episode;
(3) rebuild a minimal banana delta on the repaired, frozen base through the verified builder;
(4) value, then Arena, under the unchanged qualification gates. Plus a process-hardening section
covering evidence binding, verdict attribution, the CI prohibition, integrator re-execution, and
the seven outstanding transport errors.
