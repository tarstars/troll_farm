# REVIEW — opening solver Stage 2 design

**Verdict: ACCEPT-WITH-EDITS.** Stage 1 is enough to charter a rules-first Stage 2, but not yet to claim the measured 21-turn idle-board gain against live opponents or to use 8 trees per 3 chop as a fixed farm policy.

1. **Objective.** Keep a Pareto frontier of full roster states, not a chop sum and not one preselected `c`. Within the same roster, earliest completion remains primary. Across rosters, rank the frontier by a fixed turn-300 continuation value applied to live state: current banked score plus expected additional fruit score plus four times wood actually banked by turn 300, minus expected raid loss. The continuation policy/value function can be fixed offline; its tree, distance, carry, position, and roster inputs cannot. A raw `chop * (300-arrival)` term is not acceptable because walking is 64% of the solver's commands. The measured curve itself shows why: chop 1/2/3 arrive at median turns 43/46/58, while all three report completion-state value 25.

2. **Idle opponent.** The exact median gain — solver turn 70 versus orchard 6 turn 88.5, gap 21 on 292 map-seats — remains an idle-board potential. An opponent can change a scheduled wild tree's fruit/existence before the median first harvest at turn 9, or occupy an intended plant cell before the median plant at turn 5; those events require repair. It cannot block our route: enemy units may share cells. Iron is not depleting and enemy mining therefore does not consume ours. Do not build an adversarial path/iron allocator. Build tree/plant-state validation and test the replanner against mirrored strong and recorded/top openings. The two major measured gains should survive: one-item trips cost 7 turns median on 43/51 seats, and a late second troll costs 7 turns on the 30 affected seats.

3. **Rules first.** Ship immediate affordable turn-1 second-troll training and useful-load trips first. Make "full load" a default, not an absolute: a partial load may be correct when it clears the next bill sooner. Keep PICK-before-TRAIN and DROP-after-TRAIN affordability as a hard invariant; a seed must not delay the known bill. Water-aware planting is a useful tail heuristic, not a co-equal explanation of the 21 turns: its ablation median is 0, mean 2.1, on 18/51 seats, versus the two 7-turn effects above. Exact legality, task order, capacity, target validation, and no-op prevention stay hand-written; the planner chooses roster, tree, amount, seed programme, and mining time.

4. **Farm balance.** The derivation `F < sqrt(P/(6h))` is algebraically sound under its constant-hazard, 12-health, no-travel assumptions. Its policy interpretation is too strong. At `P=3`, `h=0.008`, `F=8`, the same model gives about 1.02 expected raids; under the corresponding Poisson approximation the chance of at least one raid is about 64%. The page itself changes the bound from 16 trees before turn 100 to 8 after, and omits the measured 64% walking share. Placement and planned conversion order should be first-class; size should emerge from fruit timing, route cost, felling turn, and accumulated turn/distance risk. Keep 8-per-3 only as an after-turn-100 sanity check. Use the verified corrections: 114/1,706 = 6.68% raided and 1,587 self-felled.

5. **Overclaims/gates.** Correct the known wording: the ablation means sum to 17 against a median gap about 18; the mean gap is 21.7. Also benchmark the actual online budget. Stage 1 uses about 1,800 rollouts and 30 seconds per free map-seat at 64 rollouts/s; one Python second buys about 60 deterministic plans plus 5 refinements, and the claimed 30–100x Rust speedup is not measured yet. This matters because 22/292 same-roster seats are already slower than orchard 6, worst by 20 turns, from search misses. Require a budget-versus-offline-frontier curve before integration. Expand beyond the `(2/3/0/c)` sweep because the task card's top openings use full tuples such as `(2/3/1/2)` and `(2/4/1/c)`.

6. **Build first.** Implement a deterministic referee-exact receding-horizon Rust controller: generate the full-roster Pareto frontier in the first-turn budget, rank it with the turn-300 live-state continuation value, execute one legal macro-action, and use each later 50 ms turn to validate and repair the script. Pass an idle budget-quality gate and a contested-tree repair gate before any learned action lane or ladder claim.
