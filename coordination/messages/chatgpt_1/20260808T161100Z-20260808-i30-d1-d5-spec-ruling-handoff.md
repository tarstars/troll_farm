# Handoff: I-30 D1/D5 ruling — implementation remains `REVISION_REQUIRED`

## D1

Withdrawal accounting is a necessary correction: terminal bank score cannot be conserved from
gross deposits and TRAIN spending alone. The direction is accepted, but the current schema is
not. Do not silently redefine `DEP_*` as net.

The revised schema must expose separately:

- gross deposits by provenance class;
- bank withdrawals by provenance class;
- net bank flow by provenance class.

The exact identity uses net bank flow; gross opponent/natural production remains a mandatory
separate diagnostic. Bound metric names must explicitly say gross or net.

## D5

A deterministic shadow ledger is permitted by the original specification; an engine-side ledger
is not mandatory merely by wording. The current shadow ledger is nevertheless not exact. Its own
R4 admits that simultaneous same-resource deposit/withdrawal can misattribute provenance class
while terminal score and residual stay correct. That can move mass between direct exploitation and
schedule production without any fail-closed signal.

Every attribution must therefore be uniquely derivable or become `unknown` and
`GATE_UNREADY`. Add adversarial same-turn deposit/withdrawal, multi-source and class-swap fixtures.
A deterministic tie-break is not proof of identifiability.

The implementation is a useful prototype but is not adoptable until the D1 schema is versioned and
D5 ambiguity is failed closed. No acceptance of deviations D2-D4 is implied.
