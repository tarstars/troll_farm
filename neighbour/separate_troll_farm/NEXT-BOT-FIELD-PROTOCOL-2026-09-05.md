# Prospective productive-economy screen

Frozen before any of these new maps are played. This compares complete policies, not individual
causal contributions of each capital-planning edit. No rating conversion will be fitted.

- Policies: `serial_safe` (V1 allocation), `parallel_safe` (V2 allocation), exact V468 and exact
  V368. Both new policies share two post-panel correctness fixes: reserve same-turn seed stock
  and print WAIT when no action exists. No other gameplay tuning during the screen.
- Three new blocks: putibuzu/6479779/seed 2609051701; delineate/6479768/seed 2609051102;
  PonyPonyCodeCode/6541377/seed 2609051603. Those seeds have not appeared in either development
  panel or earlier field pilot. The agents were ranks 7, 1 and 60 in the previous board read.
- Twelve sequential unranked games, one per policy/block, all player zero. Rotate order by block:
  V468/serial/V368/parallel; serial/V368/parallel/V468; V368/parallel/V468/serial.
- Verify exact source hashes, compile both exports, run all behavioral fixtures in both profiles,
  and compare readable/compact streams before field execution. Preserve raw responses, full
  replays, identities, initial-state hashes and every outcome. No automatic request retries.
- Stop for API/identity/seed/protocol failures and retain the partial study. Do not replace seeds
  or silently remove failed games. The same-state A/A capability was established in the prior
  pilot; this is not a claim that every agent is universally deterministic.
- Primary readout: official W/D/L and paired match points. Retain per-block margins, own/opponent
  score, wood, successful hires and errors to distinguish economic mechanisms. Three maps are
  descriptive: no p-value, general rating estimate or automatic publication.
- A new profile earns a larger, separately frozen confirmation only if it wins additional blocks
  without losing a reference win, or produces a consistent material competitive improvement
  across the tested opponents that merits further measurement. All-loss/tiny-margin changes do
  not demonstrate top-seven strength. An older policy beating both new profiles redirects work
  toward that evidence rather than preserving the new architecture by inertia.
