# Productive economy controller — frozen first experiment

The field pilot, not the old queue, motivates this candidate. Dense V543/V564 can lose all lemon
sources, spend the whole game harvesting other fruit and never issue CHOP. Existing workers must
have a complete productive continuation when another worker cannot be funded.

1. Extract V564's economy, protocol/types/navigation and movement resolver into `next_bot/`.
   Keep one editable controller and a deterministic standalone exporter, without the unused
   V468/adaptive wrappers. First verify the extracted control on V564's three exact field streams.
2. Write executable state tests before behavior changes: unreachable training resources must not
   keep a bill active; a capable existing worker must select payable wood over irrelevant fruit;
   value must clip wood to free carry and include chopping/travel/banking time; late purchases
   must leave time to repay. Preserve complete seed/plant/drop transactions and source renewal.
3. Replace ordinal-only producer allocation with capability- and trip-value-based productive work.
   Separate feasible capital acquisition from ordinary production. Do not impose a turn-100 prefix
   or assume a third/fourth worker is always necessary. Keep opening changes isolated where possible.
4. Use the historical eight-map panel for regressions and command issues, not the old +40 gate.
   Diagnose W/D/L, paired match points, margins, and per-opponent mechanisms. Add real denial
   replay fixtures, but do not call fixed command replay an adaptive opponent comparison.
5. Freeze a bounded prospective real-agent comparison after executable validation and before
   seeing new outcomes. Include V468 and the earlier V368 reference; do not choose by a lucky
   past rank. Fresh seeds and independent later confirmation are required for a publication claim.

The current experiment must establish a working productive economy, not merely fewer waits or
more fruit. Source/module identity and behavior checks are prerequisites, not evidence of rank.
No gameplay change is copied into `bot.rs` or `submission.rs` until a supported finalist is chosen.

## V1 result and bounded V2 revision (before new V2 measurements)

V1 passed ten executable behavior fixtures and the readable/compact check. Its eight-map panel
produced 156/0/36 versus V468's 177/3/12, with +12.17 wood but -34.86 margin. Every opponent-family
margin regressed. V1 used one capital funder and completed four workers only 26/192 times, versus
175/192 in archived V564. Its collection estimate also charged all resource trips serially.

V2 tests parallel funding while a finite viable bill exists: use at most two harvest-capable
funders selected by their relative chopping opportunity cost, estimate overlapping acquisition,
and finish a multi-fruit harvest load before banking unless that deposit completes the bill.
Once the bill is impossible, both workers still use productive trip values. The first hire and
all other V1 production/renewal choices stay fixed. This is a tested allocation revision, not a
claim that worker count or the old score curve establishes strength. Use the same regression maps;
no fresh validation maps have yet been opened.
