# Two-worker capital release: negative development result

## Decision

Do not publish the two-worker variant or open fresh confirmation for it. It wins the same
one of three real-agent development blocks as its renewal parent and loses eight match points
on the familiar local panel. The predeclared advancement condition was not met.

The mechanism did execute: first chop moved to turn 5 on all three official maps, from turns
116, 65 and 77. Earlier productive work alone did not add a win. This rejects this particular
unconditional ceiling with the inherited opening/production controller, not every two-worker
strategy or every possible early allocation rule.

The authenticated post-run room still shows V543, agent 6701731, at **13.92, rank 155/177**,
100/100 rollout and not in progress. No ladder publication or production-source replacement
occurred. Seventh place has not been achieved.

## Delegation and implementation

The owner authorized Claude delegation. `/home/tarstars/bin/claude-proxy` is the installed
command. A read-only review completed in 171.110 seconds for reported USD 0.885079. Its useful
scheduling observation was retained, but two errors were corrected: the capital allocator takes
at most two workers, not all three; and V439/V468 differ by the scarce-kind denial bonus, not
the separate opponent-planted-crop priority. The review did not justify its proposed raid quota.

A second bounded read-only task returned the implementation patch in 417.801 seconds, with
16 turns and reported cost USD 1.624585. Claude could not edit or publish through its allowed
Read/Grep/Glob tools. The primary agent reviewed and applied the patch. Its CLI test mistakenly
assumed pytest temporary paths were outside working storage; that was corrected before testing.
Integer validation was also tightened. Neither task spawned a Codex subagent.

`CAPITAL-RELEASE-DESIGN-2026-09-05.md` was frozen before implementation. The only gameplay change
is an early return from `planned_spec` when own workers reach `WORKER_LIMIT`. The exporter accepts
exact integer limits 2 or 4, retains previous positional-call compatibility, and defaults to 4.
No talent, denial weight, orchard quota, production valuation or movement rule was changed.
The four-worker default preserves the previous behavior; the two-worker export is an ablation,
not the selected controller. This differs from the old late-prefix experiments by releasing
capital funding immediately after the first hire, rather than preserving an opening through 100.
