## Decision question

Does activating the **exact already-tested 1:1 dual-value opponent-crop scoring** only after
the opponent reaches three workers outperform both:

1. the same scoring active for the whole game; and
2. the unchanged resident?

This is the narrow value question earned by H3′. It tests whether the workforce condition is
load-bearing. It does not reopen multiplier, ETA, provenance, target grammar, or Arena tuning.

## Why this is the only defensible arm

H3′ found a temporally ordered decline in opponent-crop contact after successful opponent
worker-three TRAIN, including before permanent loss. It did **not** identify a new action.
Most obvious interventions are already closed:

- exact 1:1 dual-value scoring was broadly active and locally qualified but lost **−7.77
  rating** at the clean 63-game Arena checkpoint;
- flat/retuned multiplier and ETA interpolation are explicitly closed;
- short commitment is closed because 91.5% of selected crops already reach contact;
- harvest-on-contact steals fruit but loses −2.325 margin and raises opponent score;
- lineage liquidation, dedicated denial workers, factory composition, action-contention
  control, and body-blocking are closed or mechanically invalid.

Therefore H3a may change only **when** the exact frozen dual-value scoring is active. Any new
bonus, different multiplier, ETA, target class, worker role, commitment, harvest rewrite, or
scheduler is outside H3a.

## Three frozen arms

All arms use the same exact current resident fallback, referee substrate, maps, opponents,
and non-treatment source.

### C0 — unchanged control

Exact resident; dual-value treatment never active.

### A1 — identical always-on treatment

Exact archived 1:1 dual-value scoring active whenever its original Phase-21 eligibility
allows. This is the negative-reference arm, not a candidate.

### C1 — workforce-conditioned treatment

The exact same scoring is inactive while the visible opponent roster is <3 and becomes
permanently active at the first resident decision whose public state contains at least three
opponent units.

The sticky activation is perspective-local and uses only the current visible state. It does
not inspect issued TRAIN commands, future events, family labels, score-behind state, or
terminal outcome. Because the new worker appears after the TRAIN step, activation begins on
the next legal resident decision without a turn-equality trigger.

No deactivation or threshold variant is permitted.

## Identification

The primary comparisons are:

- **conditioning value:** C1 − A1;
- **usable policy value:** C1 − C0;
- **reproduction check:** A1 − C0, expected to retain the direction of the archived treatment
  under the current exact substrate, though no Arena-rating equality is claimed.

A positive C1 − A1 with nonpositive C1 − C0 means conditioning merely avoids part of an
always-on harm and does not justify continuation.

## Mechanism and support gates

Interpret value only if all pass:

1. A1 activation reproduces the archived eligibility logic exactly on synthetic fixtures.
2. C1 has zero activation before visible opponent roster 3 and 100% activation thereafter.
3. At least **10%** of complete paired map/seat/family cells activate C1.
4. Activations occur in at least **six of eight** families and both seats.
5. In activated C1 cells, the treatment's candidate-set/score changes are byte-identical to
   A1 at the same state.
6. In inactive C1 states, commands and state hashes are byte-identical to C0.
7. Critical/unclassified referee issues are zero; supported issue accounting and all standing
   detectors cover every trajectory.
8. Resident/source/substrate hashes, task matrix, fresh instances, and one/parallel output
   identity pass.

Failure returns `BLOCKED` or `INSUFFICIENT_ACTIVATION`, with no value interpretation.

## Frozen value gates

Use paired terminal margin by map/seat/family and cluster uncertainty by map root.

Return `CONDITIONING_LOAD_BEARING` only if **all** hold:

- C1 − C0 mean margin ≥ **+2.0**;
- clustered 95% lower bound for C1 − C0 > **0**;
- C1 − A1 mean margin ≥ **+5.0**;
- clustered 95% lower bound for C1 − A1 > **0**;
- at least 6/8 families are nonnegative for C1 − C0;
- worst-family C1 − C0 ≥ **−1.0**;
- both seats are nonnegative;
- catastrophes no worse than C0 and negative-margin mass ≤ **1.05×** C0;
- own score is not reduced by more than **1.0** on average;
- value is not concentrated only in cells that were already permanently behind at
  activation.

The pre-loss sensitivity freezes activation cells whose next 20 turns precede permanent
negative crossover, matching H3′'s temporal boundary. Its C1 − C0 direction must be
nonnegative, but it is not a separate selection population.
