## 3. Four distinctions that change the plan

**The full cycle has already been expressed in code.** V603–V605 are not just 'protect a sapling' rules: they track a typed seed, mature production, harvesting and felling. The reported V605 trace gains 11.58 plant and 19.71 harvest commands while losing 36.34 early CHOP commands; opponent score grows by 84.33 and own wood by only 0.21. This is a coupled opportunity-cost failure, not absence of lifecycle concepts. Source: N README's V603–V605 entry and that family's results.

**A bad old acceptance gate can reject a candidate for the wrong reason.** V597–V599's strongest smoke improves outcomes from 19/1/4 to 20/3/1 but misses the old +40 own-score condition. V609 is screened at +39.83 while V610 reaches +40, yet V610 subsequently loses 42.01 own score on the wider panel. These do not turn those candidates into qualified winners; they do prevent counting every historical rejection as evidence of competitive inferiority. Source: N:CRITICAL-REVIEW and corresponding V597/V609 result files.

**Successful transactions need not pay.** V616–V618 repair a real routed seed commitment: the ledger reports 107 of 107 routed picks leading to planting, without an intervening DROP. That removes one defect but does not establish enough profitable output. The September 7 forestry/fruit/timed dispatch variants likewise pass increasing numbers of component tests while remaining below their parent. Source: N V616–V618 results; W primary reviews cited in PHASE0.

**Learning and lookahead are separate unnumbered lines.** The neighbour's H10a spatial selector fails its frozen selection gate; its V439 clone has high per-decision agreement but only 0–1 wins in 20 games. Main's current learned best is 37 strict wins of 144 versus its old champion and the clone-anchored self-play recipe is closed in its tested form. The neighbour's persistent planner eventually deploys successfully but its fresh screen ties V439 at two wins and four losses. These are different failures; none justifies either a new parameter sweep or a claim that all neural or search policies are impossible. Sources: N README/H10A_HANDOFF_2026-09-01.md; M:local_claude_1/nn-bot/GATE-S22F-ANCHORFADE-VERDICT-2026-09-03.md; W:planning/2026-09-06/policy-flat-field/SCREEN-RESULT.md.

This does **not** say those files are absent. Some are likely intermediate builds, superseded labels, or non-numbered machinery; this pass has not established their exact source/result links. Their existence, byte aliases, compiler validity, completed gate and mechanism classification must be read from the pinned source inventory and result manifests before producing a genuine build count. No endpoint maximum, highest V-number or directory count can replace that audit.

The script's optional checkout mode creates checkout-candidate-paths.json from every tracked N source path, compares copied Markdown by whole-file hash and extracts recognized mature reading assertions from W. It is supplied for coordinator execution; it was **not run against complete checkouts here**. Filename matches remain a starting index, not a completed semantic audit. Even after that mode runs, ambiguous aliases and result associations still require review.

## 5. Verdict and consequence

**E6 is delivered as a bounded census with an explicit incomplete tail, not as an exhaustive certified build ledger.** The main strategic conclusion does not require guessing those 189 labels: enough directly identified standalone and lifecycle implementations exist to refute novelty-by-name. Conversely, unclassified labels cannot justify a universal death verdict over a strategy family.

Do not commission another generic allocator while calling this inventory complete. Retain V439; audit the concrete complete-funding cancellation and its promised supplies; repair the evaluation execution contract before selecting another finalist. The next design must name its exact new mechanism, comparison parent, valid control and fresh acceptance evidence. PLAN-v2 makes that the gate rather than silently substituting another orchard rewrite.
