## 6. What a later repair may change

One controller must own the accounting that its own decision depends on. Its plan state is committed only for accepted/effective commands in the declared domain, not merely because an action was proposed before movement resolution. Reserve concrete currency/source claims with explicit expiry and cancellation; do not replace them with an arbitrary permanent tree-protection flag. Carrying capacity clips collectible wood. Future fruit is a timed delivery, not 'three fruits per tree' independent of travel, growth and opponent access.

The control remains the exact comparison parent, with no accidental shared-module mutation. Both arms must independently pass the preregistered mechanical/terminal contract. A less-broken arm beating a more-broken control is not value evidence. Known noncritical conflicts are preserved and classified; they cannot disappear through a clean-game filter.

The comparison allows NO_TRAIN and NO_PLANT. Training time, total trees, harvests and raw own score explain a result; they do not define success. A good plan may decline an investment. Native trajectory checkpoints may validate a reconstruction against a matched domain, but cannot replace competitive evaluation or justify arbitrary exact thresholds across different opponent schedules.

## 7. One executable evaluation pipeline

### Development

Use open development maps for debugging, thresholds and mechanism traces. The historical 24-map smoke, 200-map field and 72-map neural sets remain development data. An illustrative regression budget is 32 maps × 2 seats × 6 adaptive proxies × 2 arms = 768 games. Those are 32 map blocks, not 768 independent maps. A named proxy is not the real ranked program. Never continue recorded opponent commands after candidate divergence and call that adaptive play. [M instrument audit; N:EVALUATION.md; TOOLING]

### Instrument and pilot

Freeze two sources, compiler, helper pins, seed/initial-state identity rules, opponents, arm order, seat schedule and failure interpretation before play. Twenty A/A games cover five agents, two seats and two repeats. A deterministic A/A failure may identify a stochastic opponent rather than a bot bug; stop that exact-pair contract rather than drop or silently replace the opponent. A noisy-opponent design needs a separately frozen repetition scheme and budget.

The pilot contains twelve new maps, both seats, two arms: 48 games. Assign opponents before results exist; keep both seats of a map together in uncertainty calculations. Proposed screening rule: mechanical/deployment identity gates pass, the named intervention occurs, and net paired match points are positive. This is a screening condition, not a superiority claim. Freeze it before running. Nonpositive pilot closes this implementation's admission, not the economic idea for all time.

### Confirmation

The current sealed instrument provides 512 map blocks, each with one of five locked opponents at player zero. Two arms cost 1,024 games. Do not silently turn it into a five-opponent crossed design or a two-seat set. A future manifest can choose a different design before the seal opens, with its own count and variance calculation.

Freeze the primary outcome as paired match points, where win=1, draw=0.5, loss=0; preserve official platform rank outcomes including disqualifications. Report margin and economy trajectories separately. Use a specified paired block-level confidence method, fixed before results, and require its lower 95% bound above zero for a superiority claim, with independent deployment validity. This is a proposed gate needing executable implementation and review; neither old aggregator currently implements it.

The confirmation bank is read once. Opening, partial execution or failure retires it. A failed run is not permission to inspect the remaining bank and tune. If no valid positive result is obtained, the verdict is NO_PROMOTION or INCONCLUSIVE as appropriate, not equality and not a fresh hidden test on the same seeds. [M:codex_1/sealed-holdout/README.md; TOOLING]

### Execution and recovery

Use one global immutable job ledger with exact source hashes and budget. Each job is uniquely keyed by experiment, block, arm, opponent, seat and repeat. Durably record intent before POST; store response/replay bytes and hashes; only then validate and classify. Partition into at most twelve jobs per chunk. Confirmation needs 86 chunks (85 full and one four-job chunk). Stage-separate A/A + pilot + confirmation needs 92 chunks and at most 1,092 games.

A network timeout after POST leaves an ambiguous attempted job. Reconcile its existing server identity or stop; never blindly repeat it. An existing collection artifact is not a new game. Global attempt counts include failures and ambiguity, not only successful rows. Mismatched hashes, duplicate games, missing paired arms and changed opponents fail closed. Test these transitions with fake responses and simulated crashes before any official game. [TOOLING]

## 8. What this budget can resolve

The seal's original local score calculation gives about ±4.925 score points at 512 blocks. It is not an official-game power estimate or a match-point interval. For paired match-point differences in [-1,1], an intentionally conservative SD=1 normal planning calculation gives about ±8.66 percentage points at 512 blocks; a five-point effect at 80% power would need roughly 3,140 blocks under that worst-case approximation. Actual pairing may lower variance, but it must be measured. Do not buy 512 blocks while promising five-point detection. [extension_audit.py; TOOLING]

The recent unchanged main champion's SD 0.81549 gives nominal rating half-width ±1.130 for four rollouts per arm, ±0.923 for six and ±0.493 for twenty-one. Four per arm is an optional bounded budget, not a one-point guarantee. The changing ladder and correlated schedules weaken the stationary independent model. No score-margin-to-rating conversion is permitted, and a later poll of the same agent does not count as another rollout. [SCOREBOARD-v2.md; extension_audit.py]

These are proposed information budgets, not estimates of API availability, wall-clock duration or guaranteed strength gain. Any larger sample requires a fresh authorization and prospective decision rule.
