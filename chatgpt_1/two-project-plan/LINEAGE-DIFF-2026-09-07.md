## 3. Which additions are measured, and on what instrument?

**Apple permissions:** the local V248, V251, V256 and V258 comparisons report positive margin on larger panels. The later review explicitly shows why margin can grow while match points do not: V257 changes no outcome despite +1,383 total margin; V258's further +2,464 margin accompanies a net -0.5 match point. These are useful efficiency facts within the tested proxy population, not calibrated rank gains.

**Traffic repairs:** V355 and later wrappers have exact selected-failure witnesses and narrow adaptive continuations. Their unselected confirmations are often wholly dormant. Keep them in the exact incumbent until an ablation proves they cost competitive value; do not broaden their timing/cargo conditions from the selected rescue alone.

**Renewable entrance farming:** not established by V231/V439. The ledger explicitly reports young trees being felled before their first fruit and repeated failures of protection variants. The name of the wrapper is not evidence that its intended lifecycle happens.

**Denial:** the fresh V368/V468 block is a valuable mechanistic example, not a population result. V439 reproduces V368's commands on those recorded streams, but that is a replay check, not extra live V439 games. The later exact V439/V468 confirmation is the more relevant direct ablation and is inconclusive at six blocks. Do not automatically transplant or remove the bonus across a different source base.

**Whole V439 versus M:** the earlier same-panel comparisons put V439 at about 62.2% strict wins versus M's champion and 94.2% versus orchard 6; V468 is 238/81/81 and 358/11/31 on 400 games each. Those are complete-policy comparisons against local relatives. They support V439 as a serious retained reference, but the main project's own instrument audit says that beating one clear-cutter does not calibrate a ladder ranking. Source: N:README.md, September 2 gate-step entry; M:coordination/tasks/20260904-instrument-audit.md.

## 5. What to carry into a unified champion

**Carry the exact incumbent first, not a hand-selected bundle of apparently successful hunks.** Archive its readable/exported hashes, fixed compiler and current submission identity; make V439 the comparison parent by an explicit owner/coordinator decision. This is an administrative baseline choice, not a new submission and not proof it is best among all archived policies.

Keep the orchard, bounded entrance behavior and proven narrowly scoped traffic repairs together initially. Keep old main denial-on and denial-off programs as historical/adversarial controls. Do not port their telemetry by a broad source rewrite: a behavior-neutral instrument must independently reproduce command streams.

A later source-attribution study is a separate task. Cheapest useful design: one V439-minus-one-feature ablation whose suspected cost is already traced, not a factorial over every wrapper. Budget it through the same mechanical gate and actual-opponent pipeline as any other candidate. Under PLAN-v2's illustrative schedule, 20 instrument games plus a 48-game paired pilot precede one 1,024-game confirmation; none is authorized or run by this report. Ranking V439 against M is not necessary to leave the existing live artifact untouched.
