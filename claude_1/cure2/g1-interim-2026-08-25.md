### Two construction facts that are NOT in G-0 and must be reviewed as new

1. **The slot map is verified, not merely counted.** G-0 clause 2 guards the positional
   command map on arity alone. The build additionally cross-checks it against `command_by_id` —
   the ids parsed out of the MOVE commands themselves — and discards the map on any mismatch, so
   no swap fires that turn (`sf=`). This is strictly fail-closed: it can only refuse an exchange
   G-0 would have allowed, never fire one G-0 forbids. Measured `sf = 0` on 48,000 panel turns and
   6,800 fixture turns, so the guard never actually bit.
2. **Refusal-counter order.** When more than one clause fails, exactly one counter is charged, in
   G-0's clause order: `sn` (non-adjacent) before `so` (partner on the goal) before `sf`. A failing
   `d(L) < d(c)` charges nothing — it is not a refused cure, it is the case where "beyond" has no
   meaning because `next_cell` fell back off the BFS map.

## 4. The two findings

### 4.1 C-5 fired: the pair re-exchanges every second turn (the pre-committed stop)

Panel: **12 within-6-turn repeats on 4 games** — `m078:0`, `m090:0`, `m090:1`, `m118:1`.
Fixtures: **5 on 2 games** — OSC-006 (turns 3→5→7→9→11) and OSC-007 (8→11).
Consecutive-turn repeats: **0**. Ambiguous turns (two exchanges in one turn, which the wire cannot
pair): **0** — every exchange on both corpora was the only one in its turn.

The cleanest instance, OSC-006, read straight off the wire (`u0` and `u2`, both after the same two
adjacent trees):

```
t=3   u0=TREE(2,3)/…/r=S   u2=TREE(2,3)/…/r=X   sw=1   MOVE 0 2 3 ; MOVE 2 1 3
t=4   u0=TREE(2,3)/…/r=N   u2=TREE(2,2)/…/r=W   sw=0   CHOP 0 ; WAIT
t=5   u0=TREE(2,3)/…/r=X   u2=TREE(2,2)/…/r=S   sw=1   MOVE 0 1 3 ; MOVE 2 2 3
t=6   u0=TREE(2,2)/…/r=W   u2=TREE(2,3)/…/r=N   sw=0   WAIT ; CHOP 2
```

and so on to t=11. **Each exchange is legal under every clause of the predicate**, and each is
separated from its reversal by exactly the one turn Theorem 1 guarantees.

**Theorem 1 is not falsified** (C-6 = 0 over 48,000 turns). **Theorem 2 is not falsified either**,
and this is the part that matters for the ruling: measured over the right window — the first
exchange to the reversal, not the two turns either side of the reversal — **both** units' chosen
targets had moved in every one of the five fixture reversals. The reversal really does require a
planner event, exactly as proved.

**But the planner event is caused by the exchange itself.** Displacing the standing worker one
square changes which tree is nearest to it, so `select` re-picks a goal that now lies strictly
beyond its old work square — and the predicate then fires in the other direction two turns later.
G-0 §4.4 anticipated a planner oscillation with a *stationary* `M` in between; what the wire shows
is stronger and different: both units alternate, each getting one CHOP per cycle, and neither is
parked. This is the finding, and per the card and G-0 §10 it is **reported, never patched with a
lock or a timer**.

Evidence: `results/swap-loop-control.json` (fixtures), `results/c5-evidence-fixtures.json`
(cells at `t'-1` and `t'`, both targets, and the Addendum A split — written from actual cells, as
Addendum B requires), `results/panel-swap-census.json` (all 240 games).
