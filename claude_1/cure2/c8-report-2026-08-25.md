## Why this is *not* run through `fixture_harness`'s `FIXED` verdict, measured rather than asserted

The obvious route is to point `fixture_harness.py` at the candidate arm and read a `FIXED` off one
of the 34 recorded situations. It is closed, and closed for a real reason:

```
fixture_harness.py --candidate claude_1/cure2/arm-candidate.rs
    --only OSC-001,002,005,006,007,009,010,021,027,028,029,031   (the 12 that grant an exchange)
 -> 12 / 12  NOT_REPRODUCIBLE_ON_BASE, 0 graded either way
```

The harness's episode-identity gate (card `20260821-episode-identity-regrade`) asks whether the
run reproduces **the recorded episode**: the frozen window's command lines and the board at its
first turn. The library's subject bot is the **resident**; the candidate is a different lineage,
and 189 of 189 frozen lines differ on OSC-002 alone. **That verdict is correct and must not be
worked around** — a window is a property of the bot that produced it (my `20260821T094945Z`,
accepted). Any `FIXED` obtained by pointing the candidate at the resident's window would be a
comparison between two different games.

So C-8 uses a window the candidate's **own lineage** produces: the same bot with the rule switched
off. Both clauses are then the harness's own — `progress_restored` is
`fixture_harness.had_progress` **imported, not re-implemented**, and the dance is
`trace_detectors.detect_d1`, the panel's own detector. Neither word is allowed to mean one thing
to the gate and another here.

## The shape, pre-committed in the driver's docstring before any number existed

Two arms differing by exactly one line (`arm-manifest.json`, C-3): `arm-instrument.rs` (rule
**on**) and `arm-ruleoff.rs` (rule **off**); same map, seed, opponent and horizon. Reading the
exchange off the instrument's wire is a reading about the candidate because C-2 has already
established the two are equivalent in play on 240/240 games.

1. **The dance** is a D-1 episode on the **rule-off** arm: `(unit u, [lo,hi], cells, k)`.
2. **The exchange must fire**: a granted exchange (`sw ≥ 1`) at a turn in `[lo,hi]` whose `S`/`X`
   units include `u`.
3. **The dance ends**, by both clauses and never one alone: no D-1 episode for `u` on the rule-on
   arm overlapping the window, **and** `had_progress` over the window.

## The gate that makes the counterfactual legitimate

The two arms diverge, so the rule-off arm's turn numbers describe a run that is not the rule-on
arm's. That is the hazard I raised on 2026-08-21 and it is **gated, not waved away**:

| gate | requirement | result |
|---|---|---|
| **G-D** divergence identity | the first turn on which the arms' commands differ (`MSG` stripped) must be **exactly** the rule-on arm's first exchange turn, and the dance must open at or before it | every one of the 13 counted cases; the 2 that fail it are published with their reason and excluded from the claim |
| **G-B** panel identity | the rule-on arm re-run here reproduces each panel game's exchange count from `panel-swap-census.json` | **PASS — 240/240**, and the corpus total re-derives as **46 exchanges in 28 games**, the published figure |
| **G-R** duplicate agreement | all 34 fixtures are re-runs of panel games, so a `--panel` run plays each twice; the two runs of a case must return the **same verdict** | **PASS — 16 duplicated cases, 0 disagreements** (a free re-run identity check) |
| **G-E** wire honesty | every counted exchange turn carries at least one `S`/`X` unit on the wire | PASS |

G-D is what earns the word *counterfactual*: on a counted case, turns `1 … d-1` are literally
shared history — same board, same commands, the same unit entering the same alternation — and the
exchange is the only thing that could have changed what followed.

**Because of G-R the headline counts are deduplicated.** The raw run produces 43 rows over 274
game-arms; reporting that would inflate the population by 14 %. The numbers above are 27 distinct
cases over **240 distinct games**.

## Controls — so PASS is a measurement, not a tautology

| control | what it does | result |
|---|---|---|
| **N-1 inertness** | the whole pipeline re-run with the rule-on arm **replaced by the rule-off arm** (`--inert`), over the same 274 game-arms | **0 fires, 0 passes** on all 27 cases — with no rule, nothing fires and nothing passes. A pipeline that passed here would be measuring something other than the exchange. |
| **N-2 clause liveness** | `progress_restored` computed on the **rule-off** arm over the same windows | **0 of 27** — the clause says *no* on every window it is offered without the rule, so it is not a clause that cannot say no |
| **N-3 corroboration** | is the ended dance a **frozen library episode**, exactly? | **3 of the 9 passes** match unit, bounds, cells and `k` |
| **re-run** | the whole `--panel` run executed twice | byte-identical JSON |

## What this does and does not establish

**Does:**

- The exchange ends a real dance and restores progress in **9 distinct cases**, three of them
  recorded episodes; four of the nine would otherwise have run to the last turn of the game.
- The instrument can *see* a good swap. With C-7 (the counters fire 17→350 and 0→344 on a gutted
  predicate) both directions are now measured: a bad swap is counted, a good one is recognised.
- Across the corpus the rule takes the dance count from **27 in 25 games to 13 in 12 games**. That
  aggregate is descriptive; only the 13 G-D cases carry a per-case causal claim.

**Does not:**

- It does **not** show the rule ends every dance. 12 of the 25 dancing games granted no exchange
  at all, and every one of those dances persists.
- It does **not** show the exchange always helps: **4 of 13** firing cases are quiet-but-stalled.
- It says **nothing** about whether the candidate's **C-5 = 5** within-six-turn repeats are benign.
  The pre-committed STOP AND ASK stands and is the owner's ruling.
- **P3 / orchard-safety on the candidate arm remains UNMEASURED, not passed.**
- The two G-D-excluded windows (`m070:1` unit 0, `m084:1` unit 0) open after their arms had
  already diverged. One of them looks cured and one does not; **neither is claimed either way**.
- `m058:1`'s window (29–42) is close to but not identical with frozen OSC-029 (28–44), so it is
  reported as **not** corroborated rather than as approximately corroborated.
