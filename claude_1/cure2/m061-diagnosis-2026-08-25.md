## 0. The one-paragraph answer

On both seats of `m061` the map ends up with **one tree left standing**, and the champion's score
comes from **not** cutting it: the surviving apple keeps making fruit, and the pair runs a
shack-side plant/chop/bank cycle for the whole second half of the game. Candidate 1's forced
`WAIT` is what preserves that tree — the troll that wants it is permanently blocked by its
teammate. Candidate 2's exchange **removes that block**, the freed troll walks over and fells the
last tree, and the map is left with **zero plants**. And with zero plants a pre-existing planner
fallback in `main_candidates` — `idle_regeneration && chops.is_empty()` — **returns a fresh
`vec![WAIT]` and discards the candidate list it had already built**, including the two regeneration
`PICK`s worth 7500 that the clause immediately above had just generated. Both trolls then sit
goal-less to the end of the game: **131 turns on seat 0, 96 on seat 1, inventory frozen**. That is
the 75 points. The exchange is the trigger; the fallback is what converts "the map is empty" into
"the team stops"; and P4 stays silent because its own calibration excuses a stall that begins after
the world is exhausted — the arm exhausted the world itself.

At t=75 and t=76 the situation is identical in both arms: `u0` stands on the bank cell (11,2)

clause of §2.3 is satisfied, and it exchanges. The exchange is **legal, correct on its own terms,
and does exactly what it was designed to do**: it delivers the blocked troll to its goal. Its goal
was the last tree.

## 3. `m061:0` — the exchange is the initiating cause, not the proximate one

Seat 0 must be stated more carefully, and I will not overstate it. The two exchanges are at t=3
and t=28, and neither is the turn the game is lost:

- **t=3** — `u2`, carrying 3 wood to the bank, exchanges with `u0`, which is **standing on
  `TREE(2,2)` chopping it**. `u0` is displaced to (3,2) and walks back to (2,2) at t=5; two chop
  turns are lost and the tree dies at t=10 instead of t=8. (This is the rule displacing a
  *working chopper*, which G-0 clause 4's "standing partner" admits by construction.)
- **t=28** — `u2`, wanting `TREE(7,2)`, exchanges with `u0` standing on `TREE(6,2)` at health 1.
  Again the chopper is pushed off its tree for one turn; (6,2) dies at t=31 instead of t=27.
- Those four lost turns leave the pair a few turns out of phase for the rest of the shuttle. At
  **t=37–39** the difference shows: in the rule-off arm `u0` sits at (1,2) with goal `TREE(7,2)`
  and branch **`W`** — blocked by `u2` standing on (2,2) — while in the instrument arm `u2` is on
  (2,1) instead and the corridor east is clear, so `u0` walks out at t=37 and fells (7,2) by t=63.

So on seat 0 the causal statement the record should carry is: *the arms are byte-identical until
t=3; every later difference descends from the two exchanges; the loss is realised at t=37–63 when
the instrument arm's corridor happens to be clear and the rule-off arm's is not.* I am not
claiming the t=3 exchange "caused" the felling in any stronger sense than that, and the record
should not read as if I had.

## 4. Why the team then stops — measured at the clause, not argued

The obvious question is why the instrument arm does not simply switch to the same shack-side
regeneration cycle the rule-off arm runs from t≈101. Both arms have a fruit in the inventory and
an empty-handed troll standing on a shack-adjacent cell. I measured it with a print-only probe
(`arm-instrument-regenprobe.rs` / `arm-ruleoff-regenprobe.rs`, four `eprintln!` lines each, stdout
parity-gated byte-identical to their own arms on both seats).

`m061:0`, turn 100, unit 0 at (1,2) — **every input of the regeneration clause is satisfied on both
arms**:

```
instrument  REGEN t=100 u=0 cell=1,2 carried=0 plants=0 own=2 adj=1 here_empty=1 fruits=2 safe=1 turn_ok=1
            CANDS t=100 u=0 n=1 list=[("WAIT", 0.0)]
rule-off    REGEN t=100 u=0 cell=1,2 carried=0 plants=1 own=2 adj=1 here_empty=1 fruits=2 safe=1 turn_ok=1
            CANDS t=100 u=0 n=4 list=[("WAIT",0.0), ("PICK 0 LEMON",7500.0), ("PICK 0 APPLE",7499.0), ("MOVE 0 7 2",60.6)]
```

The clause fires in both arms and pushes the two `PICK`s. Then, further down `main_candidates`:

```rust
let chops = Self::yamo_chop_candidates(view, unit, type_to_cut, opponent_eta_penalty);
if idle_regeneration && chops.is_empty() {
    let mut fallback = vec![MoisanBot::wait()];
    fallback.extend(Self::idle_harvest_candidates(view, unit));
    if unit.total_carried() > 0 { fallback.extend(Self::bank_candidates(view, unit)); }
    return fallback;            // <-- returns `fallback`, DISCARDING `out`
}
```

With **zero plants on the map** `chops` is empty and `idle_harvest_candidates` is empty too, so the
function returns `[WAIT]` and throws away the 7500-point regeneration `PICK`s it had just built.
In the rule-off arm the apple is still standing, `chops` is non-empty (`MOVE 0 7 2`, score 60.6 in
the list above), the fallback is skipped, `out` survives, and `PICK 0 LEMON` wins.

**This fallback is not Candidate 2's and not Candidate 1's — it is in the champion.** It is the
same defect I reported unanswered on 2026-08-21 in the anti-benching Phase 3a handoff ("the
`idle_regeneration` fallback replaces rather than extends `out`"). What is new is its price:
here it is worth **75 points across two seats**, and it is what turns "the last tree is gone" into
"both trolls stop for the rest of the game" rather than "both trolls replant".

## 5. Why no detector fired

The instrument row's violations on `m061:1` are **identical to the rule-off row's** — two D-4
`no_progress` episodes at t=24–26 and t=64–66, nothing else — despite 96 turns of both trolls
idle with a frozen inventory. That is not a detector bug; it is `eval_p4`'s own calibration
(2026-08-06 repair #2): *"a stall that begins after the world is exhausted for the rest of the
game … is explained by the game being over, not by the bot being stuck."* Here the arm exhausted
the world **itself**, and the calibration then excuses the consequence. Stated plainly for the
record: **an arm that destroys the last resource buys itself immunity from the liveness gate.**
I am reporting that, not proposing a change to P4; the per-troll stall gate task
(`20260825-p4-per-troll-stall-gate`) is where it belongs, and I will carry it into that ruling as a
definitions input — noting that a per-troll gate keyed to "work was available" would **also** stay
silent here, because after t=100 there genuinely is no work. The harm is done earlier, at the turn
the last tree falls.

## 6. What this does and does not say about Candidate 2

- It does **not** say the predicate is wrong: every clause held, and the exchange did what it was
  built to do.
- It does say the rule has **no notion of whether the mover reaching its goal is good for the
  team**. `d(L) < d(c)` proves the exchange helps the *mover*; nothing asks what the mover will do
  when it arrives. On `m061` what it does is fell the last tree.
- It does say the rule **displaces working choppers** (seat 0, t=3 and t=28): a troll standing on
  its tree mid-chop satisfies "standing partner" exactly.
- The panel figure this bears on is C-15's net: **−24 over 240 games is one map**. `m061` is
  −75; the other 238 games are **+51**. Whether that generalises is a G-3 question and the answer
  is not in this file.

Nothing here authorises an Arena action, and no change to any arm, predicate, gate or detector is
proposed.
