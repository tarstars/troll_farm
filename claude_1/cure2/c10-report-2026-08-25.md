## Why this control comes first

G-0 §4.0 names **A-1** — "two own units ordered onto each other's cell exchange cells in that
tick" — as an *assumption* about the referee, not a proved property, and G-0 §10 pre-commits the
consequence: **C-10 below 100 % withdraws the design**, because the whole exchange premise, both
theorems and every C-5 row would then be describing a world the referee does not produce. Nothing
else in the Candidate 2 control set is worth reading until this number is 100 %.

## What was measured

For each exchange at turn `t` with mover `M` and displaced partner `B`:

    c_{t+1}(M) == c_t(B)   and   c_{t+1}(B) == c_t(M)

The **cells** come from the referee's own transcript (`trace_detectors.build_trace`), never from
the arm's payload. The **pair** comes from the `S`/`X` branch codes on the v5 wire. So the wire
says who was ordered to exchange and the referee says where they actually stood on the next turn;
the two sources are independent by construction.

## Gates, each of which fails the run rather than degrading it

- **G-B row identity — PASS.** Every one of the 28 panel games reproduced the `swaps` count
  recorded in `results/panel-swap-census.json`, and every one of the 12 fixtures reproduced the
  exact exchange turns recorded in `results/swap-loop-control.json` (`OSC-006` at 3/5/7/9/11 and
  the rest). Without this the population checked here would not be the population the G-1 report
  describes; the run is a fresh re-execution, not a re-read of a stored file.
- **G-D unambiguous pairing — PASS.** Every exchange turn carried exactly one `S` and exactly one
  `X`. A turn with any other shape raises rather than pairing by guesswork.
- **G-E observability — 0 cases.** An exchange on the final turn, or one where either unit is
  absent from the `t+1` state, has no observable post-state; such a row is counted
  `NOT_OBSERVABLE`, excluded from the rate and listed by id. There were none, so the 100 % is over
  the whole population and not over a survivor subset.

## Two observations recorded, neither a gate

- **`manhattan(c_t(M), c_t(B)) == 1` on all 66 exchanges** — clause 5's adjacency premise for
  Lemma 1 is realised, not merely commanded, in every case.
- **No third own unit stood on either exchanged cell on turn `t+1`, in any of the 66** — the
  "both cells are `granted` so no third own unit is sent to either" step of Lemma 1's proof is
  confirmed against the referee, not assumed.

## What this does and does not license

It licenses Lemma 1, and with it the *post-state* half of both theorems, on the referee we
actually run. It does **not** touch A-2 (`prev_cells`, control C-11, not yet run), it does not
bear on the C-5 stop — the loop is real and C-10 passing makes it *more* real, since the reversals
are genuine cell exchanges and not a telemetry artefact — and it says nothing about the
undiagnosed `m061` −75. Those remain open in the deferred set.
