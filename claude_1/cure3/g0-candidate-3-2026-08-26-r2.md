## Item 1 — the six-game joint-margin proof, as a conservative bound, and one fixed `M`

### 1.1 The closed form

At the first post-exchange turn the two own units are on the two-unit joint path (`:948-968`) and
both assignments cover the **same two trees with the travels swapped**. Write `K_i = chop_i +
return_i + 1` for tree `i` — the part of `chop_candidates`' denominator (`:875`) that does not
depend on which unit goes, under equal unit stats — and `w_i` for its wood. `T1` is the tree the
mover now stands on (travel 0), `T2` is its kept goal, `Delta` steps beyond `T1`. The partner sits
one step from `T1` on the mover's old cell.

```
keeping  S_A = w2/(Delta + K2) + w1/(1 + K1)
trading  S_B = w1/(0     + K1) + w2/(d + K2),   d = dist(partner cell, T2)
```

`d` is `Delta-1`, `Delta` or `Delta+1`; **`d = Delta-1` is taken everywhere below** because it is
the value that most favours trading, i.e. the conservative one. The keep survives iff
`S_B <= (1 + M) * S_A`, so the **required margin is `rho = S_B/S_A - 1`**, and because the
two-unit path updates `best_pair` only on a strictly greater sum, a tie is decided by enumeration
order rather than by the rule — so the honest requirement is **`M > rho`, strictly**.

### 1.2 Required `M`, tabulated (equal wood, `K1 = K2 = K`)

| `K` | `Delta = 1` | `Delta = 3` |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 0.3333 | 0.2800 |
| 4 | 0.2500 | 0.2153 |
| 5 | 0.2000 | 0.1755 |
| 6 | 0.1667 | 0.1484 |
| 7 | 0.1429 | 0.1287 |
| 8 | 0.1250 | 0.1138 |
| 10 | 0.1000 | 0.0924 |
| 12 | 0.0833 | 0.0778 |

**This corrects my own G-0.** I framed `Delta = 3` (m090:0, m090:1) as the dangerous case and
`Delta = 1` (m078:0, OSC-006) as the safe one. The algebra says the opposite: **`Delta = 1` is the
harder case**, because the partner's alternative is one step away in the keeping assignment while
the mover's is one step away in the trading assignment, and the trading side wins the exchange of
those two ones on the smaller denominator. The correction is recorded rather than quietly folded in.

### 1.3 The bound is robust to the two assumptions it looked like it needed

Swept numerically over `K1, K2` in `[4, 14]`, `w1/w2` in `[1/4, 8]`, `Delta` in `{1, 3}`:

- **Wood asymmetry barely moves it.** At `K = 8`, `Delta = 3`, doubling `w1/w2` from 1 to 2 moves
  the requirement from 0.1138 to 0.1177, and an eightfold ratio reaches only 0.1227. The wood
  terms appear on both sides, so they largely cancel. At `Delta = 1` with `K1 = K2` the
  requirement is exactly `1/K1` for every wood ratio swept. **The equal-wood assumption is
  therefore not load-bearing**, which is the one place I expected to have to hedge.
- **Trip asymmetry is monotone and bounded.** The requirement rises as `K1` (the tree under the
  mover's feet) falls, and the worst point of the whole swept region is
  **`rho = 0.2500` at `Delta = 1`, `K1 = K2 = 4`, `w1/w2 = 1/4`**.

### 1.4 The fixed accepted value: **`M = 0.25`**, chosen now and not from G-1

`M = 0.25` is the pre-registered cap and is the smallest value in the admissible band that
**strictly** discharges the six-game obligation for every `K >= 5` across the entire swept region.
Coverage, stated exactly:

| `M` | strict keep at `Delta = 1` | strict keep at `Delta = 3` |
|---|---|---|
| 0.15 | `K >= 7` | `K >= 6` |
| 0.20 | `K >= 6` | `K >= 5` |
| **0.25** | **`K >= 5`** | **`K >= 4`** |

**The single residual, named rather than buried:** `K <= 4` at `Delta = 1` is **not** covered by any
`M <= 0.25` — at `K = 4` the requirement is exactly 0.25 and the tie falls to enumeration order.
`K = chop + return + 1`, so `K <= 4` means a tree felled in one or two turns essentially next to the
shack. **Whether any of the six games has such a tree is unmeasured**, because no run can start from
this worktree (§4 of the r1 packet; disk). It is pre-registered as a **check**, not as a tuning
input: G-1 reports `K1, K2, w1, w2, Delta` and the realised `rho` at every exchange turn of the six
games, and if any lands at `K <= 4` the rule is **re-ruled, not re-tuned** — `M` does not move
afterwards whatever the six games say.

**The price of 0.25 over 0.15, stated in advance.** A larger margin is a stickier goal, which is
Candidate 1's failure mode moved into the planner. It is bounded by the structural guarantee (the
bonus only re-weights candidates the unmodified planner produced this turn), by the five release
predicates, and it is *measured* by the release census, the D-1/D-3 rows and the changed-game set.
If the panel shows stickiness costing own-score points, that is a result about `M = 0.25` reported
as such — not a licence to lower it and re-run.

## Item 2 — goal recording is post-resolver, and ambiguity erases

Replaces R3(c) of the r1 packet.

> **R3(c), corrected.** Recording happens **after** `MoisanBot::resolve_move_conflicts` (`:2171`),
> against the **final emitted command line**, never against `select`'s output. For each own unit,
> the emitted command carrying that unit's id in field 1 is matched by **exact string equality**
> against that unit's **pre-bonus** candidate list:
>
> - **exactly one match** — its `target` becomes the kept goal, unless the target is
>   `Target::None`, in which case the entry is erased;
> - **two or more matches with the same `target`** — that target becomes the kept goal;
> - **two or more matches with differing targets** — **erase**. Position is never used as a
>   tie-break;
> - **zero matches** — **erase**. This is the case that matters: `resolve_move_conflicts` can
>   replace a selected `MOVE` with something no candidate proposed, and the r1 wording ("after
>   `select`") would have persisted a goal for a command the resolver had already thrown away;
> - **no emitted command carrying that unit's id** (the unit is dead, or the line is a bare
>   `WAIT`) — **erase**.
>
> Ids are parsed exactly as `remember_selected_regeneration` (`:1698-1713`) parses them, so the two
> recording steps cannot disagree about which command belongs to which unit.

Erasure on ambiguity is the safe direction throughout: a missing kept goal costs one turn of
preference, a wrong kept goal is a preference for something the troll is not doing.

## Item 3 — v6 telemetry, mandatory and mathematically defined

### 3.1 `k` is mandatory

```python
VERSION  = "v6"
UNIT_RE  = re.compile(r"^u(\d+)=([^/]+)/([^/]+)/r=([^/]*)/b=([^/]*)/k=([01])$")
META_RE  = re.compile(r"^(pz|sp|wc|sw|so|sn|sf|m|kp|kr|ka|kb|kc|kd|ke|ki|xo|xn|x|xj)=(\d+)$")
```

No `?`, no optional group: a v6 unit token **without** `/k=` is a decode error, exactly as a v5
token without `/b=` is. `k=1` iff the unit's recorded goal for the next turn (per item 2) equals the
kept goal it entered the turn with; `k=0` otherwise, including every unit with no kept goal. All
per-turn fields are REQUIRED, per v5's own rule that a field which may go missing will go missing
where it matters.

### 3.2 `x` and `xj`, defined exactly

Both are **observed advantages**, not thresholds. The threshold is emitted separately and
constantly as **`m=2500`** — `M` in basis points, present on every turn so that no reader has to
infer which `M` a wire was produced with.

**`x` — per-turn maximum single-unit overrule, in basis points.**
Over own units that (i) entered the turn with a kept goal, (ii) had a strictly positive best
pre-bonus score `S_kept` among candidates carrying it, and (iii) recorded a different goal:

```
x = max over those units of  floor(10000 * (S_chal - S_kept) / S_kept)
```

- **Denominator:** `S_kept`, the unit's best **pre-bonus** score among candidates carrying the kept
  goal; strictly positive by (ii), so the division is total.
- **Numerator:** `S_chal - S_kept`, with `S_chal` the pre-bonus score of the candidate matched to
  that unit's emitted command.
- **Sign:** a unit contributes only when `S_chal > S_kept`. A unit whose chosen candidate scored
  `<= S_kept` was **outvoted by its partner's term on the joint path**, not overruled; it
  contributes nothing to `x` and is counted in **`xo=`**.
- **Rounding:** `floor` toward zero, then cast to the unsigned field. Never round to nearest.
- **Saturation:** clamped to **999999** and emitted as exactly that. A saturated value is
  decode-visible and is read as "at least 100x", never as a measurement.
- **Empty case:** `x=0` when no unit qualifies. Units with `S_kept <= 0` (the bonus is inert by
  §4.1 of the r1 packet) are excluded and counted in **`ki=`**.

**`xj` — the decision-relevant one on the two-unit path.** Emitted when exactly two own units both
entered the turn with live kept goals and the pair actually chosen was **not** the keeping pair:

```
xj = floor(10000 * (S_B - S_A) / S_A)
```

with `S_A` the **pre-bonus sum** of the keeping pair and `S_B` the pre-bonus sum of the chosen
pair; same flooring and same 999999 saturation. `xj=0` when the keeping pair was chosen or when
fewer than two units had live kept goals. If the keeping pair is not `compatible` (`:908`) or not
`stock_compatible` (`:927`), `S_A` does not exist: `xj=0` and the turn is counted in **`xn=`**.

`x` is a per-unit diagnostic of the outcome; **`xj` is the quantity the margin test actually
decides on** when two trolls are on the board, which is the case this candidate exists for. Both
are emitted because reading only `x` on a two-unit turn would attribute a joint decision to one
troll — the same class of error as reading a pair goal off a unit token.

### 3.3 Census fields

`kp=` goals kept · `kr=` goals released this turn · `ka= kb= kc= kd= ke=` the release reasons
R-a..R-e · `ki=` inert-bonus units (`S_kept <= 0`) · `xo=` outvoted units · `xn=` turns where the
keeping pair was infeasible · `m=` the margin constant. `pz=1`, `sp=0` and `b=0` remain invariants
under R3 and a violation is a defect in the rule, not a curiosity.
