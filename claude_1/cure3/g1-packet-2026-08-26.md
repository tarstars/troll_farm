### 1.1 Five construction notes — places the packet's letter has no referent in the base

Each was found while implementing the packet, each is implemented in the way that keeps the
packet's intent, and each is named here rather than absorbed silently. They are recorded in the
generator's docstring so they travel with the code.

- **F4 — `Shack` gets no `impossible` test.** §3.4 names `view.shacks[0]` as the goal cell, but that
  cell is **never** in `view.walkable`: `parse_static_map` (`:328-355`) inserts only `'.'` cells and
  the shack is `'0'`. The literal test would release **every** `Shack` goal on the turn after it is
  recorded. The neighbour form is no better — the `Shack` candidate is emitted *only* when no bank
  cell is reachable (`:590-623`), so that test fires exactly when the goal is live. `Shack` ends on
  `done` (`total_carried() == 0`) or `dead`, both reachable and bounded.
- **F5 — §5.3's `xj` fallback clause is unreachable.** `L|g ⊆ L`, so a restricted pair existing
  implies an unrestricted pair exists. Emitted 0 there; `xn` counts contests only.
- **F6 — `k=1` is widened** from "valid but not live" to "holds a valid goal whose emitted command
  does not carry it", because §5.1's three values do not cover a live-restricted unit whose command
  `resolve_move_conflicts` rewrote into something no candidate proposed.
- **F7 — `rt` is producer-independent.** §3.3's row is worded against "the unit's producer this
  turn", but §1(a) runs every release test **before any candidate is built**. Implemented as the
  row's own predicate (`plant.kind != kind`, `:714`) against the goal's own recorded kind.
- **F8 — `last_carried` and `last_inventory` are not implemented.** §3 lists them in the snapshot,
  but no predicate as written reads either. Two unread fields are exactly the inert check this
  programme has paid for.

`ERASE_WHEN_NOT_LIVE` is ruled `false` and is therefore **prose, not a constant**: the `true` arm is
code nothing would ever execute.

## 6. §9.10 — the no-margin risk gate, and it FIRES

Three conditions were pre-committed. Two are met.

1. **`ka >= 30` — MET, and not marginally.** `ka` reaches **171** turns. A troll holds one goal for
   most of a 200-turn game. The absolute form has no mechanism that can end that hold except the
   world ending it.
2. **A total give-up that turns a game's own-score delta negative by more than the noise band —
   MET.** `m061:1` is −47 own points against a measured arena noise band of σ ≈ 1.501.
3. **`nl` dominated by the producer-switch cause — NOT met.** `nl_producer = 0` over the whole
   panel; `nl` is 1,151 admissibility + 10 door + **5 other**. The residual walk-back §2 named as
   the open risk **did not materialise**, and the cause the packet expected to be rare is the whole
   distribution.

**`nl_other = 5` is a finding in its own right** (it was 0 on the fixtures): the four not-live
causes of §2 are a claim about the base, and five unit-turns falsify completeness. Small, named,
not swept.

Per the pre-commitment, and in the packet's own words: **the rule is too strong, and it is not to be
repaired by adding a margin back.** What the numbers actually point at is the release predicates —
a `Tree` goal that is only `done` when the carry fills can outlive everything else on the board —
and that is a ruling for the coordinator, not a knob for me.
