# Track E — the endgame gap: what our trolls do in the last fifty turns that the top bots' trolls do not

Date: 2026-09-02 · Task `20260902-endgame-move-gap` · claude_1 · a read, nothing played.

## The answer in one paragraph

**The MOVE gap is real, it is confined to the last fifty turns, and it is not production left on the
table by idle trolls.** Per troll and per turn our champion moves as much as the top four until turn 250
(0.50–0.56 MOVE per troll-turn against their 0.41–0.58); in turns 251–300 it drops to 0.17 while they
stay at 0.37–0.62, and the same games' opponents at 0.51. Our trolls stand because the map is felled: by
turn 250 our own clear-cut has left a median of four tree-size units standing, 64 of the 160 games are
over before turn 251 (the referee ends a game with no tree; we win 43 of those 64), and in the 96 games
that run on, a fifth of our late troll-turns carry no command — but 84 % of those idle turns are terminal
waits where no reachable tree could be felled or harvested before turn 300, and more than half fall in
the last ten turns. Everything an idle-troll rule could still recover is bounded at about **six points a
game** (three from trees left standing that an idle troll could have felled, half a point of fruit, two
and a half from co-chop wood duplication), against a last-fifty gap of **46 points** in the games we
trail. That gap decomposes as roster × idleness × output: we field 2.00 trolls to their 2.86 (×0.70), our
trolls act on 79 % of their turns to their 93 % (×0.85), and per acting troll-turn we bank 0.45 points to
their 0.48 (×0.93). **The endgame gap is the third troll, not a parking bug.** Candidate rule: none for
the endgame; the line closes into Track P, whose ladder buys the roster.

## 4. Candidate rule: none for the endgame — the line closes into Track P

What an endgame rule could recover, as an upper bound (trees feasible for an idle troll at some idle turn
and still standing when the game ended, i.e. taken by nobody): 0.24 trees a game, **3.3 wood points a game
(median 0), 0.3 fruit**, present in 11 of 96 games; plus co-chop wood duplication (two own choppers on the
death turn of a size-1 or size-3 tree take one extra wood, `engine.rs:604–627`, validated by the
reconstruction's 0 carry mismatches): 61 such fellings had an idle partner, **+2.5 points a game**. Six
points a game at most, one eighth of the 46-point last-fifty deficit in the games we trail, and a rule
that fires in the last ten turns of a lost map. The panel could read six points (the orchard-6 interval
half-width is 4.5; same-lineage pairing under 1), but it would be reading the wrong cause.

The gap is a roster gap and a map gap: our two trolls fell the map by turn 250 and stand on stumps; their
three or four keep working a map they planted. Delineate's late lead comes from trees kept alive, the
other three's from the third and fourth troll — both are Track P's macro layer (the train ladder to five
trolls, the orchard, the produce→deforest switch), not an endgame patch. **Recommendation: close Track E
as "real gap, wrong layer"; carry two numbers to Track P's loss read — MOVE per troll-turn in 251–300
(ours 0.17, the field 0.37–0.62) and tree-size units standing at the end (ours median 4) — as the two
cheapest endgame signatures to check on the port.**
