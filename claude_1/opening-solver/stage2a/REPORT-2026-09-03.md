## 2. Two defects the one-game probe found before any gate

1. **The dance** (`probe.py`, map `7b515d6d…`): the starter stepped between two cells for 20 turns. A
   dispatcher whose tasks persist in Python, ported as "recompute the argmax every turn", flips whenever an
   input depends on the troll's position without being monotone along its walk — here the bottleneck
   weights and the seed's walk, measured from the door nearest the troll, which alternates between two
   door-adjacent cells. Fixed: measured from the doors as a set; the 5 % hold; every remaining
   position-dependent term monotone.
2. **Points before the bill** (map `c14dea6a…`, the smoke's one game without a third troll in the first
   run): with only plums left on the bill and the map's one reachable plum tree 16 steps away, a near apple
   tree's quarter-point fruits every other turn outscored it; at turn 200 the shack held 65 lemons and no
   plum. Fixed as R5 says: the bill first.

Both are in the standing lesson list (the memory note and `README.md`).
