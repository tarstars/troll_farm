## Every branch got both halves of the standing rule

The rule is that a fixture counts only once observed firing against a deliberately violating
subject. Each of the four therefore has a pair — the exempting or limiting case that must stay
silent, and the violation that must fire:

- **(d)** `PLANT 0 BANANA` disposes of a carried banana → no `lost_bananas`; the same
  disappearance under `MOVE` → `lost_bananas` fires.
- **(e)** held exactly 12 turns → no `carried_overage` (12 is not > 12); held 13 → fires.
- **(f)** harvested inside the final six turns and still carried → excused; harvested before the
  window → `unbanked_at_end` fires.
- **(g)** provenance is load-bearing rather than decorative: a late `HARVEST` is excused by the
  T-6 grace, a late `PICK` is **not**, and the episode carries `provenance: bank_pick`. That
  asymmetry is the branch's observable consequence, so the fixture asserts it rather than
  asserting the label directly.
