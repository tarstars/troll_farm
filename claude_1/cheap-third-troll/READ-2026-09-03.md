# The cheap third troll — the read (step 1 of `20260902-cheap-third-troll`)

Date: 2026-09-03 · claude_1 · a read from what we hold, nothing played, nothing built.

## One page for the owner

**What a cheap troll costs.** With two trolls owned, the weakest troll worth having (speed 1, carry 1, no
harvest power, chop 1 — written 1/1/0/1) costs 3 plums, 3 lemons, 2 apples and 3 iron. Our champion never has
that in the bank: after it trains its second troll (median turn 10; turn 1 in 116 of 319 games, when the
starting draw already covers the standard troll), the bank holds a median of 1 plum, 0 lemons, 5 apples, 7
bananas and 0 iron. The cheap bill is short in every one of the 319 games — by a median of 6 items (2 plums,
2 lemons, 2 iron; the apples are already there). Bananas cost nothing and buy nothing.

**Why the bill is dear.** Two facts of this bot decide it. Its trained troll has no harvest power, so every fruit
of the bill must be picked by the starting troll, which carries one item at a time: a fruit is not a one-turn
pickup but a round trip — walk out, harvest, walk back, drop. And after training the champion banks no fruit or
iron at all for the rest of the game (0.03 items a game in 320 games); it only *spends* the bank, planting 9.8
banked fruit a game as seeds and felling them as size-1 trees for 44 points a game — 81 % of its plums and
lemons end that way. The trolls do pass fruit (a plum tree with fruit stands within one step of the empty-handed
starter on 17 troll-turns a game between turns 20 and 150; a lemon tree on 17; apples on 20), but each pickup
still costs the trip home.

**The detour, costed on each game's own board** (the trip model walks the exact post-training map, tree by tree,
with the referee's regrowth; calibrated against the champion's real openings, it under-reads them by one turn):
the starter needs a median of **37 turns** for the fruit (a quarter of the games under 26, a quarter over 55)
while the trained troll mines the iron in a median of 9 turns beside it; the starter alone would need 54. Only 107
of 319 games get the bill inside 30 turns. The wood forgone while collecting is **11 points a game** — about
sixteen turns of two-troll chopping, so under the card's thirty-turn line. The fruit spent is 8 points at face
value, and up to 25 points if those plums and lemons would have become size-1 wood, which is what the champion
does with them.

**What the troll earns.** Our own starting troll is the honest proxy for a 1/1/0/1 (same speed, carry and chop,
same policy): it banks 0.029 wood a troll-turn before turn 100, 0.042 in 101–200, 0.047 in 201–250, 0.082 late
(the swap) — 0.28 of what our 2/2/0/2 banks (0.150). The third troll would exist from turn 52 (median 49) and have
203 turns of forest. Uncontested, that is **37 points** a game; the forest is finite and shared, so as a share
shift (our 0.55 of the wood felled after its arrival becomes 0.60) it is **20 points**; the point estimate that
respects what is still standing at the end is **30 points** [28, 32].

**Recommendation: dead on paper.** The bill alone is under the card's thirty-turn line (16 two-troll turns of
wood, 8–25 points of fruit) and the troll's earnings (30 points) cover it only when its fruit is priced at face
value; priced at what the champion actually turns fruit into, the design loses 6.5 points a game with an interval
clear of zero, and the honest expectation lies between the two readings, nearer the second, because the 81 % swap
rate is measured, not assumed. The one variant that does not lose under either reading — B, buying only when the
map hands us the bill inside 30 turns — is worth at most +5 points a game and −1 at worst, in a third of the games:
not a thing the field panel can read (its half-width is about 4.5 points), and not worth a build, a bed, a smoke
and a ladder hour. The coordinator's expectation before the numbers (25 turns of collecting, a third of a strong
troll's wood, net a few points either way) was right in shape; the collecting is slower than 25 because only one
troll can harvest and it carries one item, and the fruit is dearer than its face value because of the swap.

**What would change the answer.** A cheaper fruit: a second troll with harvest power 1 (one apple more) makes the
fruit a two-troll job — a different card, one that changes the champion's second troll. Or the third troll bought
with the bank's *bananas* — the one kind the champion holds seven of; the referee prices bananas at zero for
training, so no talent draws on them. Nothing on this card touches either.

## Method, sources, limits

* **Replays:** the champion of record's two collected batches, `41202036` (agent 6667789, 160 games) and `41230202`
  (agent 6689203, 160), read from scratch copies (`/data/scratch/claude1-champ-41202036/`, `-41230202/`, written
  from the two `games-*.jsonl.gz`) through `local_claude_1/reconstructions/fits/reconstruct.py` (referee diff as
  authority; mismatch classes only the expected growth-tick and unreported-position kinds; final scores equal the
  replays' in 318 of 320, the two `final_score` notes are the pre-existing reconstructor limit). One game has 0
  turns and no TRAIN; excluded from every per-game table.
* **`replay_read.py` → `games-41202036.json`, `games-41230202.json`:** the per-game facts (opening, banks, bills'
  shortfalls at every checkpoint and their minimum, items banked per window, seeds spent, encounters, per-troll
  wood by talents and phase for both seats, fellings by turn and side, standing wood at the end).
* **`detour_cost.py` → `detour-41202036.json`, `detour-41230202.json`:** the greedy trip model on the exact
  post-training board (BFS over grass; harvest 1 a trip for the starter; MINE min(chop, carry); the referee's
  cooldown/growth rule for regrowth, water bonus included; opponents' harvests and fellings not modelled — they make
  the real detour longer, not shorter). Calibration: actual − model median +1 turn on the champion's own 203
  collecting openings.
* **`paper.py` → `paper-2026-09-03.json`:** the economics per game; bootstrap 95 % intervals over games (2,000
  resamples, seed 1). The 290 older raw replays (`/home/tarstars/prj/troll_farm/data/raw/games/`) feed only the
  field's talent table (`/data/scratch/claude1-ctt-raw290.json`, scratch).
* **Limits, named:** the third troll's earnings use our starter's rate under the champion's policy as the proxy
  (its harvest power is never used after the opening); the contested estimate is a capacity-share model, not a
  played game; the seed reading prices every plum and lemon at the swap's 4 points times the measured 0.81 share
  without charging the swap's late troll-turns (they are mostly idle turns, Track E) and without crediting the
  third troll's own swaps (it would have fewer seeds to swap). The two readings bracket the truth; the numbers
  that decide the card (the bill's shortfall, the trip lengths, the rates by talents) are exact counts.
