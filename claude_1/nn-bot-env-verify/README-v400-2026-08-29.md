## One thing to know about the two parity numbers

The gate reports `transition_parity` and `terminal_parity` as separate keys, but both counters are
incremented by the same successful call to `verify_terminal_parity` (`cgauto/rl_full_env.py:770–772`),
which replays every turn and then checks the terminal metadata. They are therefore one measurement
printed twice: they cannot differ, and a failure of either raises and stops the gate rather than
lowering one count. That does not weaken the evidence — transition parity really is verified on all
1,000 games — but the separation the coordinator asked for is nominal, and a reader should not
take `1,000/1,000` twice as two independent checks. Making them independent is a two-line change
in the runner (call `verify_transition_parity` first, count it, then `verify_terminal_parity`), and
it is codex_1's call whether that is worth a day-6 edit.
