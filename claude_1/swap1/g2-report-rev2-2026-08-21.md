## The basket exhibits — NOT met, and one half is unanswerable

`g2-baskets-rev2-2026-08-21.json`. Three arms through the shared harness; the subject arm

| OSC-027 | NOT_FIXED | NOT_FIXED | **0** | — | yes ("not alpha's shape") |

Nothing was healed on the 34 and **nothing was lost**: no fixture that was FIXED on the base is
anything else on alpha.

**OSC-005 is a substantive miss.** Its recorded episode runs turns 7–18 and resolves; alpha's only
fire is at turn **52**, outside the window entirely. Alpha does not touch that episode, and the
episode is still there.

**OSC-001 and OSC-012 are an instrument limit, and I am not working around it.**
`episode_identity` asks whether *this run replays the recorded window*. An alpha fire at or before
the window's last turn necessarily rewrites those command lines, so `grade()` returns
NOT_REPRODUCIBLE_ON_BASE for precisely the fixtures the cure reached. Measured over the 11
fixtures that reproduce on the base, the two sets are **exactly equal, 7 for 7**:

```
alpha fires inside the recorded window : 001 002 012 013 017 021 024
newly rejected by identity on alpha    : 001 002 012 013 017 021 024
```

(The first version of this check compared the sets over all 34 and disagreed on five fixtures;
they are the ones the base already fails to replay, where alpha firing inside the window carries
no information. The scoped claim is the one that holds.)

So on a **cure** arm the identity gate cannot return FIXED for any fixture the cure acted on. The
amendment's "005, 012, 001 … must turn FIXED there" is therefore unreachable as written for 001
and 012 — not failed, unanswerable. **This is handed back to the coordinator and owner**, because
the repair is a semantics decision, not a measurement: the identity gate belongs on the arm that
is supposed to replay the episode (the base), and a cure arm needs a different question — "is the
recorded shape gone from this run", which is what the panel column already measures.
