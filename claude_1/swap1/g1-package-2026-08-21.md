### Why the shadow, and what it closes

Whole-game byte-identity is evidence *only* on a fixture where α never fires: after the first
fire the two runs are different worlds and a later difference means nothing. So the probe carries
the **base's own seam function, lifted verbatim out of the base file**, and on every tick runs it
on a clone of the pre-resolve command vector with the same `view`. The G-1 claim — *a tick on
which the trigger did not fire is byte-identical to the base's* — is therefore measured on every
one of the 6,800 ticks, on the SAME input state, including ticks long after a divergence. The
gate is exact in **both** directions: a difference with no fire would mean α is not inert; a fire
that changed nothing would mean α emitted nothing.

## The blocking finding — G1-A: α creates a swap-dance

**111 re-swaps of the same unordered pair within 4 ticks, on OSC-006 (98) and OSC-011 (13).**

- **OSC-006** — the pair `{0,2}` swaps on **27 consecutive ticks, 2 through 28**, every one on
  the `NODETOUR` path with the partner's `CHOP` displaced each time. Two trolls chopping trees on
  opposite sides of each other in a 1-wide corridor: each swap satisfies one of them and
  un-satisfies the other, whose re-plan next tick sends it straight back. Fire rate on this
  fixture is **6.75 % of unit-turns** — over the card's 2 % kill rule on its own.
- **OSC-011** — the pair `{0,2}` swaps on ticks 27–31 and again at 33, all on the `YIELD` path
  with a detour available.

This is risk 1 of §8, named before any code existed and now measured: *the dance cure created a
new dance*. It is a design failure, not a tuning matter, so I am not damping it. Three remedies
are visible and **I am not choosing between them without a ruling**, because each changes what α
means:

1. **A same-pair cooldown** (α declines a pair it swapped within N ticks). Cheapest, and the one
   my own note pre-emptively refused to invent — it buries the mutual-need case rather than
   deciding it.
2. **A progress conjunct**: fire only if the exchange strictly reduces the *pair's combined* BFS
   distance to their targets. Principled, and it kills OSC-006's mutual case by construction
   (each swap leaves the sum unchanged) while leaving the yield cases untouched.
3. **Rule the mutual case out of α's scope**: T1 additionally requires that `U`'s own goal does
   not lie past `m`. This is closest to the charter's wording ("when two of our trolls need to
   *pass* each other") but needs a definition of `U`'s goal at the transport level, where only
   commands are visible — likely a widening beyond the seam, which would be a §6 exception.

My reading, offered as a recommendation and not acted on: **(2)**. It is a conjunct in the same
style as T2b, measurable, and it does not need state that survives a tick.

## Two secondary findings, reported because they change what a later gate can claim

**G1-B — OSC-027 never fires.** The card expects 005, 027, 012, 001 to fire. 005, 012 and 001 do;
**027 does not**, and it is byte-identical to the base for the whole game. The most likely reason
is the one I measured last wake in the real-end re-grade: the fixture replay is a **re-run**, not
a playback, and the champion re-run is a different game from the recorded episode on 23 of the 34
windows. If 027's recorded stall does not reproduce under the base, α has nothing to fire on and
the fixture cannot demonstrate the cure. **This is a limit on what G-2 can be asked**, and it
should be settled before G-2 is graded rather than after.

**G1-C — the card's "back on the tree within 2 ticks" is not measurable on a clean fire in this
corpus, and is false where it is measurable.** 27 of the 52 fires displaced real work, and **all
27 are inside OSC-006's dance**; the displaced verb returned after 3–29 ticks (median 15), never
within 2. Every one of the other 25 fires displaced a `WAIT`, so nothing was lost and the
expectation does not apply. The honest statement is that the expectation is currently untested,
not that it passed.
