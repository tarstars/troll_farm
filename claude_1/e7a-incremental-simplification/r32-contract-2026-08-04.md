# Round-32 contract — delete `endgame_candidates`' `safe_regeneration` (immutable)

Written before candidate generation. **Directly unlocked by round 31**: the parameter was
not single-valued in the round-30 source, because one of its three call sites forwarded
`main_candidates`' parameter. Round 31 replaced that forward with literal `true`, making all
three sites literal and this deletion provable. This is the clearest demonstration in the
programme that a deletion creates the next deletion.

## Invariant

1. `endgame_candidates` has exactly three call sites, all passing literal `true` in the
   `safe_regeneration` position after round 31 (machine-verified: one occurrence of the
   `type_to_cut` form, two of the `self.type_to_cut` form).
2. The parameter has exactly two body reads: a negated disjunct and a conjunct guard.

## Replacement (exact)

- declaration `type_to_cut:Option<PlantKind>,safe_regeneration:bool,protected_tree:Option<Cell>,`
  → `type_to_cut:Option<PlantKind>,protected_tree:Option<Cell>,`;
- `let can_plant_here=!safe_regeneration||view.plant_at(unit.cell).is_none();` →
  `let can_plant_here=view.plant_at(unit.cell).is_none();`
  (`!true||X` ≡ `false||X` ≡ `X`);
- `if safe_regeneration&&view.plant_at(*cell).is_some(){continue;}` →
  `if view.plant_at(*cell).is_some(){continue;}` (`true&&X` ≡ `X`);
- all three call sites drop the argument.

## Supported-state argument

Both reads reduce by the constant-propagation identities above; `view.plant_at(...)` is a
pure lookup, so removing the short-circuit that could previously skip it cannot change any
observable state — with the parameter fixed true the short-circuit never fired anyway.

## Rejection condition

Reject if any anchor count differs; if `safe_regeneration` survives anywhere; if the
candidate is not strictly smaller; or if any of the four gates fails.
