## What I did not measure, and will not pretend to

- **The fourth kill rule has no population on a read.** "Any P1/P2 row migrating to a parked or
  stalled shape" is a fuzz-panel gate over a candidate/parent pair; a ladder read has no parent
  stream and no panel row. I record it **NOT MEASURABLE**, not PASS. The nearest read-side
  evidence, offered as evidence and not as a substitute: longest hold `b` = **2** (the bound holds
  in the wild), 0 long-stall games, 0 D-3, worst troll 8.19 % idle in one 160-game read.
- **Long-stall is a proxy** for P4, built from P4's own imported arithmetic (`progress_turns`,
  `stall_windows`, `live_horizon`, window 60) but run on replays, which do not know the outcome of
  the final turn. Both corpora are measured with the identical function, which is what the
  comparison needs.
- **Clause (a) is underpowered and I am not using that to soften the FAIL.** 11 of 25 has a 95 %
  Clopper–Pearson interval of **[24.40 %, 65.07 %]**, which *contains* the 65.00 % bar, and
  Fisher's exact test against 52/80 gives **p = 0.1003**. The bar was pre-committed and the read
  is under it, so the clause fails; what the read cannot do is *distinguish* 44 % from 65 %. Both
  facts are on the record and neither cancels the other.
- **D-1 off replays remains an upper bound** — the adapter reconstructs plant clocks and the error
  direction invents dancing. It applies to the 25 here and to the 34 of the v3 read alike.
- The two reads are **different arms, different days, different opponent fields, no
  randomisation.** The charter fixed the v3 read as clause (b)'s comparator, and I graded what the
  charter fixed; I do not claim the difference is the rule's alone.
