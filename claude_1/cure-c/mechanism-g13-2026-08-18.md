## The headline, stated first because it is the part that does not flatter the candidate

**Most of the "over-delivery" is RE-ATTRIBUTION, not extra curing.** The G1 metric counts turns
attributed to `NO_GOAL_ASSIGNED`. A turn leaves that bucket the moment the generator offers
anything non-WAIT — **even if the troll still stands still**, because the stall is then
attributed to a later stage. Decomposing what the resident's no-goal turns *became* under the
candidate:

| fixture | resident no-goal turns | UNIT ACTED | still no-goal | re-attributed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **OSC-009** | 4 | **0** | 0 | 4 → `CANNOT_USE_WORK` |
| **OSC-031** | 189 | **0** | 89 | 94 → `CANNOT_USE_WORK`, 6 → `GOAL_SPLIT_WRONG` |

**Zero turns in either fixture became a unit doing work.** "OSC-009 4 → 0" is a true statement
about the metric and a false impression about the trolls: on all four turns the candidate's unit
waits, and the turn is now scored `CANNOT_USE_WORK` because in the candidate's world there is no
usable work for it at all.

I am reporting this against my own interest. Read as "the cure fixed four more turns", the G1.3
misses look like a bonus; read mechanically, they are the metric moving, and the owner should
rule on the second reading.

## OSC-009 — mechanism **M-TRAJ**, and my branch attribution was RIGHT

The registry said: *all 4 turns are ENDGAME-branch; C alters only the non-endgame fall-through
and never runs.* The instrument confirms the branch claim exactly — all four resident no-goal
turns carry `branch=ENDGAME`. **C's door does not run on them.** The prediction's reasoning was
sound; its arithmetic assumed a whole-game replay would preserve the state, which it does not.

- first no-goal turn **80**; first divergent turn **1**
- pre-divergence same-state pairs cured: **0**
- what the 4 turns became: **4 × `CANNOT_USE_WORK`** (unit still waits)

So the residual did not vanish because C cured it. It vanished because by turn 80 the candidate
is in a different world, one where the unit has no usable work rather than unoffered work.
