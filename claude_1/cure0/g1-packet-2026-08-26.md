## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The clause change does what §1 of the packet says it does, and the corpus says that is a bad idea.
Containment holds exactly as pre-registered — **97 diverging games, every one of them a game where
the champion's fallback fires, zero counterexamples out of 240**. The panel total moves
**+530 own-score points** (88 games up, 9 down). But the detector table is **materially worse on a
detector that was clean**: **D-2 goes 0 → 387 episodes across 18 games**, **P4 stalls 16 → 85**,
**P3 0 → 5**, and blocking games **43 → 118, all 75 newly blocking, none cured**. And the game the
edit exists for goes the wrong way: **`m061` is −18 and −9 own-score points**, not the +75 I
pre-registered. The mechanism is one line of the wire: the surviving 7,500-point regeneration
`PICK` beats every real job for a shack-adjacent empty-handed troll, it banks the fruit next turn,
and it picks it up again — **a PICK↔DROP two-cycle that runs to the end of the game**. Two
pre-registered outcomes are therefore falsified by my own run, and per §2.4 this is a **STOP AND
ASK**, not a delivery. My recommendation is in §7 and it is: **do not ship this clause as written.**

## 5. `m061`: the mechanism, at the code line, and a correction to my own packet

**What the wire says.** `m061:0`, first divergence at **t=113**:

```
t=112  base  CHOP 0;DROP 2          fix  CHOP 0;DROP 2
t=113  base  CHOP 0;MOVE 2 2 2      fix  CHOP 0;PICK 2 APPLE
t=114  base  CHOP 0;MOVE 2 3 2      fix  CHOP 0;DROP 2
t=115  base  CHOP 0;MOVE 2 4 2      fix  CHOP 0;PICK 2 APPLE
…
t=119  base  CHOP 0;HARVEST 2       fix  CHOP 0;PICK 2 APPLE
```

`m061:1` is the same shape from t=109. The champion sends unit 2 across the map to **harvest**;
arm-fix has it **PICK the fruit out of the shack stock and DROP it back, every second turn, to the
end of the game**. The clause chain is exactly the one the packet describes, now running in the
direction nobody wanted: the regeneration `PICK` is built at score **7,500** (`main_candidates`
`:1782-1797`), the fallback no longer discards it, it dominates every chop and harvest candidate,
and the very next turn `carried > 0 && is_adjacent(cell, shacks[0])` (`:1779-1781`) offers the
`DROP` that undoes it. **Nothing in the clause makes the `PICK` lead to a `PLANT`.** That is the
whole defect and it is a design gap, not a tuning one.

**The correction, and it is to my own G-0 packet.** Expectation 4 said `m061` would gain **+75**
because "the two trolls stop being goal-less for 131 / 96 turns". Re-read at the source
(`claude_1/cure2/m061-diagnosis-2026-08-25.md` §, table row *"score, rule-off → instrument"*), that
**−75 is the cost of Candidate 2's swap rule**: 75 → 39 and 82 → 43, **rule-off against
instrument**. The rule-off arm is behaviourally the champion, and it already scores **75 and 82**
— which is exactly what the champion scores in this panel. **The champion is not goal-less on
`m061` and does not lose 75 points there.** I carried a number measured against a different arm
into a prediction about the champion, and G-0 was accepted with that error in it. It is mine; I am
naming it rather than letting the −18 pass as a surprise.

## 6. Determinism, fixtures, P4b

- **Determinism: PASS.** The panel was run twice from the same config. All 240 rows are
  byte-identical field for field, and the **uncompressed** games stream is identical
  (`sha256 4898bd4a31ad58c71bb2a800fcf5625bce3a2288e3f1e32b51476ed98dc43292` both runs). The only
  difference anywhere in the two reports is `wall_time_seconds` (18.24 / 10.27). The `.gz` digests
  differ because gzip stamps mtime into its header; that is a container fact, not a result.
- **Fixtures: 34/34 identical between the arm and the champion**, verdict for verdict and field for
  field — 11 graded `NOT_FIXED`, 23 `NOT_REPRODUCIBLE_ON_BASE` on **both** arms. The 23 are a
  pre-existing property of the frozen corpus (the entry state or window commands no longer
  reproduce), not something this arm caused, and they are reported as such rather than counted as
  passes.
- **P4b: `NOT_EVALUABLE`, and my §2.3 prediction was wrong.** I wrote *"Candidate 0's arms are not
  v5 arms … I expect P4b to be evaluable here."* They are not: `--p4b` reports **GATE_UNREADY** with
  **172,364 evaluator errors**, first error `('m000', 0) turn 1: telemetry decode: no NARRATE token:
  'MSG yamo-carry-regen-transit-idle-harvest-rust'`. The champion emits a **banner** MSG, not a
  telemetry payload, so the decoder has nothing to read — 384 unit lives, **0** observable
  transitions. As pre-registered: **no proxy, no dropped table row, and the unchartered
  `20260826-p4b-narrator-param` amendment is not enacted to make it green.** A gate that cannot run
  is reported as a gate that cannot run, and the reason it cannot run here is that the arm has no
  narrator at all.

## 7. STOP AND ASK — three of §2.4's four triggers fired

§2.4 pre-committed that any of these is published on the owner's page rather than resolved by me.
Fired: **a detector materially worse** (D-2 0 → 387, P4 16 → 85, 75 newly blocking games); **`m061`
did not change in the intended direction**; **a changed game moves down by more than σ** (`m061`,
−18 and −9) — for which I *can* name the mechanism at the code line, so it is reported with the
mechanism rather than as a mystery. Expectation (1), the one whose violation would have been a
**BLOCK on my own arm**, is the one that held.

**My recommendation, which is not a ruling.** Do not ship this clause as written. The edit is
correct about what it fixes — the champion really does discard a list it just built, on 50,974
turns of 210 games — and the corpus really does pay +530 own-score points for keeping it. But
keeping the 7,500-point regeneration `PICK` alive without anything that makes it lead to a `PLANT`
converts a discarded list into a **PICK↔DROP two-cycle**, and 387 new D-2 episodes and 75 newly
blocking games are too high a price for +530 points that a smaller change might also buy.

**Two follow-ups I can name but have not taken** (no code was written after the run, and none will
be without a ruling): (a) keep the fallback's `extend` but **withhold the regeneration `PICK` from
the fallback path specifically**, which would test whether the +530 survives without the cycle;
(b) leave the clause alone and treat the discarded list as a champion defect that is **not worth
fixing at this price**. Choosing between them is a design ruling, and re-running the panel to pick
the greener one is exactly the retuning I pre-committed not to do.

**Not mine to close:** whether Candidate 0 continues at all; the champion header correction, still
OPEN (`readable/door1-champion.rs` lines 6-8 and 17-20 still assert two digests that do not
reproduce); the PR, since `gh` is absent on this VM and the deliverable of record is
`readable/diffs/candidate-0-regeneration-fallback.diff` per the 061613Z amendment.
