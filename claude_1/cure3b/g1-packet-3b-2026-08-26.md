## 1. In one breath, for the owner

The rule works exactly as D-3's read said it would, and it buys nothing.

D-3 found a troll on map `m061` that kept one goal for 171 turns and stood on two cells doing
nothing. Candidate 3b adds one release: **a troll that has sat on at most two cells for 20 turns

170 turns to 43 and 78**.

**The score does not move.** `m061` scores 32 and 35, the same as Candidate 3, still **43 and 47
points behind the champion**. Releasing the goal frees the troll, and the troll then does
something else that is worth nothing. The pre-commitment said `m061` had to come within 10 points
of the champion; it came within 43. That gate fails, and it fails on the one game the rule was
built for.

The second failure is mine, not the rule's: I pre-committed that no goal anywhere on the panel
would live longer than 60 turns, and the longest is **88** — on `m068`, a game where the troll is
*working* the whole time and the rule correctly declines to touch it. That gate was mis-specified.
It fails anyway. The card forbids re-tuning after the fact and it is right to.

What the build did prove, and it is worth keeping: the rule is **free**. It costs nothing anywhere
else on the panel — 238 of 240 games are byte-for-byte the play Candidate 3 produced, no game
Candidate 3 won is lost, and the +25 points Candidate 3 gained outside `m061` are untouched.
The line of work ends here with an understood, negative result rather than a mystery.

## 4. The one place the charter and the rule disagreed, resolved before the build

The card's summary line lists the work commands as `CHOP/PICK/DROP/PLANT` — four verbs, omitting
`HARVEST`. The rule's source (D-3's read §4(d)) and the probe that produced every number the card
quotes (`claude_1/cure3/m061/fixprobe.py:32`) use **five**, including `HARVEST`. I measured both
on the existing archives before writing any code (`claude_1/cure3/m061/workset-split.json`,
produced by `workset_split.py`):

| work set | runs cut | games touched | productive commands removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| five verbs (with `HARVEST`) | 6 | 6 | **58** — reproduces the card exactly |
| four verbs (without) | 9 | 9 | 96 |

The four-verb reading releases three extra games' trolls that were *harvesting* — the exact false
positive the "no work" clause exists to prevent. I implemented five, so that the card's own
numbers describe the thing I built. This is named here rather than absorbed silently.

## 5. One measurement error I made and corrected, before any verdict

The containment gate first read **240/240 FAIL**. The gate was fine; my reading of it was not — I
compared the arm's `MSG`-stripped stream against the champion's *unstripped* stream, and the
champion emits an announcement fragment of its own on turn 1, so every game "diverged" on turn 1.
Stripping both sides gives 240/240 identical. The fix is in `panel_read3b.py` with a comment
saying what went wrong; the gate's wording never changed. It is recorded because a gate that
turned from FAIL to PASS deserves to be read by someone else, and because the same mistake would
have made Candidate 3's containment look broken too.
