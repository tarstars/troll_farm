gating `adjudicated_by` authorship and canonical-message presence. G-1 condition 2 separately
requires a local-fixture rename of the roster coordinator to leave all 12 live quarantine entries

would make the rename test pass, but would violate the explicitly accepted no-weakening rule. I
did not make that security-policy choice implicitly.
