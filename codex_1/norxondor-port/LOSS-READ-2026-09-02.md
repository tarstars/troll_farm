# Norxondor port v2 — loss read before the one refinement loop

**Verdict:** `SWITCH_LATE/MEASURED`. The port has bought its third troll by median turn 74,
but keeps that three-troll roster in Produce mode until the roster-three deadline at turn 144.
It loses the shared wood race before that switch. The one refinement variable is therefore the
roster that ends Produce mode: implicit default **5**, refinement value **3**. No source parameter
was changed before this note and its result JSON were written.

## 4. Responsible layer

| layer | ruling | one-sentence evidence |
|---|---|---|
| funding economy | ruled out as the primary cause | It produces the intended third troll at median turn 74, and the early CHOP count (5.90) is close to the reconstructed bot's 7.00. |
| **Produce → Deforest switch** | **responsible** | The three-troll port remains in Produce to its roster-three turn-144 deadline; its chop rate is 12.67% through turn 150, then rises to 20.45% and begins closing the score gap after the switch. |
| conversion job | ruled out as the primary cause | Once Deforest is active, the port self-fells 3.79 planted trees and banks 8.90 wood items in turns 151–200, more than the champion's 6.92. |
| target choice inside a gate | ruled out as the primary cause | The inherited target layer converts the available post-switch work into a seven-point recovery; the missing score predates that gate's authority. |

## 5. Pre-registered single variable for the only loop

| field | v2 default | refinement value | exact effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `PRODUCE_ROSTER_CAP` | 5 (implicit in `roster >= 5`) | **3** | On the turn after the third troll is bought, enter Deforest; keep every floor, purchase, projected-completion test, roster-indexed deadline for games still at two trolls, conversion job, target score and tie-break unchanged. |

The third troll is bought at median turn 74 in 276 of 400 games, so this moves the common
three-troll game into Deforest at about turn 75 rather than turn 144. The 124 games that never
reach three trolls keep the existing roster-two turn-129 deadline. This is one scalar policy
boundary, not a target retune or a second conversion-job design.

The refinement succeeds only through the card's unchanged field panel. Before that verdict, its
mechanism checks are: the three-worker switch fires next turn; no switch before worker three;
no TRAIN after Deforest; compacted source equals readable source; compile, mechanics, bed,
legality and timing all remain clean.
