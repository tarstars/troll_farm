# Norxondor port: review-gated hybrid design

Date: 2026-09-02

Task: `20260902-norxondor-port`

Author: codex_1

Status: ready for the one required design review; no bot has been built from this note yet

## Decision

The hybrid is specifiable. Build the measured Norxondor economy as an explicit, small state
machine around the champion's already closed-loop-tested micro. Do not reuse the July learned
intent tree or either learned target ranker. The port's novelty is confined to the production
mode, the workforce ladder, a bounded orchard commitment, and the plant-and-cut banana conversion.

This is a behavioural port, not a claim of source equivalence. Unknown rules get one visible
default below. Rung 1, not teacher-forced agreement or action-count matching, decides whether the
whole policy is useful.

## 1. Why the July native controller failed

The July implementation failed at the **policy/state boundary**, with target error as the trigger;
the pathfinder was not the failing layer.

Evidence from the July records:

- The decomposition itself was sound: 99.856% of observed MOVE targets were the next cell and
  93.417% of episodes followed a shortest route. DROP, PICK, and MINE endpoints were legal or near
  exact. This does not point to BFS or command generation.
- The learned components were descriptions of teacher states: the 107-node intent tree reached
  76.937% held-game accuracy, the CHOP ranker retained 128 weights and about 41.8% exact target
  accuracy, and the HARVEST ranker retained 32 weights and about 37.1%. None of those numbers says
  the combined policy will recreate the inventory and geometry on which it was trained.
- `rust/src/strategies/norxondor_native.rs` has only fixed turn buckets in `phase(turn)`. It has no
  persistent Produce/Deforest state, no irreversible D transition, and no rule that makes all
  workers clear-cut after that transition. A learned `Intent::Farm` therefore stood in for a
  missing macro state.
- `farm_goal` and `command_for_goal(Intent::Farm)` allow a fruitless worker beside the shack to
  PICK whenever the classifier says Farm. There is no preselected plant cell, orchard target,
  global seed-job limit, or mode gate. Persistence then carries the speculative seed forward.
  This is the direct mechanism behind the PICK overshoot.
- Training was appended after unit commands. Because the referee applies PICK before TRAIN, a PICK
  could spend an otherwise affordable training bill in the same turn. The native controller did
  not make the economy a transaction.
- The exact purchase rule alone did not supply its own inputs. July's two-funder experiment showed
  the missing causal mechanism: two workers jointly collecting the next stage's deficits made the
  third worker attainable (+105.68 margin against Silver in that research comparison). The full
  native policy instead let per-unit predicted intents compete with funding.
- Repairs (direct CHOP/HARVEST/MINE continuation, one explicit miner, safer planting) improved
  symptoms but preserved the learned macro/micro coupling. The best full repair reached only 2.488
  workers and lost -172.663 paired margin / -97.263 score against the resident, negative against
  every opponent family. Its mean mix was about 38 CHOP and 68 PICK versus the then-replay target
  of about 159 and 17 (the fuller reconstruction now reports 189 and 14).

So the 38/68 drift was a loop: moderate target and intent errors changed stock and tree layout;
the classifier then saw non-teacher states; broad Farm decisions created more seed inventory and
fewer mature chop targets; persistent goals amplified the difference. Exact paths faithfully
carried workers toward the wrong macro objectives. Manually forcing the action counts would hide,
not repair, that cause.

Sources: the two 2026-07-18 Norxondor reports under
`data/analysis/live-agent-6553250/`, `rust/src/strategies/norxondor_native.rs`, and reconstruction
§5.8.

## 2. Frozen hybrid boundary

“As written” means an exact/high-confidence rule in the reconstruction. “Champion's code” means
copying the relevant token logic, not reimplementing its idea. “New with default” marks a choice
that the reconstruction cannot identify.

| Per-turn layer | Boundary | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Parse input, rules, board, score | **Champion's code** | Mature single-file machinery; Norxondor supplies no distinct rule here. |
| Global state | **New with default:** `Produce` then irreversible `Deforest`; no T and no second letter | P/D is measured, but T and the second flag are undecoded. Inventing them would add latent states with no rule. |
| TRAIN eligibility and spec | **Norxondor as written** | Floors, caps, componentwise maximum, same-turn trigger, roster-five cap, and no TRAIN in D are high-confidence over 443 trains. |
| TRAIN transaction | **New with default:** decide TRAIN before worker candidate generation and suppress all PICK that turn | The referee orders PICK before TRAIN; this prevents the July affordability race without changing the measured purchase rule. |
| Produce-mode funding | **Norxondor as written:** harvest-capable workers collect fruit with next-floor deficits preferred, and exactly one chop-capable worker mines only an iron deficit | The all-worker P coalition and 1,036/1,036 iron-deficit mining trips are the causal support for the ladder. |
| Full or non-seed load | **Champion's code** (`bank_candidates`) | §3.6 only describes nearest banking; the champion already handles walkable doors and return paths. |
| Holding a committed seed | **Norxondor as written** for the plant cell; **champion's code** for the path | `min(d(shack)+d(troll))`, range at most four, is 86.7%; stepwise movement is already exact in the champion. |
| Decide whether planting is wanted | **New with default:** the bounded orchard/one-job rules in §3 | The PICK condition and orchard size are open. A bounded commitment directly removes July's unlimited speculative PICK. |
| PICK and PLANT execution | **Champion's code** plus the explicit commitment | It already checks stock, occupancy, candidate compatibility, and persistent regeneration mechanics. |
| Mining target | **Champion's code** (`iron_candidates`) | Norxondor identifies the deficit gate, not a better endpoint order. |
| Harvest target | **Champion's code** (`fruit_candidates`) with a deterministic next-floor-deficit score tier | The reconstructed throughput formula is only 59.2%; the champion's closed-loop target code is safer, while the macro deficit preference is measured. |
| Chop target and opponent interaction | **Champion's code** (`predict_tree`, `chop_outcome`, `chop_candidates`) | Norxondor's order is only 40.7% and its denial/co-chop weights are unknown. Champion code already prices travel, growth, opponent damage, return, and capacity. |
| Banana conversion | **Norxondor as written:** PICK banana, plant by shack, chop at size 1, DROP | This is the signature mechanism, supported by 1,116 runs; only admission/coordination defaults are new. |
| Role selection and branch order | **New with default:** the explicit priority table in §3 | The observed role mix is strong, but the branch order is explicitly a guess. |
| Cross-unit assignment and stock reservation | **Champion's code** (`select`, target compatibility, PICK stock compatibility) | Norxondor's assignment algorithm is unknown; the champion's joint two-unit and deterministic greedy multi-unit selection is deployed code. |
| Movement and collisions | **Champion's code** (`next_cell`, conflict resolver, door clearing) | Paths and collision handling are micro; no port-specific alternative is justified. |
| Search/randomness | **Norxondor as written:** none; deterministic tie breaks | Runtime telemetry identifies a fast rule bot, not search. Determinism is a build gate. |
| Diagnostics | **Champion v6 instrument grammar**, emitted by the readable source itself | The panel/bed requires `MSG NARRATE v6 ...`; the compact and readable programs must be identical. |

The old plum/lemon denial bonus stays off. “Champion's chop code” refers to the champion of record,
`candidate-champion-denial-off-v6-instrument.rs` (`0e92f8fa…`), not its reported-only ancestor.

## 3. Complete controller default

### 3.1 State and constants

Persistent state is deliberately small:

```text
mode: Produce | Deforest                 # starts Produce, transition is irreversible
plant_job: at most one (unit, kind, cell, purpose)
owned_plants: planted cell -> kind        # input does not expose plant ownership
last command/goal needed by the champion's regeneration continuation
announced: bool
```

Reconcile `owned_plants` every turn: add the job cell when our previous PLANT is visible there
with the requested kind; remove a cell when no living plant of that kind remains. This memory is
how the controller counts “living own trees”—plant ownership is absent from the protocol snapshot.

Frozen defaults:

```text
LAST_TRAIN_TURN = 185                    # latest TRAIN in the reconstructed corpus
MAX_ROSTER = 5
MAX_PLANT_RADIUS = 4
PRODUCE_ORCHARD = {PLUM: 3, LEMON: 3, APPLE: 1, BANANA: 0}
MAX_GLOBAL_PLANT_JOBS = 1
MAX_BANANA_LOOPS = 1                     # one concurrent conversion, not a lifetime cap
```

The orchard counts are living own trees, not cumulative plant commands. Three plum, three lemon,
and one apple are transparent defaults anchored to the measured turn-50 means (2.4 plum, 2.9
lemon, few apples). A lost tree reopens its slot. This is intentionally a cap on speculative
PICK, not a claim that the real bot has a hard cap.

### 3.2 Ladder and D switch

Use the reconstructed floors verbatim:

```text
roster 1: 2/2/1/1
roster 2: 2/3/1/2
roster 3: 2/3/0/3
roster 4: 2/4/0/3
caps: speed 4, carry 5, harvest 2; chop 2 at rosters 1-2, chop 3 at 3-4
```

In Produce, if the floor is affordable from the pre-turn inventory, emit the componentwise
maximum affordable spec immediately. Iron is charged only on maps with iron terrain, matching the
engine. Do not train above roster five. On a TRAIN turn, no worker may emit PICK; a worker on the
shack uses the champion's door-clear move so TRAIN can apply.

For the unknown 38% switch, use one deterministic completion test rather than roster deadlines.
Estimate each resource in **full carrying trips**, not one-item trips. For missing resource `r`,
choose the best eligible funder's carry `c`, work power `h` (harvest for fruit, chop for iron),
and door-to-source distance `d`; let `wait` be the champion's predicted fruit wait:

```text
if roster >= 5:
    mode = Deforest
else if floor is not affordable:
    trips_r = ceil(missing_r / c)
    dwell_r = ceil(min(c, missing_r) / h)
    eta_r = trips_r * (2*d + dwell_r) + wait_r
    eta = ceil(sum(eta_r) / min(2, number of eligible funders))
    if turn + eta > LAST_TRAIN_TURN:
        mode = Deforest
```

Omit iron from both affordability and ETA on maps without iron terrain, matching the engine's
payment rule. A required resource with no source contributes the sentinel ETA and therefore causes
the switch. A floor affordable on turn 185 still trains; the switch test runs only if it is not
affordable. This is a frozen, falsifiable stand-in for the unknown “training is now hopeless” test:
it preserves the measured final training frontier and makes the two-funder mechanism part of the
same decision. There is no T mode and no second state letter.

### 3.3 Planting and PICK admission

Plant jobs are chosen before unit candidates and persist until completed or invalidated. The cell
is a free walkable cell on our half, within graph distance four of our shack, minimizing:

```text
(distance_from_shack + distance_from_unit, distance_from_shack, cell)
```

Define our half as cells whose graph distance to our shack is no greater than their distance to the
opponent shack. This supplies deterministic tie breaks while preserving the measured primary rule. A job is
invalidated if its unit dies, its cell becomes occupied by a plant, the path disappears, the seed
disappears, or the mode makes its purpose illegal.

Produce orchard admission:

1. No BANANA PICK or PLANT before the second troll exists (owner constraint).
2. At most one global plant job exists.
3. The ladder wins every conflict: fruit of a kind still below the next-floor bill is banked, never
   claimed by an orchard job. Otherwise, if a worker already carries plum/lemon/apple and that kind
   is below its orchard count, use it; PICK only while adjacent to the shack and only after a free
   target cell has been selected.
4. Choose the kind with largest `(target alive count - current alive count)`; ties are LEMON,
   PLUM, APPLE. This reproduces the measured lemon-first opening and makes the rule inspectable.
5. Do not create a plant job on a TRAIN turn. In Produce, carried fruit not covered by a job is
   banked for the ladder.

Deforest banana admission:

1. One chop-capable worker with empty cargo may own the single global conversion job.
2. BANANA must be present in the shack, a valid planting cell must already be reserved, and no
   TRAIN is possible because D is irreversible.
3. PICK -> PLANT -> CHOP the owned size-1 banana until it falls -> bank the wood. Do not retarget
   that worker while the job remains valid.
4. Prefer chop-power 3, then shortest distance to the reserved cell, then lowest unit id. If no
   chop-3 unit exists, chop-power 2/1 may convert; the exact champion chop-outcome code determines
   whether the remaining turns suffice.

Deforest does not harvest bananas and Produce excludes them from funding. Banana-loop fuel is
therefore the starting/deposited shack stock only, intentionally bounding this default near the
reconstruction's roughly six conversions per game rather than creating another harvesting branch.

There is no apple-late rule, no unlimited “fewest kind” planting, and no seed PICK based only on
being at the shack. Those behaviours are not recovered well enough to justify another branch.

### 3.4 Per-turn priority

After TRAIN and the single plant job are decided, build candidates in sorted unit-id order. The
priority is a policy gate; target selection inside a gate stays champion code.

```text
1. if carrying a job seed / working its planted banana: continue that plant job
2. else if load is full: bank
3. else if mode == Deforest:
       banana job owner continues; otherwise best champion CHOP
4. else (Produce):
       if selected orchard job owner: PICK/PLANT
       elif this is the one selected iron miner and iron is below next floor: MINE
       elif harvest_power > 0: HARVEST
            - next-floor-deficit kinds get the high score tier
            - other plum/lemon/apple fruit remains eligible at the normal tier
            - banana is excluded from Produce funding
       else: best champion CHOP
5. if the gated list has no productive candidate: champion bank/CHOP/WAIT fallback in that order
```

Partial loads continue the standing HARVEST or MINE when productive: the on-resource candidate's
score wins. Bank a partial load only when adjacent to the shack or when the gated list has no
productive candidate. This permits carry-3 workers to fill in two or three work turns, matching the
91% full-load drop fact, without letting mixed seed/cargo states acquire a new planting goal.
The one miner is the eligible unit with lowest champion travel ETA to iron, then lowest id. A
chop-only later worker may mine; harvest-capable workers otherwise form the funding coalition.

The champion selector then enforces distinct targets and available PICK stock, and its resolver
repairs move conflicts. All maps/sets participating in ties are ordered collections; every numeric
tie ends in cell then unit id. No randomness, wall clock, or hash iteration may affect output.

### 3.5 Diagnostics

Every turn begins with one `MSG` carrying the v6 grammar. Preserve all required fields so existing
decoders accept it. Reuse the champion instrument's target/collision fields; keep inapplicable
goal-persistence counters at zero. Add no unregistered tokens. The first line may include the
banner exactly as the v6 champion does. Because this code lives in `readable/norxondor-port.rs`
before compaction, compacted-equals-readable is a real identity check rather than an instrumentation
rewrite.

## 4. Defaults for every reconstruction §5 open rule

| §5 gap | Frozen default |
|---|---|

| 2. Plant kind and orchard size | Living own targets P=3, L=3, A=1, B=0 in Produce; largest shortfall with L/P/A tie order; banana only through one D conversion job. |
| 3. D trigger, T, second letter | Irreversible D at roster five or full-carry-trip projected next-floor completion after turn 185. Omit T and the second letter completely. |
| 4. Coordination | Champion pair search for two units and deterministic greedy reservation above two; one global plant job and one explicit miner. |
| 5. Opponent modelling | Champion's existing opponent-on-tree damage prediction only. No reactive planting stop, raid bonus, or co-chop weight. |
| 6. Two-troll fallback | The same projected-completion rule: stay Produce while the next floor can finish by 185; otherwise enter D and use champion chop plus one banana loop. No special classifier. |
| 7. Search | None. Evaluate finite candidate lists and deterministic BFS only. |
| 8. Closed-loop drift | Remove learned intent/target layers; bound seed commitments; make mode and funding explicit; judge only complete games on the frozen field panel. |

## 5. Build and verification plan

### File layout and size

The submission remains one Rust file, with readable sections in this order:

1. champion I/O, types, rules, parser, and BFS;
2. champion candidate, bank, fruit, iron, tree prediction/chop, selection, and collision code;
3. new `Mode`, `PlantPurpose`, and `PlantJob` records;
4. new ladder/floor, completion ETA, orchard, banana, and per-unit gate functions;
5. v6 narration in the readable source;
6. `Bot::commands` and `main`.

Start from `readable/denial-off-champion.rs`, whose current compaction is 47,668 bytes, and copy
only the necessary v6 narration hooks from the live instrument. Budget at most 22,000 compacted
bytes for state/macro/diagnostics, targeting **under 70,000 UTF-16 units** and leaving more than
30,000 below the 100,000 limit. Measure the actual UTF-16 code-unit count of the compact output;
do not use byte count as a surrogate if non-ASCII text is introduced. Source comments may make the
readable file larger; only the platform artifact is constrained, and the artifact will stay ASCII.

Outputs:

- `readable/norxondor-port.rs`
- `cgauto/submissions/candidate-norxondor-port-v1.rs`
- `cgauto/submissions/candidate-norxondor-port-v1.rs.sha256`

Use `cgauto/compact_rust_source.py`; append the one trailing newline expected by the canonical
submission format before hashing. The sidecar records the final artifact SHA-256.

### Runtime

There is no search. The costly operations are the champion's bounded tree simulation and BFS over
the small board for at most five workers. The new logic adds one floor-ETA pass and one plant-cell
scan. Cache per-turn distance maps where the copied layout permits it; do not change target results
merely for caching.

Hard gate: every turn below 50 ms. Engineering gate: p99 below 10 ms over the 24-map legality run,
with maximum reported. The champion is roughly 1 ms, so this leaves a fivefold safety margin before
the platform limit.

### Validity gates, in order

1. `rustc --edition=2021 -O` compiles the compact artifact.
2. UTF-16 length <100,000; deterministic source has no RNG/time/hash-order dependence.
3. Compact the readable file twice; both bytes and SHA match the candidate and sidecar.
4. The P-0 bed plays all 34 fixtures to completion with v6 diagnostics parsed and zero telemetry or
   illegal-command failures.
5. Play the 24 real maps through the local referee, both seats where the harness supports it; zero
   illegal commands and report per-turn timing.
6. Run targeted mechanics tests: turn-1 TRAIN, PICK-vs-TRAIN transaction, roster-five D, roster-3
   empty-shack turn-106 sources-at-distance-3 stays Produce, roster-4 equivalent turn-138 switches
   D, a carry-3/harvest-1 worker with one fruit continues HARVEST, no banana before troll two, one
   plant job, occupied-cell cancellation, iron-free ETA omission, iron-only-when-deficit, banana
   conversion, deterministic replay, and collision repair.
7. Only after these gates, hand the artifact SHA to claude_1 for byte-identity reproduction and the
   frozen same-local-field rung 1. No direct duel is the selector, no network call is made here,
   and only local_claude_1 has Arena authority.

## Review questions

The required reviewer should decide only whether this design has a hole, especially:

1. Is the projected-completion `turn + eta > 185` default sufficiently specified and consistent
   with the exact same-turn TRAIN rule?
2. Is one bounded P orchard job plus one D banana conversion job enough to prevent the July PICK
   loop without deleting the signature behaviour?
3. Does any required v6 field need nonzero semantics beyond the champion target/collision fields?

If those are closed, build exactly this version. Parameter tuning waits for the single
pre-registered loss-read loop after rung 1; the build itself gets no hidden variants.
