# Task-market orchard scheduling — mechanism result, 2026-07-19

## Verdict

**Reject before fresh discovery and seal seeds 2380--2499.** The terminal-value-rate mother task
is broad, deterministic, and implemented cleanly, but returning the starter to the resident's
inner job market destroys a much more valuable recurring APPLE stream. Do not tune its multiplier
or add a turn, score, opponent, crop, or harvest-count threshold.

The independent orchard replication left exactly one exception open: integrate orchard work into
a global task market. This experiment executed that exception. No further standalone orchard
release controller is eligible unless a new complete scheduler first demonstrates an independently
valuable alternative role.

## Causal decomposition

In the 99 active cells:

- the resident harvests 100.848 own-crop apples per game; the candidate harvests 19.121, a loss of
  81.727 bankable points;
- the candidate gains 4.687 banked wood, worth 18.747 terminal points;
- own score still falls 65.111, matching the lost APPLE stream after the modest wood recovery;
- opponent score falls only 3.758;
- candidate APPLE planting rises 1.475 per game, but this extra conversion does not repay the
  missing direct fruit; and
- terminal APPLE-tree count is almost unchanged (-0.162), so the loss is scheduling throughput,
  not asset destruction.

Median actual setup begins on turn 21 and seed repayment completes on turn 35. The damage therefore
spans most of the game; it is not a late cashout artifact.

## Interpretation at four levels

### Command level

The candidate correctly offers and selects mother work, but moving away makes later mother offers
less competitive because return travel enters the immediate score. This creates path-dependent
hysteresis: one locally preferred wood job can turn a nearby recurring option into a distant one.

### Queueing level

A water-door APPLE mother produces roughly as fast as a harvest-1 starter can service it with
alternating HARVEST and DROP. The worker is not waiting idly. Once it leaves, backlog cannot be
caught up before the fruit cap without sacrificing future arrivals.

### Economic level

The dimensional `250 * apples / turns` score is locally consistent with `1000 * wood / turns`, but
the resident's other job scores are optimistic heuristics rather than realized closed-loop value.
The market buys 18.7 wood points while forfeiting about 81.7 fruit points per active game.

### Architectural level

The catastrophic rich-opponent orchard losses are a capacity problem, not a bad idle reservation.
The exact resident has one high-throughput producer and only one remaining generalist. Releasing
the producer does not create a strong denial/production role; it exposes the weakness of the inner
continuation. An improvement must first supply a complete scheduler whose alternative work
compounds enough to beat the orchard stream.

## Closure and next move

Close universal seed release, terminal-value-rate task-market release, multiplier catalogs, and
standalone orchard thresholds. Keep secure orchard unchanged in the exact resident.

Advance a **bundle-valued productive scheduler representation audit**. The new representation must
price persistent multi-action jobs and continuation/location value—fell-and-bank, harvest-and-bank,
seed-to-crop conversion, and naturally funded training—inside a complete multi-worker scheduler.
Before optimizing a deployable policy, require a hindsight oracle over that fixed job grammar to
show robust value versus the resident across opponents. This is structurally different from the
closed one-MOVE residual, fixed Gold grammar, static-role rich proxies, and teacher-state imitation.
