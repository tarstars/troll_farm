# Species-separated renewable supply — frozen protocol, 2026-07-19

## Hypothesis

The complete adaptive farm produces high own score but also maintains a public BANANA lineage.
Against adaptive Gold, fruit captured from one of our BANANA crops can be replanted and compound
through many opponent generations. The exact resident instead uses a private APPLE orchard and
exposes zero fruit on our BANANA crops. Replacing the productive farm's commodity species may
preserve renewable throughput while removing the rival's reproductive rebootstrap path.

## Canonical intervention

Use `GoldElite::adaptive()` unchanged except for one farm-species parameter. The control remains
BANANA. The candidate uses **PLUM** for every post-funding farm operation:

- protected seed-reserve membership;
- carried-seed PLANT command;
- bank PICK command;
- ripe seed-tree harvest target; and
- the farm-specific fell threshold.

Training costs, early deficit harvesting, workforce, roles, density split, stagger, hold, farm
radius/cap, water preference, reserve count, fell thresholds, target order, anti-idle behavior, and
liquidation remain byte-equivalent in logic.

PLUM is fixed before outcomes because PLUM and LEMON have identical simulated growth and health;
PLUM is their deterministic lowest item index. APPLE is excluded a priori because its size-four
health is 20 versus 12 for PLUM and 6 for BANANA. Do not run a species catalog or switch species
after seeing results.

## Profiles and blocks

Every cell contains:

- exact resident;
- unchanged adaptive-density BANANA farm; and
- adaptive-density PLUM farm.

Mechanism/integrity uses consumed seeds 0--29, both seats, exact adaptive Gold and a byte-identical
20-worker repeat. If admissible, discovery uses fresh seeds 2260--2319, both seats, all eight fixed
opponents. Confirmation seeds 2320--2379 remain sealed unless discovery passes.

## Required telemetry and integrity

Record successful own and opponent plants by species, causal crop ownership, wood origin, command
PLANT species, terminal plant species, scores, wood, workers, and terminal turn.

Require a complete grid, complete games, byte-identical integrity repeat, at least 95% wood
provenance, unchanged control profiles, zero non-PLUM candidate PLANT commands after funding, zero
candidate BANANA crop births, and at least 300 successful candidate PLUM plants in the 60-game
mechanism panel. A code/inference violation repairs the implementation rather than rejecting the
hypothesis.

## Frozen mechanism admissibility

Open fresh discovery only if, against adaptive Gold on consumed maps:

- candidate own-score delta versus BANANA farm is at least -60;
- candidate opponent-score delta versus BANANA farm is at most -15;
- candidate opponent successful-plant delta versus BANANA farm is at most -2; and
- candidate has zero opponent-harvested BANANA fruit originating from candidate crops.

This is a coarse causal screen, not a candidate qualification result.

## Frozen discovery gate

All primary comparisons are paired PLUM candidate minus exact resident. Discovery passes only if:

- overall mean margin delta at least +3.0;
- 10% trimmed mean margin delta nonnegative;
- mean own-score delta at least +25.0;
- positive mean margin on at least five of eight opponent families;
- worst family no worse than -12.0; and
- adaptive-Gold mean margin delta nonnegative.

The mechanism comparison versus the BANANA farm must also retain adaptive-Gold opponent-score
suppression of at least 20, planting suppression of at least 3, and own-score loss no worse than
40.

Failure seals confirmation and closes static commodity-species substitution without testing
LEMON or APPLE.

## Confirmation and transfer

Confirmation must retain nonnegative 10% trimmed margin, at least +2 mean margin versus resident,
at least +20 own score, five positive families, worst family at least -12, nonnegative adaptive
margin, and the same directional mechanism gates with opponent score at most -15 and planting at
most -2 versus BANANA farm.

Passing confirmation authorizes source-size and arena-transfer audits only. It does not authorize
automatic submission or any mutation of the exact arena resident.
