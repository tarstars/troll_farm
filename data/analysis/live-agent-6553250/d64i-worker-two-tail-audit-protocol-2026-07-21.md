# D64i worker-two tail audit protocol (2026-07-21)

## Question

Why does exact D40 fail to create worker two in the four symmetric tasks that invalidate D64a's
universal workforce-safety gate?

D64i is a consumed-task mechanical audit required by D64a's invalid branch. It cannot repair D64,
read its quarantined value as promotion evidence, tune a policy, construct a candidate, or perform
a platform action.

## Frozen cohort

Replay exact D40 on:

- targets: seeds 9,830,002 and 9,830,014, both seats, opponent `resident` (four tasks); and
- matched successful-path controls: the same seeds and seats, opponent `gold_adaptive` (four
  tasks).

These task outcomes are already consumed in D64a. Controls are selected by opponent label before
this audit's telemetry exists, not by a new score scan. Run the eight traces twice and require byte
identity. Target traces continue to terminal; controls may stop immediately after successful
creation of worker two.

## Frozen telemetry

At every exact D40 decision through the audit endpoint record:

- turn, decision stage, worker count, persistent TRAIN goal, D40 selection branch, chosen action
  plane, job kind, job fruit species, and whether the step creates worker two;
- current producer TRAIN bill (with the existing no-IRON-terrain waiver), deposited inventory,
  total carried inventory, ripe board fruit, plant count, and fruit count by species;
- producer-bill deficit from (a) bank only, (b) bank plus carried stock, and (c) bank plus carried
  plus currently ripe board fruit;
- predicted deposits of active jobs, shack occupancy, whether the current worker occupies the
  shack, live own-provenance crop count, and exact state/action hashes; and
- terminal mechanical counters and final inventory for target tasks.

No alternative command is executed. D64i is observation of exact D40 only.

## Frozen blocker classification

For each target use the first applicable class:

1. `transaction_or_shack`: the deposited bill is affordable at a decision with a clear shack but
   worker two is not created at the next execution opportunity;
2. `deposit_materialization`: bank plus carried stock covers the bill at least once, while bank
   alone never covers it;
3. `ripe_acquisition`: bank plus carried plus ripe board fruit covers the bill at least once, while
   bank plus carried never covers it;
4. `source_availability`: even bank plus carried plus ripe board fruit never covers the bill; or
5. `unexplained`: none of the above applies.

Also report per-resource minimum deficit at all three stock layers, selected job/branch counts,
species planted/ripe exposure, and the last turn each missing resource is available. Controls must
demonstrate the telemetry can observe successful worker-two creation on the same maps.

## Decision rule

- If all targets share `ripe_acquisition`, freeze one prospective invariant that protects a ripe
  missing TRAIN currency from renewable consumption until it is deposited.
- If all share `deposit_materialization`, freeze one bill-preserving bank/deposit lease.
- If all share `source_availability`, repair source access/creation for the exact missing resource;
  do not alter transaction logic.
- If any target is `transaction_or_shack`, repair only TRAIN execution/evacuation and revalidate
  transaction parity.
- Mixed or unexplained causes require a broader state-machine repair protocol; no single patch may
  be inferred.

Any repair must use fresh maps for safety/value. D64 seeds may never become selection evidence.
