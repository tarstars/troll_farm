# D36 resident-anchored constrained joint oracle — protocol (2026-07-21)

## Question

D35d validates factorized persistent jobs, provenance-aware targets, and repeated job-boundary
decisions, but its productive-farm trajectory leaves opponent score +76.118 above resident.  D36
tests the required architecture pivot: **can the same complete joint option interface add material
production when every intervention begins from and returns to the exact suppressive resident?**

This is a hindsight representation upper bound.  Exact terminal opponent rollouts are unavailable
to a submission, so even a pass authorizes a learning dataset and confirmation only—not a candidate
or Arena action.

## Fresh substrate

- D33 authoritative official maps, exact referee, and stall semantics.
- Exact stable `SecureOrchardBot` resident, cloned with all internal memory at every root.
- Same eight mechanism opponents, warmed from exact actual history.
- D35c no-TRAIN factorized generic and provenance-aware target catalogs, collision rules, active-job
  executor, banking/completion semantics, creator attribution, and target bounds unchanged.
- Development seeds: **9,500,000--9,500,007**, both seats.
- Sealed confirmation seeds: **9,500,008--9,500,019**.
- One start per task: the first exact resident state with two workers at or after turn 50.  A task
  manifest must retain games that never become eligible.

This does not reopen the closed resident-local one-job grammar.  That experiment redirected one
idle/MOVE unit into BANK/FELL/HARVEST once.  D36 allocates both workers jointly, includes RENEW and
provenance targets, executes complete collision-aware bundles, values closed-loop terminal state,
and repeats at exact completion boundaries.

## Frozen terminal constraint and selection

Let `R` be the uninterrupted resident outcome for the exact seed/seat/opponent task.  An option at
any epoch is **feasible** only when its terminal opponent score is at most
`R.opponent_score + 65`.  Control is feasible at epoch zero.  Because every selected option is a
full terminal continuation, control at the next reached epoch reproduces the previously selected
feasible continuation; lack of a feasible option is therefore an integrity failure.

Among feasible control plus D35c options, select deterministically by:

1. maximum terminal own score;
2. lower terminal opponent score;
3. control over a non-control exact tie;
4. fewer overridden commands; and
5. lexicographic plan key.

The ceiling is inherited unchanged from D35b--D35d.  It is not tuned on D36.  Maximizing own score
inside that ceiling is the prospective dual-economy question: whether the resident trajectory has
at least 68 points of safe production headroom.

## Frozen repeated controller

At each live epoch enumerate control and the unchanged catalog, roll each option to terminal with
the exact cloned resident/opponent, choose by the constrained ordering, and either:

- stop permanently on control; or
- execute the selected whole bundle on the live branch, returning to the resident before its first
  post-completion command, then replan.

Preserve exact resident memory, opponent history, stall counter, provenance, and committed command
history.  Permit at most four non-control epochs and start no epoch after turn 220.  No TRAIN goal
is available.  Record the first-root constrained one-shot oracle, the unconstrained terminal-margin
choice from the same option matrix, and uninterrupted resident control.

## Integrity gate

Before value analysis require all of:

1. all 128 development tasks in the manifest and at least 100 eligible starts;
2. byte-identical one-seed rows, manifests, selected epochs, and terminal hashes;
3. exact resident controls and exact first-root option identities;
4. zero attribution/history mismatches, duplicate keys/options, collisions, illegal commands,
   TRAIN successes, hypothetical post-completion commands, or branches above three own workers;
5. selected execution status/override/end-turn equality with its recorded rollout and exact terminal
   replay equality;
6. every next epoch starts at the prior bundle's exact completion state and strictly advances unless
   terminal;
7. every selected choice equals the frozen constrained maximum and satisfies the task ceiling;
8. at least 5,000 terminal option rollouts; and
9. at least 25 tasks with two or more non-control epochs.

## Frozen development gate

The resident-anchored representation passes only if all hold:

1. a non-control option is selected in at least 25% of eligible tasks and at least two execute in
   at least 15%;
2. repeated seed-clustered own-score gain over uninterrupted resident is at least **+68**;
3. repeated opponent-score excess over resident is at most **+65**;
4. repeated margin gain over resident is at least **+25**;
5. all eight opponent-family mean own-score gains are nonnegative and at least six are +50 or more;
6. all eight opponent-family mean margin gains are nonnegative and at least six are +15 or more;
7. repetition adds at least +5 own score versus the paired constrained one-shot oracle without
   lowering mean margin;
8. selected provenance-specific targets span at least four opponent families and two epochs; and
9. catastrophe frequency and negative-margin mass do not exceed uninterrupted resident or the
   paired constrained one-shot oracle.

Pass freezes unchanged confirmation and exports epoch-state/option labels for a constraint-aware
scheduler learner.  Any failure leaves confirmation sealed and closes resident overlays: the next
architecture must be a genuinely complete learned controller trained and evaluated closed-loop,
not another target, threshold, or wrapper around the resident.
