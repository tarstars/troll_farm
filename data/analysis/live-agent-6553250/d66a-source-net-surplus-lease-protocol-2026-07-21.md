# D66a source net-surplus lease protocol (2026-07-21)

## Question

Can one atomic lease from deposited seed through **two deposited source fruits** turn D65's
source creation into reliable worker-two capitalization without changing its trigger or D40's
remaining policy?

D65i showed that the failed LEMON roots deposit zero, while successful PLUM roots deposit before
worker two. One fruit merely repays the seed removed from bank; exactly two deposited fruits create
the first +1 net bill progress. D66a tests that mechanism, not source count or placement.

## Frozen intervention

Preserve D65's eligibility test, largest-deficit species choice and tie order, one bootstrap per
species, player-favored cell, and every non-source D40 action. Replace only the source transaction:

1. move to the own-shack door and PICK exactly one deposited fruit;
2. move to the unchanged selected empty cell and PLANT it;
3. remain assigned to that exact root, issuing WAIT while it has no fruit;
4. HARVEST only that root until the existing capacity-two worker carries exactly two fruits;
5. move to the own-shack door and DROP both fruits; and
6. return at the next unchanged D40 Train boundary.

The lease never renews, switches target, changes worker stats, or issues a second source of the same
species. If the root disappears before two fruits are acquired, the job invalidates and D40 resumes;
there is no retry. The implementation is inert unless an experiment explicitly calls the new lease
method. Existing D40 and D65 return-immediately behavior must retain exact parity.

## Consumed recovery gate

Run D40 control and `source_surplus_lease` on both seats of seeds 9,830,002 and 9,830,014 against
`resident`. Require:

- exact D40 reproduction of all four historical one-worker terminal/action/state hashes;
- worker two in all four lease tasks;
- every issued lease executes one PICK, one PLANT, two HARVESTs, and one two-fruit DROP;
- every task activates the D64i missing species and creates at least one crop;
- zero source-job, illegal-command, provenance, deposit-prediction, reward, action-accounting,
  finite-state, or worker-cap failures; and
- no lease begins after worker two.

Failure closes this exact atomic lease before fresh value. Do not tune wait duration, harvest count,
target cell, species order, or retry behavior on consumed outcomes.

## Conditional fresh repeated gate

Only after the consumed gate passes, open the still-unused official seeds 9,831,000--9,831,031,
both seats, all eight D40 opponents, and both policies: 512 tasks per policy, 1,024 rows. Seeds
9,831,000--9,831,015 are development and 9,831,016--9,831,031 validation. Repeat with 20 threads
and require byte identity.

Reuse D65's integrity, inactive exact-parity, minimum eight/four-map/both-block/two-family activity,
100% worker-two and crop, maximum-three-worker, no-late-lease, catastrophic non-increase, overall
nonnegative margin / own >= -2 / opponent <= +2, validation nonnegative margin, family >= -3,
and exact one-worker-tail +20 gates. Report lease duration and WAIT/HARVEST/DROP commands explicitly.

## Decision

- Full pass validates source capitalization as a D40 substrate repair, not a submission candidate.
- Consumed failure closes the fixed two-fruit lease and moves to a broader capitalization state
  machine; fresh seeds remain unopened.
- Fresh safety/value failure rejects the repair without tuning on those outcomes.
