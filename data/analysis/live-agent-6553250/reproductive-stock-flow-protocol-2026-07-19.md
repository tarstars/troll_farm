# Reproductive stock-flow and extinction boundary — frozen protocol, 2026-07-19

## Question

Both one-chopper and capacity-separated pre-fruit denial remove roughly eleven to fourteen rival
crops per adaptive-Gold game but prevent only one to two net plantings. Does the exact resident
create an observable banana-lineage extinction boundary—low bank/carry seed stock plus no parallel
crop supply—that the productive farm policies do not? If no such state exists with material
breadth and duration, direct crop denial closes at the architectural level.

## Fixed diagnostic

Use consumed seeds 0--29, both seats, against exact adaptive Gold. Compare three unchanged command
streams:

- exact resident;
- exact `lean_m2c2h0k2` farm; and
- existing `GoldElite::adaptive()` renewable architecture.

This is a 180-game observational diagnostic and cannot qualify a candidate. Run the same release
executable twice with 20 workers and require byte identity. Do not open fresh seeds, confirmation
blocks, official games, or the arena.

Track exact causal crop provenance plus, on every observed turn after 100:

- opponent banked and carried BANANA seeds;
- fruit currently stored on opponent-owned BANANA crops;
- count of opponent-owned BANANA crops, including unfruited crops;
- zero immediate-seed turns (`bank + carry == 0`);
- zero owned-seed turns (`bank + carry + own-crop fruit == 0`);
- lineage-absent turns (zero owned seed and zero opponent banana crops);
- low-redundancy turns (at most one immediate seed and at most one crop); and
- longest consecutive zero-owned-seed streak.

At the end of turns 100, 150, 200, 250, and 300, snapshot opponent score, wood, workers,
cumulative plants, bank/carry seeds, crop count/fruit, natural banana fruit, and fruit on our
crops. Also record successful plantings in phases 1--100, 101--150, 151--200, 201--250, and
251--300 and actual post-100 exposure through the corrected terminal condition.

## Integrity

Require the exact 30-seed/both-seat/three-profile grid, all games complete, byte-identical repeat,
at least 99% fruit provenance and 95% wood provenance, monotone cumulative checkpoint plant
counts, and phase counts summing to complete totals. A checkpoint after an early terminal is
explicitly absent rather than zero-filled evidence.

## Frozen classification

Compute all turn rates with actual post-100 exposure as denominator.

A material deployable **seed-depletion boundary** exists only if the resident simultaneously has:

- zero-owned-seed rate at least 15%;
- at least 30/60 games with a zero-owned-seed streak of five turns or more; and
- zero-owned-seed rate at least 10 percentage points above both farm profiles.

A stronger **lineage-absence boundary** exists only if the resident has lineage absent on at least
5% of exposed turns, at least 20/60 games contain one or more such turns, and its rate is at least
3 percentage points above both farms.

Report low-redundancy rate, checkpoint stock distributions, phase-specific planting rates, and
terminal exposure regardless of classification. Do not select a turn, stock threshold, or crop
kind from the result.

## Decision

- If either boundary passes, the next experiment may use only that already frozen observable
  state to switch a complete scheduler; individual first-fruit target timing remains closed.
- If both fail, close direct reproductive denial entirely and prioritize productive score racing,
  terminal cashout/collapse, and field-calibrated whole-policy search.
