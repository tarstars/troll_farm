# D90 lineage and ATTACK diagnosis (2026-07-21)

## D90a: source-separated harvest ablation

On the consumed D89 discovery maps, compare the exact full factory with a controller that excludes
ordinary harvested-fruit descendants from the starter's harvest targets. A descendant may be used
only if every bank-seeded source has disappeared and that descendant has become the protected
emergency reserve. Opening, bootstrap, roles, placement, and all other decisions are identical.

The source boundary changes only 19/256 tasks. D89 already obtains 9,876/10,729 harvests from
bank-seeded crops; the ablation reduces conversion-source harvests only from 853 to 780. Versus
resident, mean margin moves from `+79.441` to `+78.008`, own score from `+162.305` to `+160.680`,
and opponent score only from `+82.863` to `+82.672`. The p10 (`-72`), worst (`-235`), catastrophe
count (11), negative-margin mass (3,112), and Gold-adaptive mean (`-6.938`) do not improve.

Reject source eligibility as the D89 tail repair. It remains a faithful implementation detail for
a future controller, but D89 already approximates the observed lineage split closely enough that
this factor cannot explain its competitive leak.

## D90b: yaichi `ATTACK` task archaeology

The remaining D88 macro state is not a crop-attack policy. Across all 35 open yaichi games it
appears in 14 games, 343 unit-turns, and 94 contiguous unit episodes. Every target is exactly one
orthogonal step from the opponent shack. The task moves an empty worker onto an opponent bank door.

All episode starts have zero carried items. The live-plant count at entry has median zero and range
zero to one; preceding states are `CHOP_TRAVEL` in 63 episodes and `DROP` in 31. Work is balanced
between starter and trained worker (170 and 173 unit-turns). Entry turn has median 174.5 and range
98--300. Therefore `ATTACK` is a resource-exhaustion/bank-blockade fallback after ordinary crop work
has disappeared, not the simultaneous rival-crop suppression missing from D89's productive phase.

Do not graft a shack-door rush onto the live factory as a D89 repair. It is causally downstream of
plant exhaustion and would be inactive in almost every sustained D89 trajectory.

## Next discriminator

D89's large positive mean and concentrated negative tail now call for strategy selection, not a
smaller source loop or an unrelated blockade. Record only state visible at the exact resident-exact
worker-two activation boundary: map/bank state, opponent workforce and stats, inventories, scores,
plant/fruit state, and opponent crop births already observed. Test whether a shallow, blocked-map
selector can abstain on D89 regressions while retaining its production gains. Any selector must be
chosen on consumed D89 maps and evaluated once on new official maps; opponent names are forbidden.
