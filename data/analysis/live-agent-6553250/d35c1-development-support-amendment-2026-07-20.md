# D35c.1 development-support amendment (2026-07-20)

## Trigger

The final byte-repeat on seed 9,300,000 produced 32 roots, 484 generic bundles,
and 313 competitive extension bundles.  Attribution, control, command, and
manifest integrity all pass.  Before computing either hindsight oracle or
inspecting any aggregate terminal score/delta, I inspected only row count,
catalog count, root exposure flags, attribution failures, direct-command
failures, and maximum worker count.

The preregistered ten-seed block projects to roughly 7,970 total non-control and
3,130 competitive bundles, below D35c's deliberately frozen 10,000/5,000
support floors.  Failing a target representation because the preregistered
sample cannot possibly reach its own evidence floor would not test the stated
hypothesis.

## Amendment

Development now consists of two fresh, disjoint ten-seed blocks:

- 9,300,000--9,300,009; and
- 9,300,040--9,300,049.

The original sealed confirmation block 9,300,010--9,300,029 remains untouched
and unchanged.  All opponents, seats, roots, provenance rules, catalogs,
capacities, tie-breaking, integrity floors, value gates, and decision rules are
unchanged.  The seed remains the primary statistical unit, now with 20
development seeds.

The two development TSV blocks will be concatenated only after independent grid
validation and sorted by seed/seat/opponent/checkpoint/option.  No threshold may
be changed if the realized 20-seed support still fails.
