# Secure-orchard conversion audit — frozen protocol, 2026-07-19

## Question

Do rare sustained secure-orchard activations explain the fresh exact-resident catastrophic
fruit-hoard tail, and is the failure specifically continued starter reservation after the mother
has already replaced its seed?

This is an observational, read-only diagnostic on an already consumed arena corpus. It cannot
estimate a counterfactual score and cannot qualify a candidate by itself.

## Fixed corpus and instrumentation

- Use exactly the 80 game ids already frozen in
  `recent-resident-restore-field-census-2026-07-19.json` (agent `6560353`).
- Fetch only `gameResult/findByGameId`; create no controlled game and make no arena write.
- Generate a stderr-only probe from the exact 62,725-byte resident source. The probe records the
  outer wrapper's `HARVEST` override and must preserve resident stdout on every reconstructed
  state stream.
- Reconstruct official turn states and compare the exact resident's actions with recorded actions.
  Count a probe event only before the first resident/recorded mismatch; retain full-stream parity
  separately.

## Measurements

For every game report activation, admissible forced-harvest count, first and last force turn,
mother cell, final fruit and wood, successful plants, opponent crops and crop wood, score margin,
and outcome cohort. Report the complete distribution rather than choosing a count threshold from
outcomes.

Separate three economic states without fitted constants:

1. no orchard harvest;
2. the first successful mother harvest, which replaces the apple seed spent to create the mother;
3. every later forced harvest, which continues exclusive starter reservation after seed
   replacement.

Join the prior behavior-neutral evidence: two deterministic local sustained activations and the
ten-game controlled top-five probe. These are supporting mechanism observations, not additional
arena outcomes.

## Integrity and interpretation rules

Require probe/resident stdout equality in all decoded games, zero unknown diff updates, no fetch
failure, and at least 60/80 full resident command reproductions. If a catastrophic game's first
force precedes a later source-reproduction mismatch, only its exact prefix is admissible.

The audit supports a candidate experiment only if both fresh fruit-hoard catastrophes—game
`896294348` against daaskare and game `896294247` against m4l0s4n—contain an admissible sustained
orchard sequence and the events explain their recorded apple accumulation. Sustained means at
least one further forced harvest after the first forced apple has been successfully banked; the
full event-count distribution is reported and no larger cutoff is selected from outcomes.
The candidate, if supported, is frozen as **seed-replacement release**: after the first forced
mother fruit is successfully banked, stop exclusive orchard reservation and return the starter
and mother to the unchanged inner scheduler. There is no turn, score, opponent, crop-count, or
harvest-count coefficient. A separate local discovery/confirmation protocol is required before
running it.

If the fruit-hoard losses are not exact orchard activations, close this branch. Do not reinterpret
ordinary inner idle harvests, add a generic fruit cap, or remove secure-orchard activation
wholesale.
