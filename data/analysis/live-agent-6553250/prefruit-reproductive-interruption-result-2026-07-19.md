# Pre-fruit reproductive interruption — result, 2026-07-19

## Verdict

**Reject and close the two-worker pre-fruit controller without tuning.** It proves that a single
farm chopper can destroy many attributed crops before first fruit, but the opponent replaces them
from redundant seed stock. The reproductive effect is far too small to repay displaced private
production. Confirmation seeds 1960--2019 remain sealed.

## Integrity

The consumed 0--29 panel passes every frozen implementation gate:

- complete 1,440-row, three-profile grid;
- byte-identical repeat with 20 workers;
- all games complete and at least 99.9% assigned chopped-wood provenance;
- zero exact-farm shadow mismatches; and
- all 74 inactive candidate cells exactly equal the farm on every outcome/provenance field.

The controller therefore opened the unchanged fresh discovery block 1900--1959. The discovery
grid contains 960 scenarios per profile, eight opponents, and both seats.

## Mechanism result

The intervention is neither rare nor mistimed:

- 807/960 cells activate; adaptive Gold activates in 119/120;
- 10,888 targets are selected;
- 10,643 selected targets disappear before fruit and 245 are observed fruiting; and
- against adaptive Gold, 1,678 targets disappear before fruit versus 31 fruiting.

Yet against adaptive Gold, candidate minus farm is only:

| Mechanism/outcome | Delta | Frozen requirement |
|---|---:|---:|
| Opponent successful plants | -2.092 | at most -10 |
| Opponent self-crop wood | -13.983 | at most -20 |
| Opponent score | -36.992 | at most -50 |
| Own score | -118.683 | at least -40 |
| Margin | -81.692 | — |

Roughly fourteen selected crops disappear per adaptive game, but net planting falls by only two.
The crop is not the lineage: once the rival holds redundant banana seeds or parallel crops, each
pre-fruit kill mostly induces replacement. The chopper still abandons the private wood loop, so
the opportunity cost compounds on our side while the rival's lineage survives.

## Architectural consequence

Closed:

- unconditional pre-fruit diversion by the productive farm's only chopper;
- tuning first-fruit, travel, chop, or target-order thresholds on seeds 1900--1959; and
- using target disappearance as a surrogate for reproductive suppression.

The next eligible controller must separate capacity as well as objectives. The smallest coherent
test is an existing complete multi-worker renewable farm with one private chopper preserved and a
second chopper eligible for pre-fruit denial only after both exist. That tests whether strong bots'
extra workers resolve the zero-sum allocation failure; it is not a bolt-on third worker to the
resident trajectory and not another threshold on this controller.
