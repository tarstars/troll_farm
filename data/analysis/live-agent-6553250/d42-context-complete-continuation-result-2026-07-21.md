## Multilevel conclusion and next hypothesis

- **Mechanism:** rank-one early/late rate deviations still have positive expected causal value, with
  a confidence bound above zero, across a much larger fresh bank.
- **Statistics:** individual sign is noisy and early magnitude shifts materially between map banks.
- **Representation:** explicit economy, workforce, active-job, plant, geometry, and opponent-distance
  context does not solve the prediction problem. Missing snapshot fields are no longer the primary
  explanation.
- **Policy abstraction:** one isolated action followed by D40 is not the same objective as a policy
  that makes repeated, interacting decisions. D41e already showed repeated activations compound
  positively. Requiring a static classifier to predict each intervention's terminal sign discards
  that sequential structure.
- **Next direction:** stop collecting supervised sign labels. The next eligible experiment is a
  constrained closed-loop policy-improvement controller over rank zero/rank one at early/late rate
  decisions, with D40 fixed everywhere else. It must expose rank-one actions during training without
  D41c's four-logit barrier and optimize terminal margin directly. A small preflight must first prove
  legal activation, reward identity, and measurable policy movement before a long PPO/Monte Carlo run.

No D42 threshold, model, feature, seed, or external bank may be reopened.
