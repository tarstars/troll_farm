# D93a factory worker-three stock-flow audit — frozen protocol

Date: 2026-07-21  
Status: frozen before execution; telemetry-only, consumed maps

## Question and scope

D89 supplies a complete renewable banana-to-wood loop with two productive roles. D92 shows that
retargeting those roles cannot restore competitive pressure. D93a asks whether that new economy
creates a repeatable legal surplus window for a third worker.

This phase cannot issue `TRAIN`, alter a command, expose a fresh map, construct a candidate, or
invoke the platform. It reruns exact resident and D89 controls on consumed maps
`9,914,032--9,914,047`, both seats, all eight local families. The audit observes the state before
each command and appends telemetry only; action and terminal hashes must reproduce the prior D89
panel exactly.

## Frozen bills

The intervention-relevant worker is D40's validated third-worker chopper `(ms=2, cc=2, hp=0,
chop=2)`. At workforce two its bill is 6 PLUM, 6 LEMON, 2 APPLE, and 6 IRON when iron terrain is
present. This is the only profile that may authorize a later causal intervention.

A cheap `(1,1,0,1)` worker, costing 3 PLUM, 3 LEMON, 2 APPLE, and 3 IRON, is recorded only as a
diagnostic lower bound. Cheap-worker availability cannot authorize training or spec tuning.

## Measurements

For every two-worker pre-command state, record:

- bank affordability for both bills;
- balanced-bill affordability after adding currently carried currency;
- exact shack occupancy;
- spawn-legal turns, defined as bank-affordable, shack clear, and more than 20 turns remaining;
- first/last legal turn and longest consecutive legal run;
- the minimum simultaneous balanced-bill deficit and its PLUM/LEMON/APPLE/IRON vector;
- per-resource deficit-turn counts; and
- terminal bank and carried stock for all six resources.

## Integrity gates

1. 512 complete rows and 256 exact resident/D89 pairs.
2. Existing action hashes, terminal hashes, scores, margins, workers, plants, and factory telemetry
   match the prior D89 discovery panel in every pair.
3. Legal-turn counts never exceed affordable-turn counts; first/last/run telemetry is internally
   consistent; all deficit partitions are nonnegative.
4. One-thread and 20-thread audit outputs are byte-identical.

## Warrant for D93b

A separately frozen third-worker causal intervention may open only if all are true for D89:

1. at least 128/256 tasks have a balanced spawn-legal turn;
2. balanced legal reach exceeds resident by at least 10 percentage points;
3. each of the eight opponent families has at least eight legal tasks;
4. the median first balanced legal turn among reached tasks is at most 240; and
5. at least half of reached tasks have a legal run of two or more consecutive turns.

These are availability gates, not evidence that training is valuable. Passing them permits one
causal D93b design; it does not make D89 or the future treatment a submission candidate. If they
fail, close direct third-worker addition on this factory and use the deficit decomposition to pick
a different architecture.
