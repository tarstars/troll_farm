# Capacity-separated reproductive denial — result, 2026-07-19

## Verdict

**Reject and close capacity-separated pre-fruit denial without tuning.** A second chopper is not
spare capacity: the adaptive renewable architecture needs both choppers in its productive loop.
Diverting only the second worker still destroys far more own production than it removes from the
rival, while redundant seeds replace almost every interrupted crop. Confirmation seeds
2080--2139 remain sealed.

## Integrity

The consumed 0--29 implementation panel passes every frozen gate:

- complete common grid and all games complete;
- byte-identical 20-worker repeat;
- at least 99.8% assigned chopped-wood provenance;
- zero adaptive-base shadow mismatches;
- zero capacity-separation violations;
- every activation has two pure choppers; and
- all 178 inactive cells exactly match the adaptive base.

This opened the unchanged fresh 2020--2079 discovery panel: 960 scenarios per profile, both seats,
and eight opponents.

## Fresh outcome

- 1,342 targets are selected;
- 1,312 disappear before fruit and 30 fruit; and
- the protected first chopper is never replaced.

The second chopper removes about 10.9 selected crops per game but prevents only one net planting.
This independently repeats the two-worker conclusion: crop extinction is not lineage extinction.
It also answers the workforce question more precisely. Strong bots use more workers because the
workers participate in coupled productive cycles; assigning an extra worker to low-yield denial
does not make that worker affordable in economic terms.

## Consequence

Closed:

- pre-fruit crop targeting by either the only chopper or a capacity-separated second chopper;
- treating multi-worker production as surplus denial capacity; and
- further first-fruit/turn/density/worker-spec tuning on consumed ranges 1900--2079.

The next discriminator is observational stock-flow analysis. Measure banked/carried banana seeds,
fruit on opponent crops, parallel crop count, planting phases, and terminal exposure. Denial can
reopen only if the exact resident visits a reproducible low-redundancy state that productive farms
do not. Otherwise prioritize score-race production and terminal control rather than rival-crop
interruption.
