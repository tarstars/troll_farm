# Secure-orchard conversion audit — independent replication protocol, 2026-07-19

## Purpose

Replicate the sustained outer-orchard mechanism on a disjoint, result-blind exact-resident battle
block after the discovery audit formally missed its overly high full-stream conformance floor.
This protocol does not alter or waive the discovery verdict.

## Frozen corpus

Use the 80 completed battles immediately older than the consumed recent-80 census. Freeze their
ids from `findLastBattlesByTestSessionHandle` metadata before fetching any game result. Every
battle must contain agent `6560353`, submission `41012883`; exclude all recent-80 ids. Record the
ordered id hash in `secure-orchard-conversion-replication-manifest-2026-07-19.json`.

## Fixed method

Use the same exact 62,725-byte source, mechanical stderr probe, official-state decoder, and
prefix-admission rule as discovery. A force at or after the first resident/recorded mismatch is
excluded. Historical continuation after any future release remains non-counterfactual and is not
scored as a candidate.

The full-stream floor is 40/80. It was fixed from two prior official-state audits—43/80 and 44/82
full reproductions—rather than discovery mechanism outcomes. Require all 80 fetches, zero unknown
diff updates, probe/resident stdout equality in all games, and every admitted event on a ripe
apple mother.

## Replication gates

Require all integrity checks plus:

- at least five games with a forced harvest after the first forced apple is banked;
- at least three distinct activated opponents;
- at least 200 admitted post-seed-replacement forces in aggregate; and
- at least two activated losses.

These floors are below half the discovery breadth/count and ask only whether the mechanism and its
downside recur. They do not demand another catastrophe and do not estimate a release benefit.

## Decision

A pass authorizes a fresh local controller discovery, not a packaged candidate, controlled game,
or arena submission. Local discovery must compare the coefficient-free seed-replacement release
with opportunity-cost exits that can preserve the seven discovery wins. Failure closes the
orchard-release branch without tuning replication thresholds.
