## Verdict

Close deterministic state-machine distillation from this 30-game snapshot. The rank-one policy
does expose a strong concurrent-scaling invariant, but it does not satisfy the preregistered
universal grammar: one of six scaler pair foundations has no successful HARVEST->PLANT episode,
and the two reference policies each omit a different required component. Under the frozen D95
rule, no D96 hand-written controller is authorized.

The negative warrant does not erase the observed structure. Joint funding, immediate
capitalization, hybrid early labor, and wood-heavy later labor are high-confidence representation
requirements for a learned whole-policy controller. They are not sufficient rules for another
fixed scheduler.

## Rank-one lifecycle

`delineate` finishes with two workers in four games, three in three, and four in three. Its six
scaler games train worker three on turns 91, 107, 110, 117, 91, and 167. Three of those games then
train worker four on turns 141, 157, and 136.

The strongest scaler observations are exact:

| Observation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Later TRAINs no more than one turn after first affordability | 9/9 |
| Later TRAINs with useful material from at least two existing workers | 9/9 |
| Scaler pair foundations with capitalization work | 6/6 |
| First trained workers using at least two material domains | 6/6 |
| Scaler pair foundations with direct HARVEST->PLANT | 5/6 |
| Later-worker successful material actions spent on CHOP/DROP | 934/995 = 93.87% |

The first trained worker is not a passive bill or a pure chopper in any scaler game. One uses
fruit plus mining, one fruit plus wood, and four use fruit, mining, and wood before worker three.
Every later bill is produced by the whole current workforce: worker-three bills use ordinals 0
and 1, while worker-four bills use 0, 1, and 2. Once trained, later workers execute 744 CHOP, 190
DROP, 20 PLANT, 18 HARVEST, 13 PICK, and 10 MINE actions.

The six scaler games average more fruit acquisition by pair age 40 than the four eventual compact
games: +4.833 starter HARVEST, +0.750 trained-worker HARVEST, +2.667 actor-ripe fruit, +3.167 banked
LEMON, +2.250 banked PLUM, and +1.500 banked IRON. They average 5.417 less banked WOOD. This is
descriptive, outcome-conditioned evidence and cannot be turned into a fitted scaler threshold.

## Why the blueprint warrant fails

Game `896494551` is the decisive rank-one counterexample. The pair harvests natural stock and
capitalizes worker three on turn 91 with both workers contributing, but performs no PLANT before
that TRAIN. Its pair-age-40 bank already contains PLUM 6, LEMON 15, APPLE 1, and IRON 3. Renewal
occurs only after scaling. Therefore renewable source maintenance is common and valuable, but is
not a universal prerequisite for the transition.

The reference policies confirm the larger architecture while rejecting one literal shared state
machine:

| Agent | Scaler games | Renewable foundations | Capitalizing foundations | Joint later funding |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| delineate | 6/10 | 5/6 | 6/6 | 9/9 |
| norxondor_gorgonax | 10/10 | 8/10 | 10/10 | 16/16 |
| wala | 10/10 | 10/10 | 6/10 | 20/20 |

Norxondor is a broadly concurrent hybrid architecture. Wala instead keeps the original pair on a
farm loop and makes later workers almost exclusively wood/logistics workers. Both coordinate the
funding bill, but neither makes every preregistered foundation observation. The reference-direction
gate and the all-rank-one-renewal gate consequently fail; all other blueprint gates pass.

## Retained requirements and next branch

D94 failed because it interrupted a finished two-worker factory for 47,707 serialized funding
turns. D95 shows what that treatment lacked: bill acquisition must be ordinary productive work by
both workers; TRAIN is a transaction boundary taken immediately at affordability; and added
capacity is reassigned without stopping the renewable economy.

These constraints should shape the next learned representation:

1. represent each worker separately, including current job, cargo, crop lineage, and material
   contribution to the pending bill;
2. choose semantic per-worker jobs/targets jointly, rather than one global four-mode override;
3. expose exact deposited/carried/ripe bill stock and reserve same-turn PICK/PLANT against TRAIN;
4. retain recurrent job progress and opponent commitment; and
5. optimize complete closed-loop trajectories with explicit own-production and tail constraints.

Before freezing D96, a collision audit found that teacher-state job supervision is already
answered by D35 and the Norxondor reconstruction: persistent labels have high replay coverage, but
their integrated imitation controller collapses under autoregressive covariate shift. D79 also
closes unrestricted scoring of every legal spatial job. Repeating supervised replay accuracy would
therefore not discriminate the remaining problem.

The revised eligible experiment is a consumed-map function-class preflight for a factorized
per-worker semantic-option policy. It must preserve D40's exact TRAIN/deficit/evacuation executor,
restrict each free worker to the first renewable-safe D40 candidate of a requested semantic mode,
and add worker identity, cargo, active parallel jobs, predicted deposits, and prior role history to
the already-passed global batch state. Matched zero-residual policies must reproduce every D61
global policy exactly; only incremental whole-game headroom can open optimization. This is not the
closed global four-mode controller or D79 all-job scorer. Do not fit a hand-written threshold from
D95, open fresh maps, run TestSession, submit, or replace the resident.
