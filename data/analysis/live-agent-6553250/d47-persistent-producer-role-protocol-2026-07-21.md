# D47 persistent post-funding producer roles — frozen protocol (2026-07-21)

## Question

D35a's field-job audit identifies a producer/producer/chopper team in which RENEW and FELL make up
roughly 94% of non-idle work. D40 trains a producer and a chopper but assigns every free worker by
one generic rate order. D46 then proves that the designated maximum-chop worker already takes
`FELL_BANK` whenever it is legal: 11,525 eligible decisions produced zero overrides.

D47 tests the complementary, coefficient-free structural hypothesis. Once all three workers exist,
the two non-designated-chopper workers persistently own renewable production while the designated
chopper and all funding mechanics remain exact D40.

## Frozen candidate

At every decision compute exact D40 prior order and designate the live worker with maximum
`(chop_power, unit_id)` as the chopper. Select D40 rank zero except when all conditions hold:

1. the D40 branch is `rate`;
2. our side has at least three workers;
3. the current worker is not the designated chopper; and
4. at least one legal candidate is `RENEW`.

Then choose the legal `RENEW` candidate with the lowest exact D40 prior rank. If that is already
rank zero, record eligibility but not an override. No target, owner, ETA, fruit, phase, health,
score, inventory, or coefficient is added. TRAIN, deficit, shack evacuation, worker specifications,
active-job reservations, provenance, the designated chopper, and all fallback behavior remain exact
D40.

## Stage A: activation-only audit

Use untouched official seeds **9,782,000--9,782,015**, both seats, and all eight frozen macro
opponents: 256 tasks. Run exact D40 control and the unchanged D47 candidate twice with 20 threads.
The candidate repeats must be byte-identical. Ignore all score and margin fields; this bank can
establish only activation and mechanical integrity.

Stage A passes only if:

1. all three 256-task grids are complete, exact, deterministic, and mechanically clean;
2. there are at least 512 role-eligible decisions and at least 256 actual overrides; and
3. candidate action hashes differ from D40 in at least 20% and at most 90% of tasks.

Any failure closes D47 without opening a value bank. Do not change the worker designation, job
kind, thresholds, or rule after observing activation.

## Conditional development

Only a Stage A conjunction opens untouched seeds **9,783,000--9,783,031**, both seats, and all
eight opponents: 512 tasks. Run exact D40 control and two independent byte-repeat D47 arms. Require:

1. complete deterministic grids and zero illegal-command, provenance, relevant-deposit-prediction,
   role-integrity, worker-cap, reward-identity, action-count, or decision-loop failure;
2. action-hash changes in 20%--90% of tasks, at least 1,024 eligible decisions, and at least 512
   overrides;
3. paired mean margin gain at least `+8`, 5%-trimmed gain at least `+5`, and the normal 95% lower
   bound across 32 map-seed means above `+3`;
4. mean own-score delta at least `+3` and mean opponent-score delta at most zero;
5. at least six of eight opponent-family mean margin deltas positive and the worst at least `-8`;
6. worker-two rate at least 95%, worker-three rate at least 88%, and crop rate at least 97%; and
7. catastrophe count (`margin <= -100`) and total negative-margin mass no worse than D40.

## Conditional confirmation

Only a complete development pass opens untouched seeds **9,784,000--9,784,031**. Run exact D40 and
the unchanged D47 candidate twice. Require the same integrity, activation, workforce, crop, and tail
gates, plus paired mean margin gain at least `+5`, trimmed gain at least `+3`, seed-mean normal lower
bound above zero, own-score delta at least zero, opponent-score delta at most `+2`, at least six
positive opponent-family means, and no family below `-10`.

## Decision rule

A development and confirmation conjunction freezes D47 as a complete-policy research checkpoint
and opens a separate deployment-size/runtime and field-domain qualification protocol. It does not
authorize candidate construction, TestSession, submission, or Arena action.

A failure at any stage seals later banks and closes this exact persistent-producer rule. Do not
force renewable work before worker three, exempt one producer, add a phase threshold, change the
designated chopper, or tune the rule or gates after seeing results.
