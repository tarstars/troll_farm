## Level 3: funding, roles, and renewable sequence

Current top behavior supports three linked mechanisms:

- **F4 coordinated later funding:** all 162 later TRAIN events have at least two useful material
  contributors. Of 94 third-worker transactions, 96.81% execute immediately when first affordable.
- **F5 hybrid/multi-role labor:** 59.28% of 361 trained workers have both harvest and chop skill;
  55.18% of 560 workers active for at least 50 turns issue both meaningful HARVEST and CHOP work.
- **F6 late renewable loop:** 73.27% of successful plants and 91.41% of final wood arrive after
  turn 100; HARVEST->PLANT occurs in 80.5% and CHOP->DROP in 100% of top appearances.

The resident has no HARVEST->PLANT transition in any of 60 target games and no later TRAIN event.
Its WAIT rate rises late and averages 9.057 commands per 100 game turns, versus 0.072 for selected
top appearances. Total action rates are affected by workforce size, but the missing transition is
not: the resident renews primarily through PICK->PLANT, while top policies repeatedly convert
harvested fruit back into sources.

## Multilevel conclusion

1. **Outcome level:** catastrophic losses remain concentrated and crop-compounded.
2. **Economic level:** the resident establishes crops reliably but maintains a much smaller source
   and wood economy.
3. **Workforce level:** the missing decision is not the first early TRAIN; it is a conditional
   third-worker transaction around the mature-economy boundary.
4. **Scheduler level:** strong policies fund that transaction cooperatively, deploy hybrid workers,
   and continue HARVEST->PLANT and CHOP->DROP loops with almost no explicit waiting.
5. **Representation level:** an isolated anti-crop score is too narrow, and universal early scale
   is contradicted. The next discriminator must ask whether deployable opening/state features can
   select selective later scale across held-out top agents.

## Decision

Freeze D63a as an agent-held current-field workforce-transition audit. Compare an outcome-blind
opening-only selector with a turn-100 state selector for later third-worker creation. If the opening
model transfers, reopen first-move recipe selection. If only the state model transfers, add a
natural-boundary capitalization action to a closed-loop controller. If neither transfers, treat
scale as policy-identity/sequence behavior and move to a recurrent or portfolio representation.

Do not create a candidate, retry the rejected crop wrapper, train universally to worker three, or
open the nine confirmation games from this result.
