# D56a deficit-scaled LEMON source preflight — result (2026-07-21)

**The LEMON source mechanism passes strongly, but the workforce conjunction fails; reject V6 and
advance to the predeclared exact deficit-vector allocator.** Among the exact 732 D55 cells blocked
before worker three, 639 (87.30%) create more successful LEMON plants and the mean rises from 2.257
to 8.530, a gain of 6.273. Both frozen mechanism thresholds pass by wide margins.

(23.75%), below V5's 324 (25.31%), and worker four in 2/640 eligible cells (0.31%), below V5's nine
(1.41%). No score, support, candidate value, TestSession, submission, or Arena conclusion is

## Multilevel interpretation

- **Transaction:** remains closed and correct. V6 neither leaks into the first-worker layer nor
  spends an executable bill.
- **Source activation:** conclusively passes. One bill-derived source floor creates abundant LEMON,
  so further increases or retuning of that floor are ineligible.
- **Commodity allocation:** LEMON specialization displaces the rest of the economy. A descriptive
  exact-pair readout on the 732 blocked cells finds +4,592 cumulative LEMON plants but -583 PLUM,
  -1,575 APPLE, and -2,679 BANANA plants. Post-carry/ripe LEMON shortage falls from 408 to 350
  cells, while IRON shortage rises from 204 to 228 and PLUM barely changes (248 to 247).
- **Trajectory conversion:** across all 1,280 pairs, V6 promotes 70 trajectories to a larger
  workforce but demotes 95 (93 by one worker and two by two workers), for a net loss of 27 workers.
  The source role solves one coordinate by consuming the labor needed for the remaining vector.
- **Next abstraction:** allocate producers from the whole exact post-stock PLUM/LEMON/APPLE/IRON
  deficit vector, and build a fruit source only when its residual bill deficit requires more
  three-fruit capacity. This is resource balancing, not parameter tuning.

## Decision

The frozen decision rule applies exactly: the LEMON mechanism passes, transaction/invariance pass,
and workforce fails. Close one-resource source building. D57 may implement one coefficient-free
exact deficit-vector source allocator using D55's predeclared PLUM and IRON secondary shortages.
It must retain V5 transaction semantics and rerun the complete workforce conjunction. Support and
platform evaluation remain closed.
