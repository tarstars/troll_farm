## Conclusions at different abstraction levels

### Command and target level

- Opponent-crop interception is a real mechanism, but contact persistence, fruit stealing,
  pre-fruit destruction, and scalar dual-value bonuses all fail complete-policy opportunity cost.
- The resident already places its own crops privately enough. The material rival supply is created
  by the rival, not leaked from our farm.
- Exact spatial targets matter: Level-3 PPO's useful gain was mainly farmer target agreement, not
  learning the correct verb.
- D11 confirms the same mechanism at greater scope: the fixed actor already chose the recovery
  MOVE verb 99.921% of the time but selected the exact source in only 7.147%.  Teacher-anchored PPO
  raises exact source choice to 45.19% prospectively while preserving 99.93% verb agreement and
  97.65% whole-task success.
- Making every crop-site command legal is insufficient under a complete opponent: D2 removed 366
  illegal selections but changed only one of 500 outcomes.  Crop survival and opponent economic
  growth, not static target validity, now define the next interaction gap.
- Opponent planting and one-worker renewable supply are already represented well enough: the
  unchanged actor prospectively retains 99.70%.  Crop destruction and workforce compounding can
  now be tested as separate causal mechanisms.
- One confirmed crop destruction is also represented well enough: the unchanged actor retains
  97.90% prospectively despite destruction in 83.85% of episodes.  Natural workforce growth is now
  the principal isolated discontinuity between the accepted one-worker stack and rejected D0.
- The first paid workforce transition is represented too: the unchanged actor retains 97.85% while
  the opponent trains in 96.35% and uses the second worker productively in 92.95%.  “More than one
  worker” is not the D0 discontinuity; scale beyond two or broader policy scope remains.
- Paid worker three is possible, but the present task usually ends before its three-role economy
  matures.  Random's timeout-length control activates the exact same feeder/crop loop strongly.
  Further late-game interaction work requires a sustained objective, not cheaper-worker tuning.
- Horizon extension alone then exposed the remaining ordering defect: funding worker three before
  securing a crop made the planter and feeder compete for the prerequisite.  Crop-before-scale
  prospectively restores 99.95% rival crop creation and 89.40% own harvest while the unchanged
  actor retains 95.40%.  Strong-bot workforce scale is a supply/scheduler policy, not a buy rule.

### Resource and workforce level

- “More workers” is not a first-move purchase rule. Strong bots fund a usually delayed third worker
  through continuous producer/funder cycles and then keep every worker productive.
- Dedicated denial workers are unaffordable because crop lineages are redundant; removing many
  individual crops prevents very few future plantings while displacing our own score production.
- Commodity substitution is coupled to training currency, carrying capacity, health, and terminal
  inventory. A safe private crop species cannot be chosen independently of the whole scheduler.

### Scheduler and architecture level

- Fixed grammars, copied roles, replay retrieval, and one-job redirection all fail because value is
  state-conditional and multi-role. The missing object is a complete closed-loop economy policy.
- A compact neural actor can learn increasingly rich requested plans when initialized by online
  behavior cloning and kept near a legal teacher with a small auxiliary loss.
- That architecture now generalizes through the first control-valid sustained opponent that
  defeats the fixed actor: the sole D11 PPO checkpoint passes an unopened 2,000-seed bank without
  metric retreat or checkpoint selection.  Deployment feasibility, not curriculum learning, is
  the immediate discriminator.
- Pure PPO is not reliable here: both from-scratch failure and an independently reproduced
  deterministic argmax collapse justify retaining the teacher anchor.
- YT is a valid scale-out path, not a default executor.  Local controls finish in 7--11 seconds and
  a 2,000-episode actor gate in 72 seconds, while prior 4M PPO runs take 68--88 minutes.  Reserve
  YT for multi-replica PPO, large evaluation matrices, or millions of Monte Carlo games after a
  valid failure; see the [YT compute assessment](yt-compute-assessment-2026-07-20.md).

### Evaluation level

- Generated maps plus the old eight local models are useful for parity and causal mechanism tests,
  but not candidate promotion. They failed both critical-trajectory coverage and exact-field
  calibration.
- Platform seed control fixes the map. It fixes the whole trajectory only for independently
  verified opponents; therefore deterministic exact blocks and stochastic map-blocked opponents
  must be analyzed separately.
- All learned curriculum results are prospective on exact unopened banks and independently
  confirmed through Level 4. They validate learning capability, not live opponent transfer.

### Goal level

- The cycle has produced a prospectively confirmed learned research checkpoint, but **no
  Arena-qualified or submitted improvement** and no evidence of movement toward rank 3 in live
  rating.  Its immediate value is architectural; compact integration and field transfer remain
  unproved.
- The curriculum still supplies the desired recipe and uses a hand-built deterministic opponent.
  It does not yet select its own opening/macro plan, react to a strategic adversary, or satisfy the
  live protocol and 100k source limit as an integrated submission.

## Final ledger

**Accepted:** layered field evaluation rules; Curriculum Levels 1, 2, 3, and 4; the isolated
Level-5 natural-forager, regenerative-planter, one-shot-reaper, naturally funded two-worker, and
crop-before-scale naturally funded three-worker abstractions without a new checkpoint; and D11
teacher-anchored learning evidence on the frozen 2,000-seed prospective bank.

**Rejected/closed:** both live crop candidates; private placement; harvest-on-contact; static farm
and proxy grammars; replay/identity continuation retrieval; direct strong-policy transplant;
isolated pre-fruit denial; extra-denier workforce; lineage liquidation; static species separation;
orchard release; resident-local one-job scheduler; complete-opponent first jump; and pre-creation
crop-site recovery as a bridge to that opponent; and unwindowed three-destruction D10.

**Pending:** autonomous high-level recipe/first-move selection, broader opponent policy scope, and
transfer under the layered field gate.  D11 int8 preservation, Levels 1--4 regression, Rust/Python
parity, K2 latency, complete live ABI reconstruction, integrated source size, complete response
latency, and forced 300-turn process safety now pass.  Fixed-recipe V5 is not itself a candidate.
Curriculum acceptance does not itself authorize an Arena submission.
