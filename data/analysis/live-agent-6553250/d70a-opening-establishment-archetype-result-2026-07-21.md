# D70a opening-establishment archetype audit — result (2026-07-21)

## Verdict

**Close the minimal hand-built opening transaction.** D70 is valid, and it confirms that later
scalers usually plant an early, private, bank-seeded first crop. Neither of the two common ordering
signatures reliably turns that first generation into renewable receipt in both held-agent
partitions, so no signature is nominated and no fresh causal prefix is authorized.

The next eligible representation is a **closed-loop opening policy portfolio** that can observe
source loss, choose species repeatedly, and transition from establishment to compounding. A fixed
one-source prefix would repeat the D65--D68 error at an earlier turn.

## Integrity

- The exact 150 D69 turn-100-eligible appearances are present: 46 later scalers and 104 eligible
  non-scalers, with exact game/agent/seat identity and agent-held partitions.
- Every trajectory length, final inventory, first/second TRAIN turn, and D69 crop milestone matches.
- Unknown replay updates and active-generation alignment failures are zero.
- The analyzer reads neither outcomes nor confirmation products. No platform action occurred.

## First-crop anatomy

Among the 46 later scalers:

- all 46 create an owned crop; 39/46 (84.78%) do so by turn 10;
- 43/46 acquire its seed with a bank PICK and three use a board harvest;
- the starter creates 44/46 first crops; later worker ordinals create only two;
- every first crop is player-favored and 45/46 are inside the broad D40 source domain;
- the first crop survives to any own renewable receipt in 33/46 (71.74%), but yields that receipt
  before worker three in only 28/46 (60.87%); and
- species are heterogeneous: 21 LEMON, 13 PLUM, 8 APPLE, and 4 BANANA.

The early signatures fail for complementary held-partition reasons:

| Early signature | Discovery | Validation | Failing receipt rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| worker two, then bank-seeded crop | 11 | 11 | discovery 5/11 = 45.45% |
| bank-seeded crop, then worker two | 5 | 10 | validation 6/10 = 60.00% |

Both signatures have enough rows, agents, and safe geometry. Only the first-generation receipt gate
fails. The same timing rule therefore cannot be rescued by broadening the planting cell or choosing
the other worker-two order.

## Species evidence

The first species is not described by a trivial inventory heuristic. Across later scalers, exact
match rates are 17.39% for largest deposited bank, 13.04% for largest D40-bill surplus, and 8.70%
for BANANA-if-available. In contrast, eligible non-scalers choose BANANA in 82/103 observed first
crops and match BANANA-if-available 79.61% of the time.

This is a useful policy-level discriminator: renewable scalers deliberately allocate first-crop
species in a way that is coupled to their later funding/compounding policy, while many non-scalers
use a simple BANANA opening. It rules out selecting a source species from current bank size alone.

## Interpretation at different levels

### Geometry

Placement is not the missing variable. Later scalers are 100% player-favored and 97.83% inside the
broad D40 domain; each common signature is 100% inside that domain in both partitions. Only 34.78%
are water-adjacent, so a wet-cell requirement would discard supported strategies without repairing
receipt.

### Generation lifecycle

The D69 phase order is carried by the crop portfolio, not necessarily generation one. D69 found
86.96% of later scalers receive renewable fruit before worker three, while D70 attributes only
60.87% to their first generation. Later or replacement generations close the 26-point gap. Early
source loss is part of successful play, not a rare exception that one safer cell can eliminate.

### Controller architecture

“Plant once, then return to D40” is too small. The controller must remember attempted species,
receipts, deaths, and reinvestment; retain the option to create another source; and decide when the
portfolio is sufficiently productive to capitalize into worker three. This is precisely the state
that D62's feed-forward four-mode interface omitted.

## Limits

D70 is behavior evidence, not value evidence. It does not prove that the observed source portfolios
improve our local controller or that top-policy species choices can be inferred from the current
56 features. It does prove that neither common one-source signature satisfies the frozen
cross-agent lifecycle gate. No threshold, species, or cell may be selected from these rows for a
fresh hand-built prefix.

## Decision and next experiment

Freeze D71 as a mechanical closed-loop establishment-portfolio environment:

- exact D40 remains an action-identical control;
- explicit bank-seeded PLUM/LEMON/APPLE/BANANA actions are available at natural Train boundaries
  and can be selected again after source loss;
- recurrent observable memory includes attempted sources, receipts, deaths, reinvestment, and live
  owned generations; and
- ordinary harvest/renew/fell/balanced batch options remain available after establishment.

D71 may validate legality, parity, action reachability, positive establishment, recurrence, and
throughput only. It may not assess outcome value or open a PPO budget until a separate closed-loop
population/upper-bound gate shows useful nontrivial action headroom.
