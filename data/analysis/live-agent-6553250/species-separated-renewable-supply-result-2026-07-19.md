## Interpretation at three levels

### Commodity level

BANANA is the only fruit excluded from training costs. Replacing it with PLUM spends the same stock
that funds movement-speed upgrades and additional workers. LEMON and APPLE have the same conflict
with carry or harvest training, so testing them would repeat the identified failure. APPLE also has
higher tree health.

### Scheduler level

The adaptive controller was tuned around a fast-growing, low-health, non-training commodity. Its
plant/harvest/fell loop is not species-neutral even though every verb generalized correctly. The
candidate accumulates renewable stock but cannot turn it into workers, wood, or terminal score at
the required rate.

### Architectural level

Removing our own BANANA leak cannot suppress a mature opponent lineage. The higher-value remaining
problem is opportunity-cost allocation inside the resident's already successful mixed economy,
not another denial role or a different fruit label.
