# D96a factorized worker-option population — frozen protocol

Date: 2026-07-21  
Status: frozen before population generation, implementation, or outcome execution

## Question

D61 proves that renewable-safe state-conditioned semantic batches have large whole-game headroom,
but D73/D76/D77 reject learned and searched policies that choose one global mode for every free
worker. D95 independently observes that rank-one scaler pairs work different material domains
concurrently and jointly fund every later TRAIN. Can the same safe semantic vocabulary expose
additional whole-game headroom when the controller chooses a mode separately for each free worker,
conditioned on the other active jobs and deposits?

This is a function-class/upper-bound preflight on already-consumed D61 maps. It cannot select a
random policy, tune a distribution, fit a learner, create a candidate, open fresh or sealed data,
call TestSession, submit, or change the resident.

## Exact substrate and action boundary

Use `CompleteMacroEnv` and the corrected D61 semantics unchanged:

- exact D40 handles TRAIN, positive-deficit funding, shack evacuation, persistent execution,
  reservations, shared PICK accounting, and transaction revalidation;
- `balanced`, `harvest`, `renew`, and `fell` request the first matching legal candidate under the
  exact D40 prior only in the ordinary Rate branch;
- absence of an own crop locks every experimental choice to balanced; and
- felling the final live own-provenance crop is illegal for experimental policies.

The global D61 controls choose one mode at the Train boundary and retain it for every worker in the
batch. A factorized policy evaluates a mode immediately before each free-worker assignment. The
choice affects only that worker's current Rate job. Other branches remain exact D40, and the next
worker is scored after earlier assignments have entered the shared active-job state.

## Frozen factorized features

For each worker concatenate:

1. the exact 56 D61 batch features captured at the current Train boundary;
2. the exact 46 D42 shared-context features for the current worker, including its stats/cargo,
   both banks, active-job kind counts, predicted active deposits, crop provenance/supply, and
   opponent distance;
3. a three-value one-hot stable worker ordinal; and
4. a four-value one-hot last successfully requested Rate mode for this worker.

The worker residual therefore has 53 features and 212 weights. It receives no opponent identity,
seed, future action, rollout, terminal value, replay label, target-specific D79 feature, fitted
threshold, or outcome statistic. Exact D40 target order remains the target selector.

## Matched population

Read the immutable 69-policy D61 population. Re-evaluate all 69 global policies unchanged.

For each `linear_00`--`linear_63`, create two factorized policies with the same frozen 224 global
weights:

- `factor_zero_XX`: all 212 worker-residual weights are zero;
- `factor_random_XX`: worker-residual weights generated by NumPy PCG64 seed 9601.

Draw one `(53, 4)` C-order array per policy from Normal(0, 0.25), subtract each row's four-mode
mean, transpose to mode-major `(4, 53)`, and round to eight decimals. No separate bias is added
because the D61 base already contains mode biases. A factorized score is the D61 base-mode score
plus the worker residual.
Ties retain mode order balanced, harvest, renew, fell.

There are exactly 197 policies: 69 global, 64 zero residuals, and 64 random residuals. Zero
residual `XX` must reproduce global `linear_XX` command-for-command; this is the key nesting gate.

## Frozen execution

Reuse consumed official maps `9,801,000--9,801,007`, both seats, and all eight D40 opponent modes:
128 tasks per policy and 25,216 rows. Run the complete matrix twice with 20 threads, sort by
`(policy, map_seed, seat, opponent)`, and require byte identity.

Record every D61 terminal/mechanics/action-plane field plus requested/executed modes by worker
ordinal, factorized feature evaluations, per-worker mode switches, batches containing at least two
Rate assignments with distinct modes, safety rejections, and a deterministic option hash.

## Frozen gates

### Integrity and nesting

All must hold:

1. two complete 197 x 128 matrices are byte-identical;
2. all 69 global-policy rows reproduce corrected D61 on every common terminal, action-plane,
   action-hash, state-hash, and option-accounting field;
3. every `factor_zero_XX` row equals its global `linear_XX` row on those same fields;
4. the population reconstructs exactly from D61 plus PCG64 seed 9601; and
5. zero illegal-command, provenance, deposit-prediction, worker-cap, reward, nonfinite-feature,
   mode-accounting, or option-hash failure occurs.

Any failure quarantines value and permits only a defect repair with the frozen design.

### Safety and representation activity

Require:

1. every random factorized policy creates at least one own crop in every task;
2. at least 48/64 random policies retain worker-three reach no more than ten percentage points
   below direct D40;
3. at least 56/64 change action hash from their matched zero residual in at least 10% of tasks;
4. at least 48/64 request at least three semantic modes globally;
5. at least 48/64 produce a mixed Rate batch in at least 25% of tasks;
6. at least 48/64 use at least two modes for both worker ordinals zero and one; and
7. random fixed-policy mean margins span at least 25 points.

### Incremental whole-game headroom

Construct two per-task hindsight oracles with the same tie order: higher terminal margin, higher
own score, lower opponent score, lexical label.

- **Global oracle:** the 68 D61 option policies, excluding only direct `d40_control`.
- **Factor oracle:** direct D40 plus the 64 `factor_random_XX` policies.

The factorized representation passes only if all hold:

1. factor-oracle mean margin gain over D40 is at least +50;
2. factor oracle strictly improves D40 in at least 85% of tasks;
3. every opponent-family factor-oracle mean gain is at least +15;
4. factor-oracle mean own-score delta is nonnegative and opponent-score delta is nonpositive;
5. factor-oracle worker-three reach is at least 85% and crop creation is exactly 100%;
6. factor-oracle mean margin is at least **5 points above** the reconstructed global oracle;
7. factor policies strictly beat the global-oracle row in at least 24/128 tasks; and
8. at least twelve random factor policies are strict factor-oracle winners in at least two tasks.

Random fixed-policy ranks and favorable arms are descriptive and permanently unselectable.

## Decision rule

- **Full pass:** freeze the factorized environment and open a separate mechanics/signal preflight
  for a compact recurrent worker-option actor initialized in the exact global-policy subspace.
- **Integrity failure:** repair only the defect and rerun the unchanged matrix.
- **Activity/safety failure:** close this factorized initialization/interface without changing
  scales, modes, or legality on the consumed bank.
- **Headroom or incremental failure:** close worker-factorization over these D40 semantic modes;
  do not train PPO, CEM, imitation, or a wider scorer on the same interface.

No branch authorizes fresh maps, confirmation, candidate construction, TestSession, submission,
resident replacement, or Arena activity.
