# D65a missing-currency seed-source repair protocol (2026-07-21)

## Question

Can one exact, coefficient-free ability to plant a missing TRAIN-currency species from deposited
seed remove D40's source-extinction worker-two tail without changing unaffected trajectories or
damaging fresh complete-game value?

D65a repairs only the D64 safety invariant diagnosed by D64i. It does not reopen D64 value, change
the worker spec, alter late scale, tune a source count, construct a submission, or perform a
platform action.

## Frozen source bootstrap

Preserve exact D40 except at a `Train`-stage boundary satisfying all conditions:

1. own worker count is exactly one and the producer TRAIN goal remains pending;
2. the producer bill is not deposited/affordable;
3. even deposited bank + own carried stock + every currently ripe board fruit cannot cover the
   bill;
4. at least one positive uncovered coordinate is a fruit (PLUM, LEMON, APPLE, BANANA) whose
   deposited bank contains at least one seed;
5. no bootstrap for that species has already been issued in this game; and
6. a deterministic player-favored empty planting cell is reachable.

Choose the largest uncovered fruit amount after bank+carry+ripe stock, breaking ties PLUM, LEMON,
APPLE, BANANA. The sole worker executes one transactional job:

1. move to the nearest own-shack door;
2. `PICK` exactly one chosen fruit from deposited inventory;
3. move to the unchanged player-favored empty cell rule;
4. `PLANT` that fruit; and
5. return immediately to an exact D40 `Train` boundary.

At most one bootstrap is issued per species per game. The job is forbidden when the bill is
already feasible from current stock, and it never executes with two or more workers. Existing D40
TRAIN, deficit, evacuation, rate, target provenance, job execution, opponent, worker limits, and
all later decisions remain unchanged.

The implementation may add a dependency-free experimental method to `CompleteMacroEnv`, but the
method must be inert unless explicitly called. Existing D40/D61 parity tests must remain exact.

## Consumed repair check

Run exact D40 and `seed_source_repair` on both seats of seeds 9,830,002 and 9,830,014 against
`resident`. This four-task block is mechanism-only and already consumed. Require:

- repair creates worker two in all four tasks;
- every target executes at least one bootstrap of the D64i missing species;
- zero mechanical/provenance/deposit failures and at least one crop per task; and
- exact D40 reproduces the four original one-worker outcomes and terminal hashes.

Failure closes this exact repair before fresh value.

## Fresh bank and repetition

Only after consumed recovery passes, run official seeds 9,831,000--9,831,031, both seats, all eight
unchanged D40 opponents, and two policies (`d40_control`, `seed_source_repair`): 512 tasks per
policy and 1,024 rows per matrix. Seeds 9,831,000--9,831,015 are development; 9,831,016--9,831,031
are validation. Repeat the full matrix with 20 host threads and require byte identity.

Record all D64 terminal/mechanical/action-plane fields plus activation count/species/turn, source-
job command counts, first worker-two/three turns, and pre-bootstrap state hash. Whenever repair
never activates, require exact terminal/action/state parity with D40.

## Frozen gates

### Integrity, recovery, and activity

1. Consumed recovery passes every requirement above.
2. Both fresh 2 x 512 matrices are complete and byte-identical.
3. Every row has zero illegal command, provenance, deposit-prediction, worker-cap,
   reward-identity, source-job, finite-state, or action-accounting failure.
4. Every inactive repair task is terminal/action/state identical to D40.
5. Repair activates in at least eight fresh tasks spanning at least four maps, both blocks, and at
   least two opponent families.

### Fresh safety

6. Repair creates worker two and at least one crop in exactly 100% of fresh tasks.
7. Repair never exceeds three workers and never executes a bootstrap after worker two.
8. Repair does not increase fresh catastrophic-loss count (`margin <= -100`) overall or in
   validation.

### Fresh value

9. Across all fresh tasks, repair mean margin delta versus D40 is nonnegative, mean own-score
   delta is at least -2.0, and mean opponent-score delta is at most +2.0.
10. Validation mean margin delta is nonnegative and no opponent-family mean margin delta is below
    -3.0.
11. On exact fresh tasks where D40 finishes with one worker, repair creates worker two in all and
    gains at least +20 mean margin. If D40 has no such fresh task, this gate is `insufficient`, not
    a pass.

## Decision rule

- **Full pass:** accept the seed-source transaction as a D40 substrate invariant, not as a
  submission candidate. Freeze a new causal experiment on fresh maps; D64 remains consumed and
  invalid.
- **Consumed recovery failure:** close this one-seed source transaction and inspect source survival
  only; do not tune count/cell/species order.
- **Fresh safety/activity failure:** reject the repair and move to a broader source-access state
  machine.
- **Fresh safety pass but value failure:** preserve the diagnosis but reject this repair; do not
  add source weights or reuse fresh seeds.

No branch authorizes confirmation access, TestSession, Arena, submission, or resident replacement.
