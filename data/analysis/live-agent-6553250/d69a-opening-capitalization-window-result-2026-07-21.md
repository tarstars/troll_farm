# D69a opening-to-capitalization window audit — result (2026-07-21)

## Verdict

**The audit is valid, but the universal recurrent phase-machine branch fails its frozen gate.**
Later scalers strongly follow the proposed order in both agent-held partitions: establish an owned
crop, receive renewable fruit, reinvest, and only then create worker three. The disqualifying result
is the negative control. Eligible non-scalers establish a crop by turn 100 in only 39/58 discovery
appearances (67.24%), below the frozen 70% floor; validation is 36/46 (78.26%).

Per the preregistered decision rule, the next representation is an **opening establishment policy**
before any recurrent learner. This is not a return to the rejected permanent farm-first controller:
the next experiment must isolate the smallest early establishment transaction and then return to
the exact complete macro controller.

No confirmation product, outcome, TestSession, submission, or Arena action was used.

## Integrity

- All 200 trajectory streams decode exactly, all final inventories match, all D63 worker-three

third TRAIN is five generations.

## What the trajectories say at different levels

### Transaction level

Later scalers establish early: their median first owned crop is turn 3, first renewable receipt is
turn 40, first reinvestment is turn 54, and worker three is created at median turn 120.5. In 39/46
later-scaler appearances the first owned crop exists by turn 10. The sequence is not merely a
same-turn correlation: receipt, reinvestment, and TRAIN must occur on strictly later observable
turns to count.

### Stock-flow level

Before worker three, later scalers invest 11.48 owned seeds and harvest 22.15 renewable units on
average, for mean renewable flow net **+10.67**. They lose 5.48 generations before the cutoff,
including 5.00 with an opponent CHOP on the death turn, yet still compound positive stock.

Eligible non-scalers through turn 100 invest 7.39 seeds but harvest only 5.58 renewable units, for
mean flow net **-1.82**. The important distinction is therefore not “a crop once existed”; it is
early establishment followed by sufficient receipt and reinvestment to survive adversarial loss.

### Policy level

The failed discovery control is policy-structured, not a diffuse two-game tail. `therealbeef`
establishes by turn 100 in 0/10 eligible non-scaler appearances and `Escdemon` in 2/10. Validation
contains another delayed-establishment policy, `yamo`, at 0/10, but its aggregate remains above the
floor because the other validation policies establish early. This supports a portfolio view:
strong Legend policies include both early renewable scalers and deliberate late/non-establishing
two-worker policies.

### Controller level

A universal phase machine would silently assume the very event it needs to cause. D62 already
showed the same implementation error in miniature: masking semantic alternatives until a crop
exists is not a positive establishment guarantee. The next controller must own the opening
establishment decision explicitly, record whether a source has produced and been reinvested, and
only then expose a compound/capitalize state machine.

## Limits

D69 is outcome-blind observational archaeology. It proves a transferable phase regularity among
later scalers and a genuine policy split among non-scalers; it does not prove that forcing an early
crop improves the resident, identify a universally correct fruit/cell/order, or authorize copying
recorded commands. Earlier full farm-first and permanent turn-75 farm controllers remain closed.

## Decision and next experiment

Freeze D70 as a narrow opening-establishment archetype audit on the same open rows. It will identify
whether the first crop is normally bank-seeded before or after worker two, which fruit and planting
geometry are used, and whether a bounded one-source transaction survives to a renewable receipt in
both agent partitions. D70 is allowed to nominate at most two coefficient-free opening
transactions. A subsequent fresh official-map causal preflight may test only those transactions as
temporary prefixes returning to exact D40. It must not reopen the old multi-worker farm-first
architecture, tune on outcomes from the public replays, or train PPO.
