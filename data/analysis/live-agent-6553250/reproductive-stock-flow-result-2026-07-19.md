# Reproductive stock flow and extinction boundary — result, 2026-07-19

## Verdict

**Both frozen materiality boundaries pass.** The exact resident creates long, broad opponent
BANANA-stock depletion and lineage-absence states that neither unchanged productive farm creates.
This is the first positive reproductive-denial discriminator after the two direct pre-fruit
controllers failed. It authorizes one state-triggered whole-scheduler experiment; it does not
resurrect individual crop timing or prove that the boundary itself causes the resident's result.

## Multilevel interpretation

### State level

At turn 200, among 59 resident games still active, the opponent has mean bank/carry BANANA stock
of only 0.525, mean 2.17 owned banana crops, and mean 3.19 fruits on those crops; the medians are
zero bank, zero carry, two crops, and zero crop fruit. Twenty-four games already have zero owned
seed and 22 have no remaining lineage. At turn 250, 21/48 active games are lineage absent.

The farm checkpoints are qualitatively different. At turn 200 the lean farm leaves the opponent
with mean 5.67 crops and 6.40 fruits on those crops, while our own farm trees expose another 8.45
banana fruits. The resident exposes zero fruit on our BANANA crops at every checkpoint because its
private orchard uses APPLE.

### Flow level

The resident's rival planting rate remains low after turn 100: 0.056, 0.089, 0.072, and 0.058
successful plants per exposed turn in the four late phases. The lean farm rises to 0.106, 0.234,
0.270, and 0.243; adaptive farm is similar. Complete opponent plantings are 1,021 under resident,
2,787 under lean farm, and 2,657 under adaptive farm.

Earlier termination explains some missing production but not the per-exposed-turn gap. The stock
and planting-rate differences coexist while games are still active.

### Architectural level

The failed pre-fruit controllers attacked a crop while seeds and parallel crops could replace it.
The resident instead creates a system state in which the opponent has no seed inventory, no fruit
on its own crops, and often no crop lineage at all. This supports a mode switch based on complete
stock, not another target-order variation.

The data are observational: the resident's entire scheduler creates the state, so activation alone
may not reproduce its causal path. A productive farm's own BANANA fruit is also a plausible
rebootstrap source, but this panel did not randomize crop species and cannot assign that effect.

## Decision and next experiment

Authorize exactly one **lineage-collapse liquidation** experiment. Use the unchanged exact resident
until, after turn 100 and after an opponent BANANA crop has previously been observed, the frozen
lineage-absence state first occurs. Then make a permanent whole-policy switch: bank carried
materials, stop training/planting/picking/harvesting/mining, and coordinate all workers to fell
remaining BANANA sources before other plants. Compare it directly with the exact resident under
command-shadow, repeatability, fresh discovery, and sealed confirmation gates.

Individual first-fruit timing, lone crop pursuit, capacity-separated pursuit, and stock-threshold
tuning remain closed.
