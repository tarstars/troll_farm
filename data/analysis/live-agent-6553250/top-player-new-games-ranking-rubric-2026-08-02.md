## Evidence boundary

The current-new open cohort is 153 games. There is only one direct current-vs-top20 game,
`897780884` versus rank-13 Astrobytes. The 2,684 top20-source open games are a benchmark,
not direct treatment evidence. Seven sealed-tagged games are excluded. A vivid benchmark
difference or the one direct game may motivate an immediate check; neither may be reported
as established field value.

## Hard vetoes

Reject or demote an idea to measurement-only if any applies:

1. provenance, exact identity, open split, or arithmetic cannot be reproduced from the
   shared manifest;
2. the trigger uses post-outcome information or the mechanism is not exact-replay
   reproducible;
3. it duplicates a closed branch, threshold retune, consumed label, or failed subsystem
   bundle without a materially new representation;
4. it lacks one narrow source seam;
5. its first discriminator cannot run now on open data or unsealed official maps with exact
   resident/referee semantics;
6. it converts margin/wins into rating or treats cross-era Arena score as causal value.

## Score for surviving ideas (100 points)

- **Evidence, 25:** exact game IDs/state-action sequence (10); current-cohort n/N plus
  lineage/seat accounting (8); temporal order, association and uncertainty (7).
- **Intervention specificity, 25:** one invariant/source seam (10); distinct from nearest
  closures (10); exact-resident anchor, no bundle (5).
- **Immediate decisiveness, 25:** runnable replay/boundary/small paired test (10);
  same-map/both-seat/control design (8); preregistered pass/fail, stop and integrity gates
  (7).
- **Payoff and safety, 25:** conservative coverage × per-event headroom with projection
  labelled (10); displacement/opponent benefit/catastrophe accounting (8); uncertainty,
  family/seat breadth and rollback risk (7).

Bands: ≥80 immediate-check shortlist; 65–79 audit first; <65 do not build. Tie-break by
cheaper decisive check, then current-cohort support, narrow execution invariant, and lower
tail risk.
