## Interpretation

1. **Root coverage:** the contested event itself is broad; 97.27% of tasks reach the exact same
   first root across all arms.
2. **Action support:** each individual rank is well represented, but several do not simultaneously
   satisfy the coarse “any crop within distance two” predicate at the first root.
3. **Value:** deliberately unknown. D81's serialized score columns may not be used to claim an
   oracle or fixed-arm effect after support failure.
4. **Translation error:** D78 modeled imminent attacks on resident-owned crops. D80/D81 broadened
   that into proximity to any crop and organized responses by prior rank. The next vocabulary
   should preserve exact crop provenance and expose semantic responses to the threatened own crop.
5. **Experimental policy:** coverage floors are useful for candidate qualification, but sealing
   all exploratory value after a modest support miss repeatedly delays mechanism discovery. The
   next exploratory protocol should report uncertainty at realized support and reserve strict
   conjunctions for deciding whether to advance, not whether outcomes may be summarized.
