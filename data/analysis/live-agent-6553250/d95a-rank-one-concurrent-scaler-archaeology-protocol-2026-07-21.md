# D95a rank-one concurrent-scaler archaeology — frozen protocol

Date: 2026-07-21  
Status: frozen before the dedicated analyzer is implemented or run

## Motivation

D94 proves that a late serialized funding bridge can collect a third-worker bill but destroys
D89's production advantage. Earlier Phase-2 experiments also reject attaching a max-bank hybrid
worker to the old Yamo continuation: paid harvest skill was not used by that scheduler. The missing
object is therefore the complete two-worker foundation and its transition to scale, not another
TRAIN condition or worker spec.

D61p already establishes, at population level, that current strong bots coordinate later funding,
use hybrid workers, and continue HARVEST->PLANT and CHOP->DROP loops. D95a drills into the current
rank-one `delineate` policy to reconstruct an implementable per-worker lifecycle. `wala` and
`norxondor_gorgonax` are reference architectures used only to test whether the inferred grammar is
rank-one-specific or shared.

This is observational mechanism archaeology. It cannot estimate the causal value of copying a
policy and does not authorize a candidate or platform action.

## Immutable scope

Read only the already-open products and verified raw replays of immutable snapshot
`20260721T105508Z-d61p`:

- ten open `delineate` appearances;
- ten open `wala` appearances; and
- ten open `norxondor_gorgonax` appearances.

Use `cgauto.analyze_d61p_field_snapshot.load_open_inputs` so every raw replay and trajectory is
manifest-verified and no `sealed_confirmation` content can be opened. Make no network request,
collect no additional replay, and read no prospective local-map block.

## Reconstruction

For every selected appearance:

1. decode every official pre/post state and assign every command to its actual unit;
2. match every spawned unit to a successful TRAIN and its exact bill;
3. track natural, actor-created, opponent-created, and ambiguous crop generations by cell;
4. record every successful HARVEST, PLANT, CHOP, PICK, MINE, and DROP with unit ordinal, crop kind,
   crop origin, cargo delta, bank state, and workforce phase;
5. define the **pair foundation** from the first successful TRAIN through the next successful TRAIN
   or terminal play;
6. take an outcome-blind **pair-age-40 snapshot** exactly 40 turns after the first successful TRAIN,
   before any observed rank-one third-worker transaction; and
7. summarize the post-scale role of each later worker.

The analyzer may report descriptive scaler/non-scaler differences at pair age 40, but it may not
fit or select a threshold from these 30 games.

## Required outputs

- exact train turns/specs, first-affordable delay, and bills by ordinal;
- deposited and acquired material by contributing worker during each funding window;
- pair-foundation action and material rates by worker ordinal;
- HARVEST source origin/species and its next successful material action;
- successful HARVEST->PLANT, HARVEST->DROP, MINE->DROP, and CHOP->DROP episode counts;
- pair-age-40 bank, crop portfolio, ripe supply, action mix, and material acquisition;
- post-scale action mix and wood/logistics share by worker ordinal; and
- one smallest state-machine blueprint, or an explicit finding that no compact grammar is
  supported.

## Integrity gates

1. Exactly 30 unique open games, ten per named agent; zero sealed game IDs.
2. Decoded turn count equals trajectory count in every game; zero unknown replay-diff updates.
3. Every spawned worker matches one successful TRAIN and every selected game has a first TRAIN.
4. Every productive cargo delta is assigned to one player unit and verb.
5. Repeated one-process and 20-process result files are byte-identical.

Any integrity failure stops the experiment without a blueprint.

## Blueprint warrant

A D96 local controller may be designed only if all of the following hold:

1. rank one reaches at least three total workers in at least four of ten appearances;
2. at least 80% of rank-one later TRAINs occur no more than one turn after first affordability;
3. at least 80% of rank-one later TRAINs have useful material from both preexisting workers;
4. every rank-one scaler pair foundation contains successful renewable fruit work
   (HARVEST->PLANT) and successful capitalization work (MINE->DROP or CHOP->DROP);
5. in every rank-one scaler game, the first trained worker uses at least two material domains among
   fruit harvest/plant, mining, and chopping before worker three;
6. later workers spend at least 70% of their successful non-MOVE material actions on CHOP/DROP;
7. the direction of the renewable-foundation and coordinated-funding observations appears in both
   reference agents' scaler games; and
8. the resulting blueprint is structurally different from both closed treatments: it must assign
   paid hybrid work explicitly rather than hand the worker to Yamo, and it must establish training
   currency concurrently rather than interrupt a completed BANANA factory.

Passing authorizes one disabled-by-default D96 implementation and a consumed-map mechanism panel.
It does not authorize fresh maps, TestSession, submission, or resident replacement. Failure closes
rank-one state-machine distillation from this snapshot and returns the next choice to a learned
whole-policy representation.
