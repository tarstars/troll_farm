# D69a opening-to-capitalization window audit protocol (2026-07-21)

## Question

Do current strong Legend trajectories share a transferable phase order—establish an owned source,
receive and reinvest renewable fruit, then add worker three—that can support a recurrent whole-game
controller, or is workforce creation better represented as an opening policy portfolio without a
common sequence?

D69a is an outcome-blind timing/stock-flow audit of the already-open D61p snapshot. It neither
estimates intervention value nor reads confirmation data, trains a policy, constructs a candidate,
or performs a platform action.

## Frozen population and integrity

Use the exact 200 selected-top-agent appearances already materialized by D63a from snapshot
`20260721T105508Z-d61p`: ten appearances from each of 20 agents, preserving D63's agent-held
discovery/validation partition. Load trajectories only through the passed D61p open-input loader.

Require exact game/agent/seat identity against D63a, zero unknown replay updates, exact final
inventories, exact D63 third-worker turns, at least 95% attribution of successful top-player PLANT
generations to a sole creator, and no confirmation product read.

## Frozen milestones

Reconstruct planted generations from empty-before/occupied-after cells and simultaneous commands.
For each selected appearance record:

1. first and second successful TRAIN turns;
2. first unambiguously owned crop birth;
3. first successful own harvest from an own-created crop (`renewable receipt`);
4. first later own PLANT after that receipt (`reinvestment`);
5. first opponent CHOP/HARVEST contact with an own-created crop;
6. live owned generations at worker-three creation;
7. before worker three (or through turn 100 for eligible non-scalers): owned seeds invested, own
   renewable units harvested, generations destroyed, and renewable flow net
   `harvested units - planted seeds`.

Milestones use only information observable by their recorded turn. They do not infer ownership for
ambiguous simultaneous creation.

## Frozen cohorts and gates

Primary cohorts are D63's 150 turn-100-eligible appearances:

- `later_scaler`: exactly two workers at turn 100 and a later third worker;
- `eligible_non_scaler`: exactly two workers at turn 100 and no later third worker.

Report the same phase ordering for all 94 third-worker appearances. For the recurrent phase-order
branch, require in **both** D63 agent partitions among later scalers:

- at least 90% establish an owned crop before worker three;
- at least 70% receive renewable fruit before worker three;
- at least 50% reinvest after that receipt before worker three; and
- median live owned generations at worker three is at least two.

Also require at least 70% of eligible non-scalers in both partitions to establish an owned crop by
turn 100. This last gate distinguishes a common establishment phase from establishment itself being
the workforce selector.

## Decision rule

- If all phase-order and integrity gates pass, freeze a recurrent phase-conditioned batch-option
  interface with a positive establishment invariant and explicit establish/compound/capitalize
  memory. D40 remains the exact initialization; no PPO budget opens until a mechanical preflight.
- If establishment is common but receipt/reinvestment order fails, use a closed-loop policy
  portfolio rather than a universal phase machine.
- If establishment itself separates the cohorts, test an opening establishment policy before any
  recurrent learner.
- If integrity fails, repair only acquisition/attribution and repeat unchanged.

No branch authorizes confirmation access, TestSession, submission, or resident replacement.
