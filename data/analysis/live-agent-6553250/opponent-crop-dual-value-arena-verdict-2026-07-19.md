## Why the prefix result did not transfer

The treatment changed 111/131 resident prefixes exactly as intended, but doubling the current
tree score does not estimate the downstream opportunity cost of abandoning another target.  A
crop can carry high immediate conversion value while pulling a worker away from the resident's
coordinated regeneration, orchard, banking, or endgame schedule.  Broad causal activation is
therefore compatible with severe policy-level loss.

The failure also rules out a simple monotone story that “more opponent-crop priority is better.”
Flat +100 was near parity at its 160-game plateau; value-scaled priority is much worse.  Do not
interpolate a multiplier or retune ETA using these arena games.
