# D71a closed-loop opening-portfolio environment — result (2026-07-21)

## Verdict

**Full mechanical pass.** The eight-action environment preserves exact D62 balanced behavior,
makes every explicit species action broadly reachable, carries source lifecycle memory across
natural job boundaries, exercises retry after source death, and exceeds the frozen throughput/CPU
gate by a wide margin.

Freeze D71 as infrastructure. The next experiment is a recurrent random-policy population and
whole-game oracle gate on disjoint official maps. D71 does not authorize PPO or a candidate.

## Exactness and integrity

- Constant balanced matches all 16 exact D62 reference tasks in terminal scores, workforce,
  TRAIN count, all crop counters, action hash, and state hash.
- Both 3,072-row representation matrices are byte-identical (SHA-256
  `c60d2791a9aa5120cc400a6e487a07610b573fe4d629ca27aee6b61c1fa2d34c`).
- All direct-command, provenance, deposit-prediction, finite-feature, legal-mask,
  source-assignment, boundary, option-count, source-count, and reward-identity failures are zero.
- Maximum episode length is 137 portfolio boundaries, far below the 5,000 limit.
- The 10,198 invalidated ordinary/source jobs are environmental lifecycle outcomes. They are not
  illegal commands or accounting failures and are intentionally observable to a closed-loop
  controller rather than hidden by the mechanics gate.

## Representation activity

The grid executes 249,593 boundary decisions. All eight actions occur:

| Action | Decisions |
|---|---:|
| balanced | 210,252 |
| harvest | 9,208 |
| renew | 9,568 |
| fell | 9,443 |
| seed PLUM | 3,072 |
| seed LEMON | 3,069 |
| seed APPLE | 2,560 |
| seed BANANA | 2,421 |

Explicit creation succeeds 3,072 PLUM, 3,069 LEMON, 2,560 APPLE, and 2,417 BANANA times. The four
missing BANANA creations are valid source-job invalidations after assignment, not mechanical
errors. Every one of the 2,048 fixed-species tasks and all 512 cyclic tasks creates an owned crop.

At 8,393 pre-crop boundaries, at least two explicit species actions are legal 97.64% of the time;
the ordinary harvest/renew/fell actions remain locked exactly as in D62. This repairs D62's
negative-only safety mask with a positive establishment action without disturbing its fallback.

## Closed-loop lifecycle evidence

Every cyclic task makes at least two explicit attempts. Of 512 cyclic tasks:

- 482 observe at least one ended own generation;
- 70 issue a later explicit source action after a prior death;
- all 512 create crops;
- cumulative own-provenance receipts are 14,033 fruit units; and
- 9,421 later generations are created after a prior renewable receipt.

These counts are not value evidence. They prove the interface can express exactly the repeated
generation behavior D70 showed was missing from a one-source prefix.

## Parallel performance

The independent runs process 3,309.4 and 3,299.3 boundary transitions/s. Effective CPU use is
19.52 and 19.57 cores, with reported utilization 1,951% and 1,956%. This clears the gates of 400
transitions/s and 12 cores and leaves substantial room for a compact recurrent population or PPO
later.

## Limits

Probe scores and margins were never aggregated. The fixed species and cyclic probes are mechanics
drivers, not policy candidates. A source action can be legal and still lose its crop, and broad
action reachability says nothing about which species/order is valuable against a strategic
opponent.

## Decision and next experiment

Freeze D72 before reading any disjoint outcomes:

- deterministic random recurrent policies over the exact 72 features and eight masked actions;
- exact balanced control plus a descriptive cyclic mechanics probe;
- repeated official-map evaluation against all eight unchanged opponent modes;
- a crop-safe whole-game population oracle gate requiring own production, opponent suppression,
  cross-family gains, and material strict use of explicit source actions; and
- no policy/checkpoint selection, PPO, field access, candidate, or platform action.

Only a passing function-class/headroom gate can open a separate recurrent optimization preflight.
